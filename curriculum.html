おもてなし

O · MO · TE · NA · SHI

This course introduces Omotenashi, the Japanese way of hospitality — serving from the heart with sincerity, transparency, and no hidden agenda. Derived from omote (surface) and nashi (without), it represents pure and genuine care offered wholeheartedly.

Course Goals

  • Understand the philosophy of Omotenashi — its origins, meaning, and why it remains central to Japanese service culture
  • Develop a mindset shift: from completing tasks to anticipating needs before they are expressed
  • Build practical vocabulary and expressions used in real hotel, restaurant, and service environments
  • Recognise how seasonal events, cultural context, and guest emotions shape the service experience
  • Apply Omotenashi principles with consistency — not as performance, but as genuine character
