Omotenashi
The uniquely Japanese approach to hospitality — anticipating needs before they are expressed, with genuine warmth and attention to detail.
  • ◊
    Kokoro — Heartfelt Service
    Service comes from the heart. Every interaction is an opportunity to create a memorable experience.
  • ◊
    Sasshi — Anticipatory Empathy
    Read the room. Understand unspoken needs and respond before being asked.
  • ◊
    Keigo — Respectful Language
    Master the three levels of Japanese honorific speech to address guests with appropriate formality.
  • ◊
    Wa — Harmony in Service
    Create balance and harmony in every guest interaction, from the physical environment to personal conduct.
