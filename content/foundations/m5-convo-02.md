## Understanding Hotel Keigo

Hotel Japanese is different from ordinary polite Japanese.

The goal is not simply to "speak politely," but to:

- reduce pressure on the guest
- create emotional comfort
- sound refined and trustworthy
- maintain professional distance without sounding cold

This is why hotel staff often use:

- honorific language (尊敬語)
- humble language (謙譲語)
- formal hospitality phrasing
- softened sentence endings

## Expression 1

ご予約のお名前をお聞かせいただけますでしょうか？

**Direct Meaning**

"May I ask for the name on your reservation?"

**Structural Analysis**

| Part | Role |
|------|------|
| ご予約 | honorable reservation expression |
| お名前 | beautified noun |
| お聞かせ | honorific formatting |
| いただく | humble receiving |
| ます | politeness |
| でしょうか | softened question |

**Why This Expression Is Important**

A beginner learner may say:

❌ お名前を教えてください

This is grammatically correct, but in hotel service it sounds:

- too direct
- too casual
- transactional

By contrast:

✅ お聞かせいただけますでしょうか

sounds:

- softer
- more respectful
- emotionally considerate

The guest feels guided rather than questioned.

## Expression 2

田中様でいらっしゃいますね

**Key Point**

The verb いらっしゃる elevates the guest.

Compare:

| Plain | Hospitality |
|-------|-------------|
| 田中さんですね | casual polite |
| 田中様でいらっしゃいますね | professional hospitality |

**Psychological Effect**

This phrasing:

- confirms identity politely
- avoids sounding interrogative
- creates smooth interaction flow

The ending 〜ですね also softens the confirmation.

## Expression 3

承っております

**Meaning**

"We are offering / serving"

**Why Hotels Use This Expression**

Hotels avoid simpler verbs like:

- やっています
- しています

because they sound operational rather than service-oriented.

承る originally carries the nuance of:

- respectfully receiving
- respectfully handling

This gives the hotel language a refined service tone.

## Expression 4

記載してございます

**Why Not Just 書いてあります？**

書いてあります is not wrong. However:

- it sounds ordinary
- it lacks hospitality tone
- it feels informational rather than service-oriented

記載してございます sounds:

- more formal
- more polished
- more suitable for premium customer interaction

## Quiz

**What is the main reason hotels use 承っております instead of しています？**

- A. しています is grammatically incorrect in formal settings
- B. 承る carries a nuance of respectfully receiving, giving a refined service tone
- C. しています is only used for casual situations with friends
- D. 承っております is easier for guests to understand

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Hotels avoid しています because it sounds operational rather than service-oriented. 承る carries the nuance of respectfully receiving or handling something, which gives the hotel language a refined, guest-centered tone.
