title: Essential Vocabulary for Handover
---

Every hotel has its own handover vocabulary. These words are not optional. Each term exists to communicate status quickly and without confusion. Using the right word at the right time shows you understand how the shift works.

## Core Operational Terms
| Japanese | English | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| 引き継ぎ (hikitsugi) | handover | The overall process of passing information between shifts |
| 申し送り (moushiokuri) | shift note / report | The written document or log entry itself |
| 対応中 (taiouchuu) | currently handling | An issue being worked on right now |
| 対応済み (taiouzumi) | already handled | An issue that has been resolved |
| 未対応 (mitaiou) | not handled yet | A request received but not acted upon |
| 要対応 (youtaiou) | requires action | Something that must be dealt with |
| 保留 (horyuu) | on hold | Waiting for more information or a decision |
| 確認待ち (kakuninmachi) | waiting confirmation | Awaiting approval or verification from another party |
| 完了 (kanryou) | completed | Finished with no further action needed |
| 依頼あり (irai ari) | request received | A request has been logged into the system |

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Status Flow</span>
依頼あり (received) → 対応中 (handling) → 対応済み / 完了 (done)<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>要対応</code></span> and <span class="keigo-jp"><code>確認待ち</code></span> mean the task is blocked. Never leave a task in these states without naming who is responsible.
</div>

## Guest Operation Terms

| Japanese | English | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| チェックイン対応 (chekkuin taiou) | check-in handling | Guest currently at check-in, VIP arrival |
| チェックアウト対応 (chekkuauto taiou) | check-out handling | Late check-out, express check-out requested |
| 延泊希望 (enhaku kibou) | extension request | Guest wants to stay additional nights |
| キャンセル (kyanseru) | cancellation | Reservation cancelled, partial or full |
| 部屋変更 (heya henkou) | room change | Guest moved or requested a different room |
| クレーム対応 (kureemu taiou) | complaint handling | Active complaint being managed |
| VIP対応 (VIP taiou) | VIP handling | Special attention required for VIPs |

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Key Distinction</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>クレーム対応</code></span> means a complaint is actively being handled. If resolved, update the status to <span class="keigo-jp"><code>対応済み</code></span> and include the resolution details.
</div>
<br><br>

## Facility and Technical Terms

| Japanese | English | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| 設備不良 (setsubi furyou) | facility issue | Any equipment not functioning correctly |
| 空調不良 (kuuchou furyou) | air conditioning issue | AC/heating not working in a room or area |
| 給湯不良 (kyuutou furyou) | hot water issue | No hot water or temperature problems |
| 水漏れ (mizu more) | water leak | Leak from pipe, ceiling, or fixture |
| 騒音クレーム (souon kureemu) | noise complaint | Guest complaints about noise from other rooms or outside |
| 故障中 (koshou chuu) | out of order | Equipment temporarily unusable |
| 修理手配済み (shuuri tehai zumi) | repair arranged | Repair has been scheduled or contractor contacted |

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Facility Reporting Rule</span>
Always include location + problem + status. Example: <span class="keigo-jp"><code>305号室：空調不良。修理手配済み。</code></span> Never write just "AC broken."
</div>
<br><br>

## Time and Status Expressions

| Japanese | English | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| 即対応 (soku taiou) | immediate action | Must be handled right now, cannot wait |
| 本日中 (honjitsu chuu) | within today | Must be completed before end of today's shift |
| 明日対応予定 (ashita taiou yotei) | planned for tomorrow | Scheduled for the next day |
| 夜間対応 (yakan taiou) | night shift handling | Assigned to the night shift team |
| 継続対応 (keizoku taiou) | ongoing handling | Will continue across multiple shifts |

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Time Sensitivity</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>即対応</code></span> is the most urgent label. If something is marked <span class="keigo-jp"><code>即対応</code></span>, the next staff should stop what they are doing and handle it first.
</div>

## Quiz

**Which term means "requires action" in a hotel handover?**

- A. 対応済み
- B. 要対応
- C. 完了
- D. 保留

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** 要対応 means "requires action." 対応済み means already handled, 完了 means completed, and 保留 means on hold.
