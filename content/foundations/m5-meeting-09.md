This lesson continues the written examples series with two cases that require more detail in the handover entry: pending requests that span shifts, and VIP arrivals that need specific preparation.

## Case 4: Pending Request (Incomplete Task)

A guest wants to extend their stay but room availability has not been confirmed.

<div class="keigo-pair">
<div class="bad-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Wrong</span>
<span><code>延泊したいって言ってました。まだ確認中です。</code></span><br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge bad">って quotation style</span> Casual spoken Japanese; not appropriate for written records.<br>
<strong>No room number:</strong> Incoming staff does not know which guest to follow up with.<br>
<strong>No owner:</strong> Who is confirming availability?
</div>
<div class="good-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Correct</span>
<span><code>501号室：延泊希望あり（1泊追加）。フロント確認待ち。</code></span><br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge good">Room + Request + Owner</span> Complete and actionable.<br>
<strong>Specific request:</strong> 1 additional night tells the front desk exactly what to confirm.
</div>
</div>

The 確認待ち status is only complete when it names who is waiting. フロント確認待ち tells the incoming staff exactly where to follow up. 確認待ち alone leaves ownership unclear.

## Case 5: VIP Arrival (Priority Guest)

A VIP guest is scheduled to arrive during the evening shift.

<div class="keigo-pair">
<div class="bad-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Wrong</span>
<span><code>VIPのお客様が来るかもしれないので、よろしくお願いします。</code></span><br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge bad">かもしれない</span> Uncertainty is not acceptable for VIP notifications.<br>
<strong>よろしくお願いします:</strong> Not an operational instruction; incoming staff does not know what is expected.
</div>
<div class="good-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Correct</span>
<span><code>【VIP対応】山田様（305号室）：18:00頃ご到着予定。ウェルカムフルーツ準備済み。チェックイン時マネージャー対応。</code></span><br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge good">All required information</span> Who, which room, when, what is prepared, who should handle.
</div>
</div>

VIP entries use the 【VIP対応】 header to make them immediately visible when scanning the note. Every VIP entry must include: guest name and room, arrival time, preparation status, and handling instruction.

## What Makes Pending and VIP Entries Different

Completed tasks (対応済み) require only three elements: room, issue, status. Pending and VIP entries require more because they create work for the incoming shift.

| Entry type | Required elements |
|---|---|
| <span>対応済み</span> (completed) | Room + Issue + Status |
| <span>確認待ち</span> (pending) | Room + Issue + Owner + Deadline |
| <span>VIP対応</span> (VIP) | Name + Room + Arrival + Preparation + Handler |

## Quiz

**A VIP guest is arriving at 19:00 and welcome flowers have been arranged by the concierge. Which entry is correct?**

- A. VIPが来ます。よろしく。
- B. 【VIP対応】鈴木様（801号室）：19:00ご到着予定。ウェルカムフラワー準備済み（コンシェルジュ）。チェックイン時マネージャー対応。
- C. VIPゲストがいらっしゃいます。確認お願いします。
- D. 801号室に鈴木様が来るかもしれません。

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A VIP entry must contain: the guest name, room number, confirmed arrival time, preparation status with responsible party, and the handling instruction. Option B provides all of these. Option A is informal and gives no actionable information. Option C omits the arrival time, room number, and preparation details. Option D uses かもしれません (might come), which introduces unacceptable uncertainty into a VIP notification; arrival time should be confirmed before writing the entry.
