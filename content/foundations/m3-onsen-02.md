title: Onsen Etiquette — The Rules of the Bath
---

The communal onsen operates according to a set of customs that are not merely polite suggestions — they are the shared code that makes the experience safe, comfortable, and meaningful for everyone present. For hospitality professionals, knowing these rules in detail is essential for guiding guests confidently.

**Before entering the bath:**

1. **Remove all clothing and leave it in the changing room.** Onsen are entered without swimwear in most traditional settings.
2. **Wash thoroughly at the shower station (カラン, *karan*).** Every bather must wash and rinse completely before entering the communal bath. This is both a hygiene requirement and a cultural expectation — entering the bath without washing is a serious breach.
3. **Rinse the shower station after use.** Leave the space ready for the next person.

**In the bath:**

- **Enter slowly and quietly.** Onsen are places of calm. Splashing, loud conversation, and running are inappropriate.
- **Small towel handling:** The small modesty towel (*tenugui*) is not taken into the bath. It may be placed folded on the head or left at the bath's edge.
- **No swimming or vigorous movement.** The bath is for soaking, not exercise.
- **Time your soak.** Onsen water is hot, typically 40–44°C. First-time bathers should soak in shorter intervals (10–15 minutes) and rest between sessions.
- **Hair must not touch the water.** Long hair should be tied up or pinned.

**After bathing:**

- Rinse briefly in the shower before returning to the changing room.
- Pat dry before leaving the wet area to avoid slipping and to keep the changing room floor dry.
- Do not use a hair dryer in the wet area.

## Key Phrases

**カラン (karan)** — the individual shower station at an onsen facility used for washing thoroughly before entering the communal bath; using it is mandatory, not optional

**ご入浴前に、シャワーでお体をよくお流しください。** *(Go-nyūyoku mae ni, shawā de o-karada o yoku o-nagashi kudasai.)*
"Please wash thoroughly at the shower station before entering the bath." — the essential instruction for all first-time onsen guests; delivered warmly at the changing room entrance or in the orientation

**小タオルはお湯の中にお入れにならないようお願いいたします。** *(Ko-taoru wa o-yu no naka ni o-ire ni naranai yō o-negai itashimasu.)*
"Please keep the small towel out of the water." — a key etiquette point that many first-time onsen visitors overlook; can be explained gently alongside the towel's permitted uses (folded on the head or left at the bath edge)

## Quiz

**What must every bather do before entering the communal onsen bath?**

- A. Soak feet in a footbath for five minutes
- B. Wash and rinse thoroughly at the shower station
- C. Request a towel from the attendant
- D. Sign a hygiene declaration form

**Correct Answer:** Wash and rinse thoroughly at the shower station

**Explanation:** Washing completely before entering is both a hygiene requirement and a fundamental social expectation in onsen culture. Entering the communal bath without washing is considered deeply inconsiderate to other bathers.
