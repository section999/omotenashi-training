title: The Professional Handover Standard
---

The technical skills of handover writing are learnable in hours. The professional standard is something different. It is the habit of thinking about the next shift before you think about your own convenience.

## What the Professional Standard Looks Like

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Traceable</span>
<em>Can the incoming staff trace every guest situation from start to current state?</em><br><br>
Every entry must include who, what, and what was done. Time, room number, and responsible party should always be present.
</div>

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Actionable</span>
<em>Does every entry tell the incoming staff what to do?</em><br><br>
If an entry contains only a problem with no status or next step, it is not actionable. It is noise.
</div>

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Complete</span>
<em>Is there anything the incoming staff would need to ask about?</em><br><br>
If the answer is yes, the handover is not done yet.
</div>

## The Shift-End Mindset

Hotel shifts do not end when the clock changes. They end when the incoming staff has everything they need to take over without any gaps. This means finishing the handover is part of the job, not an afterthought. Your shift continues until the handover is complete.

## Quiz

**Which of the following best describes a "traceable" handover entry?**

- A. An entry that uses formal Japanese
- B. An entry that includes who, what, and what was done; with room number and responsible party
- C. An entry that is brief and does not take time to read
- D. An entry that the outgoing staff reviewed before submitting

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Traceability means the incoming staff can follow every guest situation from start to current state. This requires three elements: who is involved (room number or guest name), what happened (the issue or request), and what was done (the status label and responsible party). Formal language, brevity, and self-review are all valuable but none of them constitutes traceability on their own.
