title: Opening and Conducting the Verbal Briefing
---

Written handover notes cover what happened during the shift. Verbal handover is what happens face-to-face when the outgoing staff briefs the incoming team. Both are required for professional shift transitions.

## Why Verbal Handover Matters

Written notes capture facts. Verbal briefings add the nuance that notes cannot easily convey: tone of voice, the level of urgency, the emotional state of a difficult guest, or the context behind an unusual decision.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<strong>Urgency cues:</strong> "This guest is expecting a callback in the next 30 minutes."<br>
<strong>Context:</strong> "The noise complaint was resolved, but the guest seemed unsatisfied."<br>
<strong>Judgment guidance:</strong> "If the VIP in 305 calls, escalate immediately to the manager."<br>
<strong>Real-time updates:</strong> Information that occurred in the last few minutes before handover.
</div>

None of this nuance fits cleanly into the structured format of a written entry. The verbal briefing is where these details are passed on.

## Opening the Verbal Briefing

A clear opening signals to the incoming staff that the briefing has begun and they should focus.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>では、引き継ぎをお伝えいたします。</code></span><br>
<em>dewa, hikitsugi o otsutae itashimasu.</em><br>
I will now brief you on the handover.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>まず、最優先事項からご説明いたします。</code></span><br>
<em>mazu, sai yusen jikou kara go-setsumei itashimasu.</em><br>
I will start with the priority items.
</div>

## Conducting the Briefing

The verbal briefing follows the same five-section order as written notes: priority guests first, then active problems, room status, pending tasks, and special notes.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>305号室の山田様がVIPでいらっしゃいます。</code></span><br>
<em>305 goushitsu no Yamada-sama ga VIP de irasshaimasu.</em><br>
Mr./Ms. Yamada in Room 305 is a VIP guest.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>到着時は必ずマネージャーが対応するようにお願いします。</code></span><br>
<em>touchaku ji wa kanarazu maneja ga taiou suru you ni onegai shimasu.</em><br>
Please make sure a manager handles the arrival.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>クレームが1件継続中です。詳細は申し送りにございます。</code></span><br>
<em>kuremu ga 1-ken keizokuchuu desu. shousai wa moushiokuri ni gozaimasu.</em><br>
One complaint is still ongoing. The details are in the written notes.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>210号室の騒音クレームは夜間対応済みですが、経過の確認をお願いします。</code></span><br>
<em>210 goushitsu no souon kuremu wa yakan taiouzumi desu ga, keika no kakunin o onegai shimasu.</em><br>
The noise complaint for Room 210 was handled during the night shift, but please check in on it.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>305号室のエアコンは明日修理予定です。今夜もし連絡があればすぐ対応を。</code></span><br>
<em>305 goushitsu no eakon wa ashita shuri yotei desu. kon'ya moshi renraku ga areba sugu taiou o.</em><br>
Room 305's AC repair is scheduled for tomorrow. If they call tonight, please attend to it immediately.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>これが本日の未対応タスク一覧です。優先順位をご確認ください。</code></span><br>
<em>kore ga honjitsu no mitaiou tasuku ichiran desu. yusen jun'i o go-kakunin kudasai.</em><br>
Here is today's list of pending tasks. Please check the priority order.
</div>

## Quiz

**What is the correct opening phrase for a verbal handover briefing?**

- A. じゃ、説明します。
- B. では、引き継ぎをお伝えいたします。
- C. 今日のことを話します。
- D. 聞いてください。

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** では、引き継ぎをお伝えいたします uses formal register appropriate for internal professional communication: では signals a structured transition, 引き継ぎ is the operational term for handover, and お伝えいたします is the humble form of conveying information. The opening phrase matters because it cues the incoming staff that the briefing has started and they should focus. Options A, C, and D are casual and do not communicate the professional structure of a shift transition.
