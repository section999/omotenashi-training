title: お vs ご Prefix Rules
---

In hotel keigo, **お** and **ご** are beautifying prefixes (美化語 / bikago) added to nouns to make language polite and guest-oriented. They signal that the speaker is treating the listener with respect. However, choosing the wrong prefix sounds unnatural to native speakers and can make even correct keigo feel slightly off.

## The Basic Rule

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">The Rule</span>
<span class="keigo-badge info">お</span><br>
Used with <strong>native Japanese words</strong> (和語 / wago).<br>
These often have single hiragana readings: <span class="keigo-jp"><code>お部屋</code></span> (へや), <span class="keigo-jp"><code>お金</code></span> (かね), <span class="keigo-jp"><code>お酒</code></span> (さけ), <span class="keigo-jp"><code>お水</code></span> (みず)<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">ご</span><br>
Used with <strong>Chinese-origin words</strong> (漢語 / kango).<br>
These have on'yomi readings and often appear as two-kanji compounds: <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご予約</code></span> (よやく), <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご確認</code></span> (かくにん), <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご連絡</code></span> (れんらく), <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご利用</code></span> (りよう)
</div>

|---|---|---|

| お手洗い | 手洗い | restroom |

## Key Phrases

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Examples with お</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お荷物をお預かりいたします。</code></span><br>
・ o nimotsu o oazukari itashimasu (I will hold your luggage)<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お部屋のご案内をいたします。</code></span><br>
・ o heya no goannai o itashimasu (I will show you to your room)<br><br>

・ ocha o omochi shimashita (I have brought your tea)
</div>

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Examples with ご</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご予約を確認いたしました。</code></span><br>

## Quiz

**Which prefix is correct for 予約 in hotel keigo?**

- A. お予約
- B. ご予約
- C. No prefix needed
- D. おご予約

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** 予約 is a 漢語 (Chinese-origin word), so it takes ご. お is used with 和語 (Japanese-origin words).
