title: The Three Functions of Keigo in Hotels
---

Hotel keigo is based on three operational functions. These functions constantly work together during guest interactions. Professional staff switch naturally between them depending on who is acting, who receives the action, and what atmosphere is required.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">尊敬語 &mdash; Respect the Guest</span>
Sonkeigo is used when describing the guest's actions, condition, or status.

<span class="keigo-badge info">Purpose</span> Elevate the guest, show respect, maintain professional distance<br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Examples</span> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>いらっしゃいます</code></span>, <span class="keigo-jp"><code>お召し上がりになります</code></span>, <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご覧になります</code></span>, <span class="keigo-jp"><code>お休みになります</code></span><br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Example</span> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>お客様はもうお休みになりました。</code></span>

The staff raises the guest linguistically. This is extremely important in Japanese hospitality culture.
</div>

<div class="keigo-card green">
<span class="keigo-card-title">謙譲語 &mdash; Lower the Staff Position</span>
Kenjōgo is used when staff describe their own actions.

<span class="keigo-badge info">Purpose</span> Show humility, show service attitude, place the guest above the staff<br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Examples</span> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>お伺いします</code></span>, <span class="keigo-jp"><code>承ります</code></span>, <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご案内いたします</code></span>, <span class="keigo-jp"><code>確認いたします</code></span><br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Example</span> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>担当者がお伺いいたします。</code></span>

The staff intentionally lowers themselves linguistically. This creates the feeling: "We are here to serve you."
</div>

<div class="keigo-card amber">
<span class="keigo-card-title">丁寧語 &mdash; Maintain Professional Tone</span>
Teineigo creates the general atmosphere of professional communication.

<span class="keigo-badge info">Purpose</span> Sound calm, sound polished, avoid roughness<br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Examples</span> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>〜でございます</code></span>, <span class="keigo-jp"><code>〜しております</code></span>, <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ありがとうございます</code></span><br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Example</span> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>朝食は1階でございます。</code></span>

This is not strongly respectful or humble. It simply sounds professional and refined.
</div>

## All Three Together

In real hotel operations, all three types are mixed constantly.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Natural Blending</span>
<code>山田様でいらっしゃいますね。</code> <span class="keigo-badge info">尊敬語</span><br>
<code>こちらがお部屋の鍵でございます。</code> <span class="keigo-badge info">丁寧語</span><br>
<code>スタッフがご案内いたします。</code> <span class="keigo-badge info">謙譲語</span>
<br><br>
Within three sentences, guest elevation, professional tone, and staff humility are all used together naturally.
</div>

## Quiz

**Which keigo type is used to describe a guest's action such as お休みになりました？**

- A. 謙譲語 (Kenjōgo)
- B. 尊敬語 (Sonkeigo)
- C. 丁寧語 (Teineigo)
- D. casual language

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** 尊敬語 (Sonkeigo) elevates the guest by using respectful forms for the guest's actions, condition, or status.
