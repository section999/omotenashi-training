title: Winter, Warmth, Gratitude, and New Year
---

Winter in Japanese hospitality is shaped by two emotional poles: the intimacy of warmth against the cold, and the solemn gratitude of the year's end. The period from December through January is among the most culturally rich in the hospitality calendar, culminating in *oshōgatsu* (お正月), the New Year.

**Seasonal greetings (December–February):**
- *"寒い日が続いておりますが、どうかお体にお気をつけください。"*, "Cold days continue, please take care of yourself."
- *"本年もお世話になりました。来年もどうぞよろしくお願いいたします。"*, "Thank you for your support this year. We look forward to continuing our relationship next year."
- *"明けましておめでとうございます。本年もよろしくお願い申し上げます。"*, New Year's greeting (used from January 1st until approximately January 7th).

**Environment:**
- Traditional New Year's decorations: *kadomatsu* (門松, pine and bamboo gate decorations), *shimekazari* (注連飾り, sacred rope ornaments), *kagami mochi* (鏡餅, stacked rice cakes).
- Warm lighting, dark lacquerware, heavy ceramics with winter motifs.
- After New Year, subtle plum blossom (*ume*) themes signal the arrival of late winter.

**Food and drink:**
- *Osechi ryōri* (おせち料理): the traditional New Year's meal, each dish carrying symbolic meaning (black beans for health, herring roe for fertility, and so on). Knowing the symbolism allows you to explain the meal meaningfully to guests.
- Winter hot pot (*nabe*): communal, warming, deeply seasonal.
- Warming sake, amazake (甘酒, sweet fermented rice drink), and yuzu-based drinks.

**Year-end gratitude:**
The concept of *kansha* (感謝, gratitude) intensifies at year's end. For hospitality professionals, this is a time to express genuine appreciation to returning guests. Handwritten notes, small seasonal gifts, and personal acknowledgements carry real weight in this period. That said, they matter more at this time than at any other point in the year.

## Key Phrases

**寒い日が続いておりますが、どうかお体にお気をつけください。** *(Samui hi ga tsuzuite orimasu ga, dōka o-karada ni o-ki wo tsukete kudasai.)*
"Cold days continue: please take care of yourself."

**明けましておめでとうございます。本年もよろしくお願い申し上げます。** *(Akemashite omedetō gozaimasu. Honnen mo yoroshiku o-negai mōshiagemasu.)*
"Happy New Year. I look forward to your continued support this year.": used from January 1st to approximately January 7th

**本年もお世話になりました。来年もどうぞよろしくお願いいたします。** *(Honnen mo o-sewa ni narimashita. Rainen mo dōzo yoroshiku o-negai itashimasu.)*
"Thank you for your support this year. We look forward to continuing our relationship next year."

## Quiz

**Until approximately when is it appropriate to use the New Year's greeting "明けましておめでとうございます"?**

- A. Until the end of January
- B. Until approximately January 7th
- C. Until February: it is used through winter
- D. Only on January 1st itself

**Correct Answer:** Until approximately January 7th

**Explanation:** In Japanese culture, the New Year's greeting is used from January 1st until around January 7th (松の内, *matsu no uchi*: the period when New Year's decorations remain displayed). After this period, standard greetings resume.
