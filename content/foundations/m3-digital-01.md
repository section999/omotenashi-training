title: Digital First Impressions — Email and Enquiry Etiquette
---

For many guests, the first contact with a property is not in person — it is a written enquiry by email or contact form. This message arrives before any human interaction, and the response to it is the first experience the guest has of the property's hospitality. It may determine whether they book or turn elsewhere.

**The email as a service act:**

A well-written reply to a guest enquiry demonstrates the same qualities as excellent in-person service: attentiveness, care, and expertise. A poorly written one — delayed, generic, or careless — signals that the property's attention to detail does not extend to communication.

**Structure of a professional email response:**

1. **Salutation**: Address the guest by name and with appropriate formality. *"Dear Mr Smith"* or *"Dear Ms Yamada"* — never *"Hi there"* or *"Hello,"* without a name.
2. **Acknowledgement**: Thank the guest for their enquiry before answering it. *"Thank you for your enquiry regarding our property."*
3. **Response**: Answer the question clearly and completely. Do not omit parts of the enquiry and force a follow-up.
4. **Proactive information**: Anticipate the guest's next question. If they ask about room types, also mention seasonal activities or current promotions that may be relevant.
5. **Close**: Invite further questions warmly. *"Please do not hesitate to contact me directly if you have any further questions."*
6. **Signature**: Full name, title, property name, direct contact number.

**Response time:**

The standard for hospitality email enquiries is within 24 hours — ideally within four hours during business hours. A delayed response communicates indifference. If the full answer requires time, send an acknowledgement within the hour: *"Thank you for your message — I am checking the details you need and will come back to you shortly."*

## Key Phrases

**お問い合わせいただきありがとうございます。** *(O-toiawase itadaki arigatō gozaimasu.)*
"Thank you for your enquiry." — the standard opening line for any professional email response to a guest; acknowledges the guest's contact before addressing the substance

**詳細につきましては確認次第ご連絡いたします。** *(Shōsai ni tsukimashite wa kakunin shidai go-renraku itashimasu.)*
"I will contact you with the details as soon as I have confirmed them." — the correct phrase when a full answer requires time; acknowledges the enquiry and commits to a follow-up rather than leaving the guest waiting in silence

**何かご不明な点がございましたら、お気軽にお問い合わせください。** *(Nanika go-fumei na ten ga gozaimashitara, o-ki garu ni o-toiawase kudasai.)*
"Please feel free to contact us if you have any questions." — the standard professional email closing; invites further dialogue and signals genuine willingness to assist

## Quiz

**A guest enquiry asks about room availability and dining options. The room is available but the dining question requires checking with the restaurant team. What should you do?**

- A. Reply only about the room availability and wait until you have all information before mentioning dining
- B. Acknowledge the full enquiry, answer what you can immediately, and confirm you will follow up on the dining question with a specific timeframe
- C. Forward the email to the restaurant team and ask them to reply directly
- D. Reply that you will have a full answer within 48 hours

**Correct Answer:** Acknowledge the full enquiry, answer what you can immediately, and confirm you will follow up on the dining question with a specific timeframe

**Explanation:** A partial response with a clear commitment to follow up is more professional than a delayed complete response. The guest knows their full enquiry has been received and that a timely answer is coming.
