This lesson continues from the previous lesson's standards with three additional cross-department scenarios: a concierge-to-restaurant reservation, a reverse direction exchange (housekeeping contacting the front desk), and the important case where the receiving department cannot fulfill the request immediately.

## Concierge to Restaurant

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<strong>Concierge:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>コンシェルジュの〇〇です。本日の夕食のご予約で、202号室の田中様、2名様、19時でお願いできますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>konsheruju no 〇 〇 desu. honjitsu no yushoku no go-yoyaku de, 202 gou shitsu no tanaka sama, 2 mei sama, 19 ji de onegai dekimasu deshou ka.</em><br>
This is [Name] from the concierge desk. I would like to make a dinner reservation for Mr./Ms. Tanaka in Room 202, two guests at 7:00 PM tonight. Is that possible?<br><br>
<strong>Restaurant:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>承りました。田中様、2名様、19時でございますね。</code></span><br>
<em>uketamawarimashita. tanaka sama, 2 mei sama, 19 ji de gozaimasu ne.</em><br>
Understood. Mr./Ms. Tanaka, two guests, 7:00 PM.
</div>

## Housekeeping to Front Desk (Reverse Direction)

Cross-department communication also flows in reverse, with departments contacting the front desk to pass information back:

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<strong>Housekeeping:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ハウスキーピングです。605号室の清掃が完了いたしました。</code></span><br>
<em>hausukipingu desu. 605 gou shitsu no seisou ga kanryou itashimashita.</em><br>
This is housekeeping. Cleaning of Room 605 is complete.<br><br>
<strong>Front Desk:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ありがとうございます。確認いたしました。</code></span><br>
<em>arigatou gozaimasu. kakunin itashimashita.</em><br>
Thank you. Noted.<br><br>
<strong>Housekeeping:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>なお、バスルームのシャワーヘッドが古くなっておりましたのでご連絡いたします。</code></span><br>
<em>nao, basu rumu no shawa heddo ga furuku natte orimashita node go-renraku itashimasu.</em><br>
Also, I am letting you know that the shower head in the bathroom is aging.
</div>

## When the Other Department Cannot Fulfill the Request

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ただいま手が離せない状況ですが、〇〇分後には対応できます。</code></span><br>
<em>tadaima te ga hanase nai joukyou desu ga, 〇 〇 fun go ni wa taiou dekimasu.</em><br>
We are currently occupied, but we can address it in approximately [X] minutes.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>申し訳ありませんが、現在対応が難しい状況です。上の者に確認してご連絡いたします。</code></span><br>
<em>moushiwake ari mase n ga, genzai taiou ga muzukashii joukyou desu. ue no mono ni kakunin shite go-renraku itashimasu.</em><br>
I am sorry, but we cannot handle that at this moment. I will check with my supervisor and get back to you.
</div>

Upon hearing this, the requesting department should relay the updated timeline to the guest, not leave them waiting without explanation.

## Quiz

**The front desk receives a report from a guest in Room 412 that the room heating is not working. The staff member is about to call maintenance. Which is the correct opening for this cross-department call?**

- A. もしもし、フロントですけど、412号室が寒いって言ってます
- B. フロントの〇〇です。412号室のお客様より暖房が作動していないとのご連絡がございました。早急にご対応いただけますでしょうか
- C. お電話ありがとうございます
- D. 412号室に行ってください

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Option B follows the cross-department standard: self-identification (フロントの〇〇です), room number and guest issue stated immediately, and a clear request with urgency framing (早急にご対応いただけますでしょうか). Option A is too casual and imprecise. Option C is a guest-facing phone opening that is inappropriate for an internal call. Option D is a direct instruction without context or professional framing, which can create friction between departments and does not confirm whether the request can be fulfilled.
