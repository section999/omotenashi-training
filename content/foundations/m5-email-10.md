title: Relationship-Building Closings
---

For repeat guests and loyal customers, hotels often use closings that strengthen the ongoing relationship.

Common expressions include:

今後ともよろしくお願い申し上げます。

or

今後とも変わらぬご愛顧のほどよろしくお願い申し上げます。

These expressions communicate:

- appreciation,
- respect,
- and hope for continued patronage.

The phrase:

「今後とも」

means:

"from now on as well"
or "going forward."

This wording naturally fits repeat guests because it assumes a continuing relationship.

The longer version:

「変わらぬご愛顧」

means:

"your continued patronage and support."

This is often used for:

- VIP customers,
- luxury hotel guests,
- members,
- and long-term clients.

These closings help create loyalty and long-term trust between the hotel and the guest.

However, they should generally not be used for first-time guests because they assume an already established relationship.

---

## Quiz

**Why should 「今後ともよろしくお願い申し上げます」 generally not be used for first-time guests?**

- A. It is too casual
- B. It assumes an already established relationship
- C. It sounds angry
- D. It is too short

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** 「今後とも」 means "going forward" and assumes a continuing relationship. Using it for a first-time guest sounds presumptuous and unnatural.
