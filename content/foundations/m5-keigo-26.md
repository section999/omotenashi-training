title: Edge Cases: Receiving and Telephone
---

Two more edge cases require careful judgment: situations where the staff is the recipient of the guest's action, and telephone interactions where there are no visual cues to confirm who is speaking.

## Edge Case 3: When the Staff Member Is the Recipient

Sometimes staff receive something from a guest (a form, a payment, a request). The humble verb いただく applies even when the physical action is the guest handing something over; receiving a guest's action is still a service act.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>こちらの用紙にご記入いただけますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>kochira no youshi ni go-kinyu itadakemasu deshou ka.</em><br>
Could you fill in this form?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>カードをお預かりいたします。</code></span><br>
<em>kaado o o-azukari itashimasu.</em><br>
I will take your card.
</div>

In the first phrase, the staff humbly receives the guest's completed form (いただく). In the second, the staff humbly holds the card (お預かりいたします). When the guest gives and the staff receives, humble language applies because the act of receiving is in service of the guest.

## Edge Case 4: Telephone Context

On the telephone, there is no visual confirmation of who is speaking. Keigo must be applied based on conversational role alone.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>田中様がご予約なさっているとのことでございますね。</code></span><br>
<em>tanaka-sama ga go-yoyaku nasatte iru to no koto de gozaimasu ne.</em><br>
I understand you have a reservation, Mr./Ms. Tanaka.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ただいまご確認いたします。少々お待ちくださいませ。</code></span><br>
<em>tadaima go-kakunin itashimasu. shoushou o-machi kudasaimase.</em><br>
I will confirm that now. Please wait a moment.
</div>

When the caller is the subject (performing an action), apply 尊敬語 to that action. When the staff member commits to an action, apply 謙譲語.

## Putting All Four Edge Cases Together

| Situation | Subject | Keigo |
|---|---|---|
| Colleague's presence | Colleague | おります (not いらっしゃいます) |
| Guest's family | Family member | 尊敬語 (same as guest) |
| Staff receiving from guest | Staff | いただく / お預かりいたします |
| Telephone, caller acts | Caller (= guest) | 尊敬語 |

## Quiz

**A guest requests a wake-up call. Which response is most appropriate?**

- A. わかりました。セットします。
- B. かしこまりました。ご指定の時間に承りました。
- C. 了解です。やっておきます。
- D. はい、わかりました。

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** かしこまりました acknowledges the guest's instruction at hotel register. ご指定の時間に承りました uses 承ります (the humble form for formally receiving requests) and ご指定 (ご prefix for the 漢語 word 指定, the guest's specification). This is the appropriate response for receiving a service request. Options A, C, and D use わかりました or 了解, which are acceptable in internal communication but fall below hotel keigo standard for guest-facing interactions.
