title: Difficult Situations and Graceful Refusals
---

Some of the most challenging interactions in hospitality involve situations where the hotel cannot fulfill a guest's expectation — a lost reservation, a fully booked property, or a request that falls outside what is possible. How a hotel handles these moments reveals the true depth of its service culture.

The key principle: **a professional never says "impossible" directly**. Instead, they offer the closest possible alternative, frame limitations as circumstances rather than policies, and maintain the guest's dignity throughout.

## Situation 1: Reservation Not Found

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">No Reservation Found Script</span>
<strong>Guest:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>田中で予約しているはずなのですが。</code></span><br>
I should have a reservation under Tanaka.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>かしこまりました。確認いたします。少々お待ちくださいませ。</code></span><br>
Certainly. I will check. Please wait a moment.<br><br>
<em>[After checking — reservation not found]</em><br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>大変恐れ入りますが、現在の予約システムでは田中様のご予約が確認できない状況でございます。</code></span><br>
I am very sorry, but we are currently unable to locate a reservation under the name Tanaka in our system.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご予約の確認番号やご利用になったご予約サイトがございましたら、お教えいただけますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
Do you happen to have a confirmation number or the name of the booking site you used?<br><br>
<em>[If still not found and rooms are available]</em><br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>誠に申し訳ございません。本日はお部屋のご用意が可能でございますので、改めてご手配させていただけますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
We sincerely apologize for this situation. As we do have availability tonight, may we arrange a room for you now?
</div>

## Situation 2: Walk-in Guest When Fully Booked

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Fully Booked Walk-in Script</span>
<strong>Guest:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>今夜泊まれますか。予約はしていないのですが。</code></span><br>
Can I stay tonight? I don't have a reservation.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご来店いただきましてありがとうございます。ただいまの空き状況を確認いたします。</code></span><br>
Thank you for coming in. I will check our availability now.<br><br>
<em>[Fully booked]</em><br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>あいにく本日は満室となっております。大変申し訳ございません。</code></span><br>
Unfortunately, we are fully booked tonight. We are very sorry.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>もしよろしければ、近隣の提携ホテルをご案内することも可能でございます。いかがでしょうか。</code></span><br>
If you would like, I can direct you to a nearby partner hotel. Would that be helpful?
</div>

Note: Suggesting a nearby hotel in a walk-in fully-booked situation is appropriate because the hotel has no room to offer. This differs from availability calls (lesson 6) where you always try to offer alternative dates first.

## Situation 3: Guest Insists on an Unavailable Room

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Unavailable Room Request Script</span>
<strong>Guest:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>前回泊まった部屋と同じ部屋にしてほしいんですが、701号室です。</code></span><br>
I'd like the same room I stayed in last time — Room 701.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>かしこまりました。確認いたします。</code></span><br>
Certainly. Let me check.<br><br>
<em>[Room 701 is occupied]</em><br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>誠に恐れ入りますが、701号室は現在ご利用中でございまして、ご用意が難しい状況でございます。</code></span><br>
I am truly sorry, but Room 701 is currently occupied and unfortunately cannot be offered at this time.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>同じ階の同タイプのお部屋でしたらご用意が可能でございます。眺望も同様でございますが、いかがでしょうか。</code></span><br>
We do have the same type of room available on the same floor, with a similar view. Would that work for you?
</div>

## The Language of Graceful Refusal

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Key Refusal Phrases</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>あいにく〜</code></span><br>
Unfortunately... (soft, expresses genuine regret)<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>誠に恐れ入りますが〜</code></span><br>
I am truly sorry, but... (formal, softens a limitation)<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>〜が難しい状況でございます</code></span><br>
It is difficult to 〜 at this time (frames impossibility as circumstance, not policy)<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>〜でしたらご用意が可能でございます</code></span><br>
We are able to offer 〜 (alternative) — always follow a refusal with an offer
</div>

## Situation 4: Request That Exceeds Hotel Capabilities

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Exceeding Capability Script</span>
<strong>Guest:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>今夜、部屋にピアノを用意してもらえますか。</code></span><br>
Could you arrange a piano in my room tonight?<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>誠にありがたいご要望でございますが、ピアノのご用意につきましては当ホテルのご対応範囲外となっておりまして、誠に恐れ入ります。</code></span><br>
Thank you very much for the request. I am truly sorry, but arranging a piano is outside of what our hotel is able to provide.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ただ、ご要望の内容によっては、別の形でご対応できる場合もございますので、もう少しお聞かせいただけますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
However, depending on the underlying need, there may be another way we could help. Could you tell me a little more?
</div>

## The Principle Behind Graceful Refusals

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Why the Language Matters</span>
Saying 「できません」 (We cannot do that) is factually identical to 「難しい状況でございます」 (It is difficult at this time) — but they communicate entirely different things.<br><br>
できません closes the conversation and positions the hotel as inflexible.<br>
難しい状況でございます opens a space for alternatives and positions the hotel as genuinely trying.<br><br>
The guest's emotional response depends not just on what is said — but on how much care is communicated in the saying of it.
</div>

<div class="keigo-card blue">

## Quiz

**A guest insists on staying in Room 701, but it is currently occupied. Which response is most appropriate?**

- A. 「701号室はできません」— 701 is not possible.
- B. 「701号室は今は無理です」— 701 is impossible right now.
- C. 「誠に恐れ入りますが、701号室は現在ご利用中でございます。同階の同タイプのお部屋でしたらご用意が可能でございます」
- D. Ask the current guest in Room 701 to move

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The correct approach frames the limitation as circumstance (現在ご利用中でございます — currently in use) rather than a flat refusal, and immediately offers an alternative of equivalent value. できません and 無理です both close the conversation with no resolution offered, which damages the guest relationship. Always soften the refusal with 誠に恐れ入りますが and follow with a genuine alternative.
