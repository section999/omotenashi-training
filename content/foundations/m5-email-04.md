title: First-Time Guest Greetings
---

A first-time guest has no prior relationship with the hotel. The opening must close that gap by expressing gratitude and warmth from the first sentence.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Standard First-Time Greeting</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>この度は、ご予約いただき、誠にありがとうございます。</code></span>
<br>
<em>kono tabi wa, goyoyaku itadaki, makoto ni arigatou gozaimasu</em>
<br>
"Thank you very much for making a reservation on this occasion."
</div>

Breaking this down:

| Part | Japanese | Meaning |
|------|----------|---------|
| Opening phrase | この度は | "On this occasion": marks this as a new relationship |
| Action | ご予約いただき | "for making a reservation": humble form showing respect |
| Gratitude | 誠にありがとうございます | "sincerely thank you": polite and warm |

The key phrase is **この度は** ("on this occasion"). It signals that the hotel recognizes this is a first interaction. Using it correctly tells the guest: "We see you as a new guest and welcome you accordingly."

Using a repeat guest expression like **いつもお世話になっております** for a first-timer would sound automated and careless.

Common uses:

<span class="keigo-badge info">Reservation</span> confirmations
<span class="keigo-badge info">Pre-arrival</span> emails
<span class="keigo-badge info">Welcome</span> guidance
<span class="keigo-badge info">Inquiry</span> responses

The guest should feel the hotel is genuinely happy to welcome them. For international guests, this warmth builds trust before arrival.

## Quiz

**What does 「この度は」 imply when used in a hotel email?**

- A. This is a new interaction or relationship
- B. This is an emergency
- C. This is an internal message
- D. This is a regular customer

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:** 「この度は」 means "on this occasion" or "this time," naturally fitting a new interaction or first-time relationship with a guest.
