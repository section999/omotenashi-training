title: The お Prefix
---

In hotel keigo, the prefixes お and ご are added to nouns and verb stems to create a polished, guest-oriented tone. Choosing the wrong prefix sounds unnatural and can undermine otherwise correct keigo. This lesson covers お.

## The Basic Rule

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-badge info">お</span> Native Japanese words (和語 / wago)<br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お部屋</code></span> (へや), <span class="keigo-jp"><code>お荷物</code></span> (にもつ), <span class="keigo-jp"><code>お水</code></span> (みず)<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">ご</span> Chinese-origin words (漢語 / kango)<br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご予約</code></span> (よやく), <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご確認</code></span> (かくにん), <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご利用</code></span> (りよう)
</div>

お is used with native Japanese words (和語) with kun'yomi readings. ご is used with Chinese-origin words (漢語) with on'yomi readings, which often appear as two-kanji compounds.

## Common Hotel Words with お

| <span>With お</span> | Plain Form | English |
|---|---|---|
| <span>お部屋</span> | <span>部屋</span> | room |
| <span>お荷物</span> | <span>荷物</span> | luggage |
| <span>お水</span> | <span>水</span> | water |
| <span>お酒</span> | <span>酒</span> | alcohol |
| <span>お茶</span> | <span>茶</span> | tea |
| <span>お食事</span> | <span>食事</span> | meal |
| <span>お風呂</span> | <span>風呂</span> | bath |
| <span>お手洗い</span> | <span>手洗い</span> | restroom |
| <span>お名前</span> | <span>名前</span> | name |
| <span>お布団</span> | <span>布団</span> | futon |

## お in Sentences

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お荷物をお持ちいたします。</code></span><br>
<em>o-nimotsu o o-mochi itashimasu.</em><br>
I will carry your luggage.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お部屋はこちらでございます。</code></span><br>
<em>o-heya wa kochira de gozaimasu.</em><br>
Your room is this way.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お名前をお聞かせいただけますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>o-namae o o-kikase itadakemasu deshou ka.</em><br>
Could you tell me your name?
</div>

## Quiz

**Which sentence correctly applies the お prefix?**

- A. ご部屋はこちらでございます。
- B. お予約を確認いたしました。
- C. お名前をお聞かせいただけますでしょうか。
- D. お確認いたします。

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** お名前 is correct because 名前 is a native Japanese word (和語) and takes お. Option A incorrectly applies ご to 部屋, which is 和語 and takes お. Option B incorrectly applies お to 予約, which is a Chinese-origin word (漢語) and takes ご. Option D incorrectly applies お to 確認, which is also 漢語 and takes ご (ご確認).
