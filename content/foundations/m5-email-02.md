title: Understanding Guest Relationships
---

One of the most important concepts in Japanese hotel communication is relationship awareness.

Japanese business culture changes language depending on familiarity, social relationship, and communication history.

Because of this, hotel staff must recognize whether the guest is: a first-time visitor, a repeat guest, a VIP customer, or a long-term member.

<div class="keigo-card green">
<span class="keigo-card-title">First-Time Guest Tone</span>
For first-time guests, the hotel should sound: welcoming, appreciative, and polite.
</div>

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Repeat Guest Tone</span>
For repeat guests, the hotel should acknowledge: loyalty, continued support, and relationship continuity.
</div>

This distinction may seem small, but in Japanese hospitality culture, it strongly affects the naturalness of the email.

<div class="keigo-pair">
<div class="good-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">First-Time Guest</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>この度は、ご予約いただき、誠にありがとうございます。</code></span>
<br><br>
This expression acknowledges a new relationship beginning.
</div>
<div class="good-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Repeat Guest</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>いつもお世話になっております。</code></span>
<br><br>
This expression assumes an ongoing relationship already exists.
</div>
</div>

<div class="keigo-card red">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Watch Out</span>
Using the wrong expression can make the email sound automated or inattentive.
</div>

Good hotel communication makes guests feel: "This hotel understands who I am."

That emotional awareness is an important part of omotenashi hospitality.

## Quiz

**Which expression is appropriate for a repeat guest?**

- A. この度は、ご予約いただき、誠にありがとうございます
- B. いつもお世話になっております
- C. はじめまして
- D. ご無沙汰しております

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** いつもお世話になっております assumes an ongoing relationship and acknowledges continued support. A is for first-time guests, and C/D are inappropriate for hotel correspondence with a repeat guest.
