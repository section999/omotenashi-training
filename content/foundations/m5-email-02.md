title: Understanding Guest Relationships
---

Hotel communication shifts tone based on the guest relationship. Staff must assess familiarity and history before choosing expressions.

Guests fall into three categories:

**First-Time Guest Tone:** welcoming, appreciative, polite. The relationship is new.

**Repeat Guest Tone:** acknowledges loyalty, continued support, and relationship continuity. The relationship is established.

**VIP & Long-Term Member Tone:** elevated keigo and personalized references. The relationship carries history and status.

This distinction may seem small, but it determines how natural the email sounds.

Using the wrong expression makes the email sound automated or inattentive. Good hotel communication should make guests feel: "This hotel understands who I am." That awareness is omotenashi through language.

## Quiz

**Which expression is appropriate for a repeat guest?**

- A. ご無沙汰しております
- B. この度は、ご予約いただき、誠にありがとうございます
- C. いつもお世話になっております
- D. はじめまして

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** いつもお世話になっております assumes an ongoing relationship and acknowledges continued support. A (ご無沙汰しております) is used after a long silence, B is for first-time guests, and D (はじめまして) is for initial introductions.
