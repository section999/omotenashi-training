title: Facility Notice Template
---

Hotels sometimes need to send operational notices such as: maintenance schedules, temporary closures, renovation information, or facility restrictions.

These emails should sound professional, respectful, and considerate.

Even operational announcements are part of hospitality communication.

## Standard Template

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Opening</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>平素より当ホテルをご利用いただき、誠にありがとうございます。</code></span>
<br><br>
This creates a formal and professional tone suitable for notices.
</div>

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Body</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>下記の日程にて館内設備点検を実施いたします。点検作業中は、一部施設がご利用いただけない時間帯がございます。お客様にはご不便をおかけいたしますが、何卒ご理解のほどお願い申し上げます。</code></span>
<br><br>
This section explains the situation clearly, politely acknowledges inconvenience, and requests understanding.
<br><br>
The phrase <span class="keigo-jp"><code>何卒ご理解のほどお願い申し上げます</code></span> is commonly used in professional notice emails.
</div>

<div class="keigo-card green">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Closing</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご不明な点がございましたら、お気軽にご連絡くださいませ。</code></span>
<br><br>
This softens the notice and maintains hospitality tone.
</div>

## Professional Tip

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Guideline</span>
Notice emails should avoid sounding too technical or cold. Even operational messages should still feel guest-oriented.
</div>

## Quiz

**Which phrase is commonly used in Japanese facility notice emails to politely request understanding?**

- A. さようなら
- B. 何卒ご理解のほどお願い申し上げます
- C. また来てください
- D. ご確認ください

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** 「何卒ご理解のほどお願い申し上げます」 is a standard polite phrase used in professional notice emails to request the guest's understanding and cooperation.
