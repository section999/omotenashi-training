Hotels sometimes need to send operational notices such as:

- maintenance schedules,
- temporary closures,
- renovation information,
- or facility restrictions.

These emails should sound:

- professional,
- respectful,
- and considerate.

Even operational announcements are part of hospitality communication.

## Standard Template

**Opening**

平素より当ホテルをご利用いただき、誠にありがとうございます。

This creates a formal and professional tone suitable for notices.

**Body**

下記の日程にて館内設備点検を実施いたします。
点検作業中は、一部施設がご利用いただけない時間帯がございます。
お客様にはご不便をおかけいたしますが、何卒ご理解のほどお願い申し上げます。

This section:

- explains the situation clearly,
- politely acknowledges inconvenience,
- and requests understanding.

The phrase:

「何卒ご理解のほどお願い申し上げます」

is commonly used in professional notice emails.

**Closing**

ご不明な点がございましたら、お気軽にご連絡くださいませ。

This softens the notice and maintains hospitality tone.

## Professional Tip

Notice emails should avoid sounding too technical or cold.

Even operational messages should still feel guest-oriented.

## Quiz

**Which phrase is commonly used in Japanese facility notice emails to politely request understanding?**

- A. さようなら
- B. 何卒ご理解のほどお願い申し上げます
- C. また来てください
- D. ご確認ください

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** 「何卒ご理解のほどお願い申し上げます」 is a standard polite phrase used in professional notice emails to request the guest's understanding and cooperation.
