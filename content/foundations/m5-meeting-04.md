title: Guest and Reservation Terms
---

Guest-related vocabulary is the most frequently used category in any handover entry. Knowing these terms precisely allows you to write entries that identify who is involved and what stage their reservation is at.

## Guest Classification Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <span>VIPゲスト<br><em>bui ai pii gesuto</em></span> | VIP guest; requires special handling |
| <span>一般ゲスト<br><em>ippan gesuto</em></span> | regular guest |
| <span>リピーター<br><em>ripiitaa</em></span> | returning guest |
| <span>団体客<br><em>dantai kyaku</em></span> | group guest |
| <span>宿泊客<br><em>shukuhaku kyaku</em></span> | in-house guest (currently staying) |
| <span>チェックイン待ち<br><em>chekkuin machi</em></span> | guest waiting to check in |

VIP guest entries require the guest's name, room number, and any special arrangements confirmed. Do not use VIPゲスト without these details.

## Reservation and Stay Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <span>ご予約<br><em>go-yoyaku</em></span> | reservation |
| <span>チェックイン<br><em>chekkuin</em></span> | check-in |
| <span>チェックアウト<br><em>chekkuauto</em></span> | check-out |
| <span>延泊<br><em>enpaku</em></span> | extended stay |
| <span>早退<br><em>soutai</em></span> | early departure |
| <span>キャンセル<br><em>kyanseru</em></span> | cancellation |
| <span>ノーショー<br><em>nooshoo</em></span> | no-show |
| <span>到着予定<br><em>touchaku yotei</em></span> | scheduled arrival |

延泊 entries must specify how many additional nights (e.g., 1泊追加) and who is confirming availability.

## Using These Terms in Entries

Each guest or reservation term becomes precise only when combined with a room number and a status label.

## Quiz

**A guest has not arrived despite having a reservation. Which term correctly describes this situation?**

- A. 早退
- B. ノーショー
- C. 延泊
- D. チェックアウト

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** ノーショー (no-show) describes a guest who held a reservation but did not arrive or cancel. 早退 means early departure (the guest leaves before their planned check-out date). 延泊 means extending the stay. チェックアウト means the guest has checked out. When recording a no-show in the handover, also note whether the reservation was cancelled and whether the room was released.
