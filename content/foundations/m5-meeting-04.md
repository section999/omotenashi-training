title: Handover Writing Style Rules
---

Japanese hotel handover writing follows strict rules.

## Rule 1: Absolute Objectivity

**Wrong:**

ちょっとエアコンが変な感じです

This is subjective and unclear.

**Correct:**

空調不良あり

or

305号室：空調不良。修理手配済み。

## Rule 2: No Softening Words

Avoid:

- ちょっと
- たぶん
- 〜と思います
- なんか

These are not allowed in operational notes.

## Rule 3: Use "Status First" Structure

Always write:

[Room / Guest] + [Issue] + [Status]

**Example:**

210号室：騒音クレームあり。夜間対応済み。

## Rule 4: No Explanation Culture

**Bad:**

おそらく機械が少し古いので壊れたと思います

**Good:**

設備不良。修理依頼済み。

---

## Quiz

**Which word is NOT allowed in professional hotel handover writing?**

- A. 対応済み
- B. たぶん
- C. 修理手配済み
- D. 要対応

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** たぶん (maybe) is a softening word that creates ambiguity. Handover notes must be factual and unambiguous. Other options are all acceptable operational terms.
