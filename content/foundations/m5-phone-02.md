title: Standard Hotel Phone Call Structure
---

All hotel calls follow a fixed structure. This is not stylistic. It is a risk control system designed to prevent misunderstanding, repetition errors, and emotional escalation.

**Structure:** Greeting → Identification → Request recognition → Confirmation → Action → Result → Closing

**Each step has a function:**

- Greeting: stabilizes tone and sets professionalism
- Identification: clarifies responsibility (who is speaking)
- Request recognition: reduces guest anxiety by showing understanding
- Confirmation: prevents operational mistakes (names, dates, numbers)
- Action: shows transparency ("we are doing something now")
- Result: gives closure and certainty
- Closing: ends interaction cleanly and professionally

**Example:**

お電話ありがとうございます。
フロントでございます。
ご予約についてでございますね。
確認いたしますので少々お待ちくださいませ。
かしこまりました。
お電話ありがとうございました。

**Important operational rule:** Even if the request is simple, the structure must never be skipped. Skipping structure increases the probability of miscommunication, especially in reservations and complaints.

## Quiz

**What is the purpose of the "Confirmation" step in the hotel phone call structure?**

- A. To end the call
- B. To prevent operational mistakes with names, dates, and numbers
- C. To greet the guest
- D. To transfer the call

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The Confirmation step prevents operational mistakes by verifying key information such as names, dates, and numbers before proceeding.
