title: Standard Hotel Phone Call Structure
---

All hotel calls follow a fixed structure. This is not stylistic. It is a risk control system designed to prevent misunderstanding, repetition errors, and emotional escalation.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Call Structure</span>
Greeting → Identification → Request recognition → Confirmation → Action → Result → Closing
</div>

Each step has a function:

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Step Functions</span>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Greeting</span> stabilizes tone and sets professionalism<br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Identification</span> clarifies responsibility (who is speaking)<br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Recognition</span> reduces guest anxiety by showing understanding<br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Confirmation</span> prevents operational mistakes (names, dates, numbers)<br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Action</span> shows transparency ("we are doing something now")<br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Result</span> gives closure and certainty<br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Closing</span> ends interaction cleanly and professionally
</div>

<div class="keigo-card green">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Full Example</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お電話ありがとうございます。</code></span> (Greeting)<br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>フロントでございます。</code></span> (Identification)<br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご予約についてでございますね。</code></span> (Recognition)<br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>確認いたしますので少々お待ちくださいませ。</code></span> (Action)<br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>かしこまりました。</code></span> (Result)<br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お電話ありがとうございました。</code></span> (Closing)
</div>

<div class="keigo-card red">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Important Rule</span>
Even if the request is simple, the structure must never be skipped. Skipping structure increases the probability of miscommunication, especially in reservations and complaints.
</div>

## Quiz

**What is the purpose of the "Confirmation" step in the hotel phone call structure?**

- A. To end the call
- B. To prevent operational mistakes with names, dates, and numbers
- C. To greet the guest
- D. To transfer the call

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The Confirmation step prevents operational mistakes by verifying key information such as names, dates, and numbers before proceeding.
