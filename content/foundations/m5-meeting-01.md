title: What Hotel Handover Is and Why It Matters
---

A hotel operates 24 hours a day with rotating shifts. No single staff member sees a guest from arrival to departure. The only thing that connects those experiences is the handover. When handover is done correctly, the guest never feels the shift change. When it is done poorly, they do.

## The Definition of 引き継ぎ

引き継ぎ (hikitsugi) translates as "handover" or "shift handover." In hotel operations, it refers to the full process of transferring all relevant guest and operational information from one shift to the next.

The written record is called 申し送り (moushiokuri), sometimes translated as "shift notes" or "handover report." 申し送り is not just a log. It is a service-continuity document.

The next shift must be able to serve every guest as if they themselves had been present since check-in. Not approximately. Not mostly. Completely.

## What Breaks Without Good Handover

Service failures caused by handover gaps are among the most damaging to guest satisfaction. They are also among the most preventable.

<div class="keigo-card blue">

| Failure | Consequence |
|---|---|
| VIP not flagged | Special service not prepared |
| Complaint not recorded | Next staff unaware; guest feels ignored |
| Room issue not noted | Guest calls again; problem still not resolved |
| Pending request missing | Request forgotten entirely |
| Medical need not transferred | Potential safety risk |

<br>
The worst outcome is a guest saying: "I already told someone this yesterday." That phrase signals a complete handover failure and is very difficult to recover from.
</div>

## Why Handover Is Treated as a Professional Standard

In Japanese hotel culture, handover is not informational. It is a responsibility. Writing it carelessly, skipping sections, or leaving items unresolved before a shift ends reflects poorly on the staff member's professional judgment.

Your shift ends when the next shift has everything they need. Not when the clock changes.

## The Three Questions Every Handover Answers

No matter how complex the shift was, the handover must answer three questions for the incoming team:

**1. Who needs attention right now?**

VIP guests, guests with active complaints, guests with special needs.


**2. What is unresolved?**

Maintenance issues, pending requests, items waiting for confirmation.


**3. What else must I know?**

Room changes, late check-outs, behavioral concerns, medical notes.

## Quiz

**What is the primary purpose of 引き継ぎ in a hotel?**

- A. To fill in paperwork required by management
- B. To allow the outgoing staff to leave on time
- C. To ensure the next shift can serve every guest as if no shift change occurred
- D. To record incidents for legal documentation

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The purpose of 引き継ぎ is service continuity. The incoming shift must be able to handle every guest situation without gaps, delays, or asking guests to repeat themselves. While documentation and compliance have their place, the operational goal is always seamless guest service across all shift boundaries.
