title: Room Availability Calls
---

Availability calls are not simple information delivery. They are decision-support interactions. Guests are actively deciding whether to book, so clarity and tone directly influence conversion.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Flow</span>
Request → Confirmation → System check → Result → Alternative offer
</div>

<div class="keigo-card green">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Example Call</span>
<strong>Guest:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>今日空いていますか</code></span><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>本日の空室状況を確認いたしますので少々お待ちくださいませ</code></span><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>あいにく本日は満室となっております</code></span>
</div>

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Why あいにく Matters</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>あいにく</code></span> softens rejection while maintaining clarity and professionalism.
</div>

Continuation:

<div class="keigo-card green">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Alternative Offer</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>もしよろしければ別日程でしたらご案内可能でございます</code></span>
</div>

<div class="keigo-card red">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Key Rule</span>
Never end a call with "no availability." Always extend service options.
</div>

## Quiz

**What is the key rule for availability calls when there are no rooms available?**

- A. End the call immediately
- B. Never end with "no availability"; always extend service options
- C. Apologize repeatedly
- D. Transfer to another hotel

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Even when there is no availability, always offer alternatives such as different dates. This maintains guest interest and preserves the hotel's service image.
