title: The Root Cause of All Keigo Errors
---

The five common keigo errors covered in previous lessons (plain verbs for guest actions, sonkeigo on staff, double keigo, register mixing, and わかりました instead of かしこまりました) all share the same root cause.

## The Pattern Behind Every Error

Every keigo error is a reversion to the language that feels instinctive, rather than an application of the trained hotel standard.

When learning keigo, the new forms feel effortful. The plain or everyday forms are automatic. Under any kind of pressure, the brain defaults to what requires less effort. This is not a character flaw; it is how language acquisition works. The solution is not discipline or willpower. It is repetition until the hotel forms become the instinctive ones.

## When Errors Happen Most

Errors are most frequent in predictable conditions:

- Under fatigue or sustained stress
- Handling an unexpected situation for the first time
- Switching rapidly between multiple guest interactions
- Turning from a colleague conversation directly to a guest

Recognizing these conditions makes it possible to increase alertness before an error occurs, rather than correcting it afterward.

## The Standard Is Not Perfection

Professional keigo is not about never making an error. It is about having trained the hotel forms deeply enough that they are the default under pressure, not the exception when things are calm. A staff member who only produces correct keigo when relaxed has not fully internalized it. A staff member whose keigo holds under stress has.

## Quiz

**A staff member's keigo is correct in calm situations but breaks down during busy periods. What does this indicate?**

- A. The staff member has an advanced keigo skill set; errors during busy periods are acceptable
- B. The staff member needs to study more vocabulary
- C. The hotel forms have not yet replaced the casual forms as the default; more repetition is needed until hotel keigo becomes instinctive under pressure
- D. This is normal for all hotel staff and cannot be improved

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Keigo that holds only when conditions are easy has not been fully internalized. The goal is for hotel forms to become the instinctive defaults, not a layer applied on top of casual language when attention is available. Errors under pressure reveal that the casual forms are still the brain's first choice. The solution is sustained repetition: not studying more vocabulary, but drilling the hotel forms until they are automatic. Options A and D accept a lower standard; option B misidentifies the cause.
