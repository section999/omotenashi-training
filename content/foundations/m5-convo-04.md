title: In-Room and Concierge Service Requests
---

After check-in, a significant portion of guest-staff interaction happens through in-room telephone calls and concierge visits. These requests range from simple housekeeping needs to complex arrangements like restaurant reservations, sightseeing tours, and transportation. Handling them well requires both the right language and the right mindset: **every request, no matter how small, is an opportunity to deepen the guest's trust in the hotel**.

## Housekeeping Requests

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<strong>Guest:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>タオルを追加でお願いしたいのですが。</code></span><br>
<em>taoru o tsuika de onegai shitai no desu ga.</em><br>
I'd like some extra towels, please.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>かしこまりました。ただいまお持ちいたします。少々お待ちくださいませ。</code></span><br>
<em>kashikomarimashita. tadaima o-mochi itashimasu. shoushou omachi kudasaimase.</em><br>
Certainly. I will bring them to you right away. Please wait a moment.<br><br>
<em>[When delivering the towels]</em><br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>失礼いたします。タオルをお持ちしました。</code></span><br>
<em>shitsurei itashimasu. taoru o o-mochi shimashita.</em><br>
Excuse me. I have brought the towels.<br><br>
<strong>Guest:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ありがとうございます。</code></span><br>
<em>arigatou gozaimasu.</em><br>
Thank you.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ほかに何かご入用のものはございますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>hoka ni nani ka go iriyou no mono wa gozaimasu deshou ka.</em><br>
Is there anything else you need?
</div>

<em>uenokouen e no iki kata o oshie te morae masu ka.</em><br>

<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>かしこまりました。当ホテルから上野公園ですと、電車でお越しの場合は最寄りの○○駅から山手線にお乗りいただき、上野駅でお降りいただくのが便利でございます。徒歩で約5分でございます。</code></span><br>
<em>kashikomarimashita. tou hoteru kara uenokouen desu to, densha de okoshi no baai wa moyori no ○○ eki kara yamate sen ni o nori itadaki, ueno eki de o ori itadaku no ga benri de gozaimasu. toho de yaku 5 fun de gozaimasu.</em><br>

## Handling Requests You Cannot Immediately Answer

Not every concierge question can be answered instantly. The professional response is to acknowledge, take time to find the answer, and follow up, never to guess or provide uncertain information:

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<strong>Guest:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>近くにおすすめの和食レストランはありますか。</code></span><br>
<em>chikaku ni osusume no washoku resutoran wa arimasu ka.</em><br>
Are there any good Japanese restaurants nearby?<br><br>
<strong>Staff (uncertain):</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>かしこまりました。少々お待ちいただけますでしょうか。確認してまいります。</code></span><br>
<em>kashikomarimashita. shoushou omachi itadake masu deshou ka. kakunin shite mairimasu.</em><br>
Certainly. Could you give me a moment? I will check for you.<br><br>
<em>[After checking]</em><br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>お待たせいたしました。徒歩5分ほどのところに○○という和食のレストランがございます。評判もよく、本日もご予約が可能でございます。ご予約をお取りすることもできますが、いかがでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>o-matase itashimashita. toho 5 fun hodo no tokoro ni ○○ toyuu washoku no resutoran ga gozaimasu. hyouban mo yoku, honjitsu mo go-yoyaku ga kanou de gozaimasu. go-yoyaku o o-tori suru koto mo dekimasu ga, ikaga deshou ka.</em><br>
Thank you for waiting. There is a Japanese restaurant called ○○ about 5 minutes on foot. It has a good reputation and reservations are available today. I could make a reservation for you, would you like that?
</div>

**The key phrase 確認してまいります (I will check and come back) is one of the most powerful professional expressions in hotel service.** It communicates: "I take your question seriously enough to verify before answering, rather than guessing."

## Proactive Service, Anticipating Needs

Excellent concierge service does not just answer questions, it anticipates the next need:

<div class="keigo-card blue">
After providing directions:<br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>地図もご用意できますが、いかがでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>chizu mo go-youi dekimasu ga, ikaga deshou ka.</em><br>
Shall I prepare a map for you?<br><br>
After suggesting a restaurant:<br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご予約をお取りすることもできますが、いかがでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>go-yoyaku o o-tori suru koto mo dekimasu ga, ikaga deshou ka.</em><br>
Shall I make a reservation for you?<br><br>
After arranging transport:<br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>タクシーのご手配もできますが、いかがでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>takushi no go tehai mo dekimasu ga, ikaga deshou ka.</em><br>
Shall I arrange a taxi for you?
</div>

These proactive offers embody the omotenashi principle: anticipating needs before they are expressed.

## Quiz

**A guest asks the concierge for a restaurant recommendation, but the staff is unsure which restaurants are currently open. What is the correct response?**

- A. Give the first restaurant that comes to mind without checking
- B. Say 「申し訳ありません、分かりません」and end the conversation
- C. Say 「少々お待ちいただけますでしょうか。確認してまいります」and return with verified information
- D. Suggest the guest search online themselves

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** When uncertain, the professional response is to acknowledge the request, pause to verify the information, and return with accurate guidance. Guessing risks giving the guest incorrect information. Saying "I don't know" without follow-up abandons the request. The phrase 確認してまいります signals that the staff takes the question seriously enough to verify, which is itself an act of service.
