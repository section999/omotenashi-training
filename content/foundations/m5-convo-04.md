## Mistake 1: お名前は？

**Why It Sounds Unprofessional**

Although grammatically understandable, it feels:

- abrupt
- overly direct
- lacking service tone

In hospitality settings, direct questioning can feel cold.

**Better Version**

✅ ご予約のお名前をお聞かせいただけますでしょうか？

This sounds:

- guided
- respectful
- welcoming

## Mistake 2: 部屋は702です

**Why It Feels Too Plain**

This expression sounds functional rather than hospitable.

Hotels generally avoid plain です forms during formal guest interactions.

**Better Version**

✅ 702号室でございます

The use of でございます:

- softens delivery
- raises formality
- maintains elegant service tone

## Mistake 3: 朝食は1階です

**Problem**

The sentence communicates information correctly, but:

- lacks refinement
- sounds mechanical
- does not feel service-oriented

**Better Version**

✅ 1階レストランで承っております

This expression sounds:

- more professional
- more polished
- more guest-centered

## Summary of Mistake Patterns

| Common Mistake | Problem | Hospitality Version |
|----------------|---------|---------------------|
| お名前は？ | Too direct, abrupt | お聞かせいただけますでしょうか |
| 部屋は702です | Plain, functional | 702号室でございます |
| 朝食は1階です | Mechanical, unrefined | 1階レストランで承っております |

The key principle is that information should be delivered in a way that feels guided and respectful, not transactional.

## Quiz

**Why should hotel staff avoid saying 部屋は702です to a guest?**

- A. The room number is incorrect
- B. It sounds functional rather than hospitable, and hotels avoid plain です forms in formal interactions
- C. Guests cannot understand です
- D. It is grammatically incorrect

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** 部屋は702です sounds functional and lacks hospitality tone. Hotels use でございます instead of です to soften delivery, raise formality, and maintain an elegant service tone during guest interactions.
