title: ございます and Sentence Endings
---

ございます is the refined existence form that replaces あります in hotel speech. Paired with the appropriate sentence endings, it completes the professional tone that 丁寧語 establishes throughout every interaction.

## ございます vs あります

Both mean "there is / we have," but ございます is the hotel-level form. Using あります in a guest-facing sentence drops the register below the hotel standard.

<div class="keigo-pair">
<div class="bad-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Everyday polite</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>プールがあります。</code></span><br>
<em>puuru ga arimasu.</em><br>
There is a pool.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご不明な点はありますか？</code></span><br>
<em>go-fumei na ten wa arimasu ka?</em><br>
Do you have any unclear points?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge bad">Too plain</span> あります is functional but drops the hotel register.
</div>
<div class="good-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Hotel 丁寧語</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>プールがございます。</code></span><br>
<em>puuru ga gozaimasu.</em><br>
There is a pool.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご不明な点はございますか？</code></span><br>
<em>go-fumei na ten wa gozaimasu ka?</em><br>
Do you have any unclear points?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge good">Correct</span> ございます maintains hotel register throughout.
</div>

## しております vs しています

しております is the 丁寧語 form of しています. It conveys that the staff is in the middle of an action, with an added feeling of service attention.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ただいまご確認しております。</code></span><br>
<em>tadaima go-kakunin shite orimasu.</em><br>
I am confirming that right now.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>担当者が対応しております。</code></span><br>
<em>tantou-sha ga taiou shite orimasu.</em><br>
A staff member is handling that.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ただいまお調べしております。</code></span><br>
<em>tadaima o-shirabe shite orimasu.</em><br>
I am looking into that now.
</div>

## Hotel Sentence Endings at a Glance

| Ending | Use |
|---|---|
| <span>でございます</span> | <span>Stating facts; refined form of です</span> |
| <span>ございます</span> | <span>Refined existence; replaces あります</span> |
| <span>いたします</span> | <span>Staff action; humble form of します</span> |
| <span>くださいませ</span> | Polished instruction to the guest |
| <span>でしょうか</span> | Softened question or confirmation |

## Quiz

**A guest asks if the hotel has a gym. Which response is correct?**

- A. はい、ジムがあります。
- B. はい、ジムがございます。
- C. はい、ジムでございます。
- D. はい、ジムがいらっしゃいます。

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** ございます is the hotel-standard existence form that replaces あります in guest-facing statements. Option A uses あります, which is correct everyday Japanese but drops below the hotel register. Option C uses でございます, which is the copula form (for "A is B"); it does not work for expressing existence ("there is"). Option D applies いらっしゃいます to a facility, which is only used for people, not places or objects.
