title: 謙譲語: The Core Rule
---

謙譲語 (kenjogo) is the humble speech system used to lower the staff's position. While 尊敬語 raises the guest, 謙譲語 lowers the staff. Together they create the defining language structure of Japanese hospitality: the guest is always linguistically above the staff.

## The Core Rule

Use 謙譲語 whenever **staff** are the subject and the action is directed toward or performed for the guest. The humble verb form communicates service commitment without explicitly saying so.

<div class="bad-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">No 謙譲語</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>確認します。</code></span><br>
<em>kakunin shimasu.</em><br>
I'll confirm.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge bad">Incomplete</span> Sounds like a colleague talking to another colleague.
</div>
<div class="good-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">謙譲語 applied</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>確認いたします。</code></span><br>
<em>kakunin itashimasu.</em><br>
I will confirm.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge good">Correct</span> いたします is the humble form of する; it places the staff in a service role.
</div>

Saying 確認します and 確認いたします both mean "I will confirm." The second carries an additional meaning: "I will confirm, and I do so in service of you." The humble form delivers that automatically. Guests perceive it, even if they cannot explain why the second sounds better.

## The Direction of the Hierarchy

| Subject | Form to Use | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Staff acting for guest | <span>謙譲語</span> | Lowers the staff; elevates the service |
| Guest acting | <span>尊敬語</span> | Elevates the guest's action |
| General statement | <span>丁寧語</span> | Maintains professional register |

謙譲語 is not about self-deprecation. It is about expressing that the action exists to serve. Every humble form carries the implicit meaning: this action is for you.

## Quiz

**Why does 確認いたします sound more professional than 確認します in hotel service?**

- A. いたします adds an extra syllable that makes the sentence sound longer and more serious
- B. いたします is the humble form of する; it communicates that the action is performed in service of the guest, not just as a neutral activity
- C. 確認いたします is 尊敬語 that elevates the topic of confirmation
- D. する verbs are always incorrect in hotel service

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** いたします is the 謙譲語 form of する. Using it instead of します signals that the staff is performing the action humbly in service of the guest. This is the core function of kenjogo: not just to sound polite, but to structurally position the staff below the guest. Option A mistakes formality for length. Option C incorrectly identifies it as sonkeigo. Option D is wrong; する verbs are used constantly but require the humble form いたします.
