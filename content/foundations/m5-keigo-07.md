title: Building Complete Keigo Sentences
---

Knowing individual keigo verbs and prefixes is not enough. Professional hotel keigo requires knowing how to construct complete, natural-sounding sentences. This lesson focuses on how all three systems combine within a single sentence and across a full hotel exchange.

## The Sentence Structure Framework

Before forming any hotel sentence, ask three questions in sequence. The answers determine every keigo choice.

**1. Who is the subject?**

| Subject | Keigo Type | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Guest is acting | <span>尊敬語</span> | Use an honorific verb form |
| Staff is acting | <span>謙譲語</span> | Use a humble verb form |
| No clear actor | <span>丁寧語</span> | Use a refined baseline form |

**2. Are any nouns or verb stems present?**

| Word origin | Prefix | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Japanese-origin (<span>和語</span>) | <span>お</span> | <span>部屋</span> → <span>お部屋</span> |
| Chinese-origin (<span>漢語</span>) | <span>ご</span> | <span>予約</span> → <span>ご予約</span> |
| Loanword (<span>外来語</span>) | none | チェックイン → チェックイン |

**3. What is the sentence ending?**

Choose the ending that fits the sentence type:

| Ending | Use |
|---|---|
| <span>でございます</span> | Stating facts; refined form of です |
| <span>ございます</span> | Refined existence; replaces あります |
| <span>いたします</span> | Staff action; humble form of します |
| <span>くださいませ</span> | Polished instruction to the guest |
| <span>でしょうか</span> | Softened question or confirmation |

## Pattern 1: Guest Is Present / Identity Confirmation

Used at arrival and any time a guest's identity or presence is being noted.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>[Name]様でいらっしゃいますか。</code></span><br>
<em>[Name]-sama de irasshaimasu ka.</em><br>
Are you Mr./Ms. [Name]?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>[Name]様でいらっしゃいますね。</code></span><br>
<em>[Name]-sama de irasshaimasu ne.</em><br>
You are Mr./Ms. [Name], correct?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご予約の[Name]様でいらっしゃいますか。</code></span><br>
<em>go-yoyaku no [Name]-sama de irasshaimasu ka.</em><br>
Are you Mr./Ms. [Name] with a reservation?
</div>

Components: ご (prefix for 予約) + でいらっしゃいます (sonkeigo for the guest's existence).

## Pattern 2: Information Delivery

Used when telling the guest facts about the hotel, their room, or a service.

Structure: <span class="keigo-jp"><code>[Subject]は[information]でございます。</code></span>

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お部屋は702号室でございます。</code></span><br>
<em>o-heya wa 702-goushitsu de gozaimasu.</em><br>
Your room is Room 702.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>朝食は1階のレストランでございます。</code></span><br>
<em>choushoku wa ikkai no resutoran de gozaimasu.</em><br>
Breakfast is at the restaurant on the 1st floor.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>チェックアウトは11時でございます。</code></span><br>
<em>chekku-auto wa juuichi-ji de gozaimasu.</em><br>
Checkout is at 11:00.
</div>

Components: お (prefix for 部屋) + でございます (teineigo refined statement).

## Pattern 3: Asking the Guest to Act

Used when requesting a guest to do something. Always use the softened form.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>○○いただけますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>○○ itadakemasu deshou ka.</em><br>
Could you please ○○?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>こちらにご記入いただけますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>kochira ni go-kinyu itadakemasu deshou ka.</em><br>
Could you please fill this in?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>パスポートをご提示いただけますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>pasupouto o go-teiji itadakemasu deshou ka.</em><br>
Could you please show your passport?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>少々お待ちいただけますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>shoushou o-machi itadakemasu deshou ka.</em><br>
Could you please wait a moment?
</div>

Components: ご/お (prefix) + いただける (humble receiving) + でしょうか (teineigo question).

## Pattern 4: Staff Announcing Their Action

Used when the staff member states what they are about to do or are doing.

Structure: <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ただいま○○いたします。</code></span> (I will ○○ right now.)

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ただいまご確認いたします。</code></span><br>
<em>tadaima go-kakunin itashimasu.</em><br>
I will confirm that right now.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お部屋までご案内いたします。</code></span><br>
<em>o-heya made go-annai itashimasu.</em><br>
I will guide you to your room.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご予約を承りました。</code></span><br>
<em>go-yoyaku o uketamawarimashita.</em><br>
I have received your reservation.
</div>

Components: ご (prefix) + いたします / 承りました (kenjogo verb).

## Pattern 5: Apologizing and Softening Limitations

Used when the hotel cannot fulfill a request fully. Structure: apology buffer + situation + alternative.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>誠に恐れ入りますが、○○でございます。</code></span><br>
<em>makotoni osoreirimasu ga, ○○ de gozaimasu.</em><br>
I am truly sorry, but ○○.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>誠に恐れ入りますが、ただいま満室でございます。</code></span><br>
<em>makotoni osoreirimasu ga, tadaima manshitsu de gozaimasu.</em><br>
I am truly sorry, but we are fully booked at this time.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>あいにく○○が難しい状況でございます。</code></span><br>
<em>ainiku ○○ ga muzukashii joukyou de gozaimasu.</em><br>
Unfortunately, ○○ is difficult at this time.
</div>

Components: 誠に恐れ入りますが (kenjogo apology buffer) + でございます (teineigo statement).

## A Full Check-In Exchange: Annotated

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>いらっしゃいませ。</code></span> <span class="keigo-badge info">尊敬語</span><br>
<em>irasshaimase.</em><br>
Welcome.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご予約のお名前をお聞かせいただけますでしょうか。</code></span> <span class="keigo-badge info">謙譲語 + 丁寧語</span><br>
<em>go-yoyaku no o-namae o o-kikase itadakemasu deshou ka.</em><br>
Could you tell me the name on your reservation?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>田中様でいらっしゃいますね。2泊でございますね。</code></span> <span class="keigo-badge info">尊敬語 + 丁寧語</span><br>
<em>tanaka-sama de irasshaimasu ne. nihaku de gozaimasu ne.</em><br>
You are Ms./Mr. Tanaka, correct? A 2-night stay, correct?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ただいまお部屋をご用意いたします。</code></span> <span class="keigo-badge info">謙譲語</span><br>
<em>tadaima o-heya o go-youi itashimasu.</em><br>
I will prepare your room right now.
</div>

In this exchange: いらっしゃる is sonkeigo for the guest's existence/arrival; お聞かせいただく is humble receiving with correct ご/お prefixes; いらっしゃいます is sonkeigo and でございます is teineigo; ご用意いたします is kenjogo for the staff's action.

## Quiz

**A staff member wants to say "I will guide you to your room." Which is correct?**

- A. 部屋に行きます
- B. 部屋を見せます
- C. お部屋までご案内いたします
- D. お部屋にいらっしゃいます

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** お部屋までご案内いたします uses three keigo elements correctly: お (prefix for 部屋, a native Japanese word), ご案内 (ご prefix for the 漢語 verb stem), and いたします (kenjogo for the staff's action). Option A uses plain language with no keigo. Option B uses 見せます, which means "to show" rather than "to guide" and has no keigo. Option D uses いらっしゃいます, which is sonkeigo appropriate only for the guest, not for the staff.
