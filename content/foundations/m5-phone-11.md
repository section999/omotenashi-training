title: Wake-up Call and Taxi Requests
---

These are simple requests but require high accuracy because they affect guest schedule directly.

**Wake-up call:**

モーニングコールお願いします
→ お時間をお伺いしてもよろしいでしょうか
→ 6時です
→ 6時のモーニングコールで承りました

**Taxi:**

タクシーお願いします
→ かしこまりました。ご希望のお時間はございますでしょうか
→ すぐでお願いします
→ タクシーを手配いたします

**Key rule:** Time and urgency must always be repeated for operational safety.

---

## Quiz

**What is the key rule for wake-up call and taxi request handling?**

- A. Use casual language
- B. Time and urgency must always be repeated for operational safety
- C. Transfer to another department
- D. Ask the guest to call back

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Time-based requests like wake-up calls and taxi orders directly affect the guest's schedule. Every time and urgency detail must be repeated clearly to prevent operational errors.
