title: Closing and Receiving the Verbal Briefing
---

The close of a verbal briefing and the incoming staff's response are as important as the opening. A briefing that ends without confirmation is not complete.

## Closing the Verbal Briefing

The outgoing staff closes with a summary, an invitation for questions, and a statement that refers the incoming staff to the written record.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>以上が本日の引き継ぎ事項です。何かご不明な点はございますか。</code></span><br>
<em>ijou ga honjitsu no hikitsugi jikou desu. nani ka go-fumei na ten wa gozaimasu ka.</em><br>
That concludes today's handover. Do you have any questions?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>詳細は申し送りをご確認くださいませ。何かあればすぐにご連絡ください。</code></span><br>
<em>shousai wa moushiokuri o go-kakunin kudasaimase. nani ka areba sugu ni go-renraku kudasai.</em><br>
Please check the written notes for details. Contact me immediately if anything comes up.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>本日もよろしくお願いいたします。</code></span><br>
<em>honjitsu mo yoroshiku onegai itashimasu.</em><br>
I am counting on you for today's shift.
</div>

## Receiving the Briefing

The incoming staff also has responsibilities during a verbal briefing. Passive listening is not enough; any unclear item must be confirmed before the outgoing staff leaves.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>かしこまりました。</code></span><br>
<em>kashikomarimashita.</em><br>
Understood.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>○○は、○○ということでよろしいでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>○○ wa, ○○ to yuu koto de yoroshii deshou ka.</em><br>
Regarding ○○, I understand it means ○○. Is that correct?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>501号室の延泊確認は、本日15時までに必要でしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>501 goushitsu no nobe haku kakunin wa, honjitsu 15-ji made ni hitsuyou deshou ka.</em><br>
Does the extension confirmation for Room 501 need to be done by 15:00 today?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>承りました。</code></span><br>
<em>uketamawarimashita.</em><br>
I have received the briefing.
</div>

## What the Incoming Staff Is Responsible For

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<strong>Confirm unclear items:</strong> Ask during the briefing, not after the outgoing staff has left.<br><br>
<strong>Verify deadlines:</strong> Every pending task should have a time attached before the briefing ends.<br><br>
<strong>Identify owners:</strong> If a task has no named owner, ask who is responsible before confirming receipt.
</div>

The incoming staff should not use わかりました as the primary acknowledgment during a professional briefing. かしこまりました and 承りました signal that the responsibility has been formally received.

---

## Quiz

**During a verbal briefing, the outgoing staff says: 「501号室の延泊はフロント確認待ちです」. What should the incoming staff say next?**

- A. わかりました
- B. それは誰かが対応する予定ですか
- C. 承りました。確認はいつまでに必要でしょうか。
- D. Nothing; the incoming staff only needs to listen

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** When receiving a verbal briefing, the incoming staff should confirm unclear details immediately. 承りました (I have received this) is the appropriate acknowledgment at hotel standard, and asking いつまでに必要でしょうか (when does it need to be done by?) ensures the task has a clear deadline before the briefing ends. Option A (わかりました) is below hotel keigo standard for formal task receipt. Option B suggests the incoming staff does not understand their own responsibility. Passive listening without confirming key details is not professional.
