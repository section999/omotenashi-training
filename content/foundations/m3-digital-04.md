title: Messaging Apps and Digital Guest Communication
---

Many guests now prefer to communicate with properties through messaging platforms — LINE, WhatsApp, WeChat, or direct booking-platform messaging — rather than phone or email. This shift brings opportunities for more natural, responsive communication, but also risks if the professional standard of hospitality is not maintained in a digital format.

**The register challenge:**

Messaging apps carry an inherent informality — they are the tools people use with friends and family. The hospitality professional must resist the pressure to match that informality. A message from a guest on WhatsApp deserves the same care and professionalism as an email, even if the medium is casual.

Avoid:
- Abbreviations (*"Sry for wait"*, *"thx for ur msg"*)
- Emoji in formal contexts (a brief, appropriate emoji can warm a personal conversation, but use judgment)
- One-word replies (*"OK"*, *"Sure"*) without context or warmth

**Response speed:**

Messaging platforms create an expectation of faster replies than email — often within minutes during business hours. This expectation should be managed: if your property uses messaging as a service channel, staff the channel adequately, or set a clear auto-response indicating when replies can be expected.

**Accuracy and confirmation:**

Because messaging is informal and fast, errors slip through more easily. Before sending any message containing a booking detail, a price, a room type, or a time, re-read it. A message confirming the wrong arrival time or check-out date creates problems that are difficult to walk back.

**Formal matters belong in email:**

Complaints, refunds, contract terms, and any communication that may need to be referenced later should be moved to email. A message thread on WhatsApp is not an appropriate medium for resolving significant service failures or agreeing to financial matters.

## Quiz

**A guest messages the property on WhatsApp asking to confirm their check-in time. The correct reply is:**

- A. "3pm 👍"
- B. "Hi! Yes 3pm is fine, see u then!"
- C. "Thank you for your message. Your check-in time is 3:00 p.m. We look forward to welcoming you. Please let us know if there is anything we can arrange in advance."
- D. "Check-in is at 3PM. Please bring your reservation confirmation."

**Correct Answer:** "Thank you for your message. Your check-in time is 3:00 p.m. We look forward to welcoming you. Please let us know if there is anything we can arrange in advance."

**Explanation:** Even on an informal messaging platform, the professional standard of hospitality applies. The response is warm, clear, complete, and proactive — offering to assist further rather than simply closing the exchange.
