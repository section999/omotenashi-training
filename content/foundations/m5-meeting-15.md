This lesson uses the same handover note from Lesson 14 to practice reading: completing partial entries and making operational decisions.

## Reference Note

```
最優先情報：
305号室：VIPゲスト（鈴木様）。ウェルカムフルーツ対応済み。

対応中案件：
210号室：騒音クレーム。夜間スタッフ対応済み。経過確認要。

客室状況：
412号室：清掃未完了。15:00頃対応予定。

未完了タスク：
延泊希望あり（501号室）。フロント確認待ち。

特記事項：
604号室：ナッツアレルギーあり。レストランに申し送り済み。
```

## Fill in the Blank

Complete each partial entry with the correct status term.

**a.** `412号室：清掃未完了。15:00頃 ___。`
→ 対応予定

**b.** `501号室：延泊希望あり。フロント ___。`
→ 確認待ち

**c.** `210号室：騒音クレーム対応済み。経過 ___。`
→ 確認要

Any status term that accurately reflects the actual state of the task is acceptable. The goal is to confirm that the label you choose matches the situation precisely.

## Making a Decision from the Note

<div class="keigo-card yellow">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Scenario</span>
You are the incoming night staff. It is now 22:00.<br><br>
<em>Which task requires your attention first, and why?</em>
</div>

Room 412 was marked for cleaning at 15:00. It is now 22:00 and no update has been recorded. This entry is overdue. Before any other task, confirm whether the room was actually cleaned. If it was not, resolve it or escalate immediately.

The status terms record the plan from the previous shift. Your responsibility is to verify the plan was carried out, and act on anything that was not.

## Reading Checklist

1. Identify every entry with a pending or blocked status (確認待ち, 要対応, 未対応).
2. Check any timed entry that has passed without an update.
3. Confirm VIP entries have all required preparation.
4. Note any entry with 経過確認要; these require a follow-up check, even if the original issue was handled.

## Quiz

**You receive the handover note in this lesson. It is 22:00. Room 412 was marked for cleaning at 15:00, but no update has been recorded. What should you do?**

- A. Ignore it because the previous shift already noted it
- B. Mark it as 対応済み since the scheduled time has passed
- C. Check whether the room was cleaned and act on the result
- D. Wait until morning and ask the day shift

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** A status entry without a follow-up update is not confirmation that a task was completed. 15:00頃対応予定 records a plan, not a result. The incoming staff is responsible for verifying overdue entries and acting on any that were not resolved. Ignoring the entry or deferring it to the next shift risks a guest arriving at an unprepared room. Marking it as 対応済み without verification would record false information in the handover record.
