title: Complaint Handling
---

Complaint calls are the highest emotional risk scenarios. The primary goal is not solving immediately, but stabilizing emotion first.

**Flow:** Apology → Acknowledgment → Action → Assurance

**Example:**

隣がうるさいです
→ ご不便をおかけし申し訳ございません
→ すぐに確認いたします
→ スタッフがお部屋へお伺いいたします

**Why apology comes first:** In Japanese hospitality logic, emotional validation precedes problem-solving.

**Bad response:**

それはですね… feels defensive and increases tension.

## Quiz

**What is the first step in handling a complaint call?**

- A. Explain the cause of the problem
- B. Apologize and acknowledge the inconvenience
- C. Transfer to a manager
- D. Ask for more details

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** In Japanese hospitality logic, emotional validation precedes problem-solving. The first step is always to apologize and acknowledge the inconvenience to stabilize the guest's emotions.
