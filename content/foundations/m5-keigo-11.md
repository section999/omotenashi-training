This lesson applies 謙譲語 to real hotel scenarios and addresses the critical error of applying humble forms to the guest; this is the reverse of correct keigo structure.

## Applied 謙譲語: Hotel Scenarios

<div class="keigo-pair">
<div class="bad-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Casual (wrong)</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>部屋まで行きます。</code></span><br>
<em>heya made ikimasu.</em><br>
I'll go to the room.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge bad">Wrong</span> 行く has no humble quality in plain form.
</div>
<div class="good-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">謙譲語 (correct)</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お部屋までご案内いたします。</code></span><br>
<em>o-heya made go-annai itashimasu.</em><br>
I will guide you to your room.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge good">Correct</span> ご案内いたします layers the service role with humility.
</div>
</div>

<div class="keigo-pair">
<div class="bad-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Casual (wrong)</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>荷物を運びます。</code></span><br>
<em>nimotsu o hakobimasu.</em><br>
I'll carry the luggage.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge bad">Wrong</span> No honorific prefix; no humble verb form.
</div>
<div class="good-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">謙譲語 (correct)</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お荷物をお持ちいたします。</code></span><br>
<em>o-nimotsu o o-mochi itashimasu.</em><br>
I will carry your luggage.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge good">Correct</span> お荷物 (honorific prefix) + お持ちいたします (humble form of 持つ).
</div>
</div>

## Critical Error: 謙譲語 Applied to the Guest

Just as 尊敬語 must never be applied to staff, 謙譲語 must never be applied to guests. This error reverses the hospitality relationship and linguistically demotes the guest.

<div class="keigo-pair">
<div class="bad-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Wrong</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お客様がおります。</code></span><br>
<em>okyakusama ga orimasu.</em><br>
The guest is here.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge bad">Wrong</span> おります is 謙譲語 for いる. Applying it to the guest lowers them.
</div>
<div class="good-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Correct</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お客様がいらっしゃいます。</code></span><br>
<em>okyakusama ga irasshaimasu.</em><br>
The guest is here.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge good">Correct</span> いらっしゃいます is 尊敬語 for いる; it elevates the guest's presence.
</div>
</div>

<div class="keigo-pair">
<div class="bad-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Wrong</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お客様が参ります。</code></span><br>
<em>okyakusama ga mairimasu.</em><br>
The guest is coming.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge bad">Wrong</span> 参ります is 謙譲語 for 来る. Applying it to the guest treats them as lower-status.
</div>
<div class="good-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Correct</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お客様がお越しになります。</code></span><br>
<em>okyakusama ga o-koshi ni narimasu.</em><br>
The guest is coming.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge good">Correct</span> お越しになります is the 尊敬語 form appropriate for the guest's arrival.
</div>
</div>

The rule is absolute: 謙譲語 forms belong to the staff. The guest always receives 尊敬語.

## Quiz

**A staff member needs to announce that the guest has arrived. Which sentence is correct?**

- A. お客様が参ります。
- B. お客様がおります。
- C. お客様がいらっしゃいます。
- D. お客様がいたします。

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The guest's existence and arrival must always be expressed in 尊敬語. いらっしゃいます is the sonkeigo form of いる and 来る, making option C correct. Option A uses 参ります, the 謙譲語 form of 来る; applying it to the guest lowers them. Option B uses おります, the 謙譲語 form of いる; same error. Option D uses いたします, the humble form of する, which makes no grammatical sense here. 謙譲語 forms belong to the staff only.
