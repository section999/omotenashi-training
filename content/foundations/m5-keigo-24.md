Knowing keigo rules is one skill. Applying them instantly in the middle of a real service interaction is another. This lesson trains the judgment required to choose the correct keigo type in any situation.

## The Core Decision

Every keigo choice comes down to one question: who is performing the action?

| Subject | Keigo Type | Verb Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Guest | <span>尊敬語</span> | Use an honorific verb form |
| Staff | <span>謙譲語</span> | Use a humble verb form |
| None | <span>丁寧語</span> | Use a refined baseline form |

Once the subject is identified, apply the correct verb form and prefixes to build the full sentence.

## Quiz

**A colleague will bring the guest's luggage to the room. How should the staff explain this to the guest?**

- A. スタッフが荷物を持っていきます。
- B. スタッフがいらっしゃいます。
- C. スタッフがお荷物をお部屋にお届けいたします。
- D. スタッフが召し上がります。

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The staff member (not the guest) is performing the action, so 謙譲語 is required. お届けいたします uses the humble form of 届ける, correct for a staff action. お荷物 correctly prefixes the Japanese-origin word 荷物 with お. Option A is plain language with no keigo. Option B uses いらっしゃいます (尊敬語), which should not be applied to a colleague. Option D uses 召し上がります (尊敬語 for eating), which makes no sense in this context.
