title: Reservation Confirmation Template
---

Reservation confirmation emails are often the first direct communication between the hotel and the guest. Because of this, the message should create reassurance, professionalism, and a welcoming atmosphere.

The guest should feel: "My reservation has been handled properly."

A good reservation confirmation email should thank the guest, confirm important details, and create anticipation for the stay.

## Standard Template

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Opening</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>この度は、当ホテルをご予約いただき、誠にありがとうございます。</code></span>
<br><br>
This opening expresses gratitude and acknowledges the new reservation politely.
</div>

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Body</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご予約内容を確認いたしました。ご滞在中、快適にお過ごしいただけますよう、スタッフ一同準備を進めております。ご到着前にご不明点などございましたら、お気軽にお問い合わせくださいませ。</code></span>
<br><br>
This body section confirms the reservation, reassures the guest, and offers support before arrival.
<br><br>
The phrase <span class="keigo-jp"><code>スタッフ一同</code></span> means "all of our staff" and creates a stronger hospitality feeling.
</div>

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Closing</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご到着を心よりお待ち申し上げております。</code></span>
<br><br>
This closing creates emotional warmth and anticipation.
</div>

## Professional Tip

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Tone Check</span>
Reservation confirmation emails should sound: calm, welcoming, and organized. Avoid sounding too casual or overly robotic.
</div>

## Quiz

**What does the phrase 「スタッフ一同」 add to a reservation confirmation email?**

- A. A casual tone
- B. Technical information
- C. A sense of urgency
- D. A stronger hospitality feeling by involving all staff

**Correct Answer:** D

**Explanation:** 「スタッフ一同」 means "all of our staff" and creates a stronger hospitality feeling by showing that the entire team is prepared to welcome the guest.
