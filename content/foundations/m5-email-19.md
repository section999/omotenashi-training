title: Reservation Confirmation Template
---

Reservation confirmation emails are often the first direct communication between the hotel and the guest. Because of this, the message should create:

- reassurance,
- professionalism,
- and a welcoming atmosphere.

The guest should feel:

"My reservation has been handled properly."

A good reservation confirmation email should:

- thank the guest,
- confirm important details,
- and create anticipation for the stay.

## Standard Template

**Opening**

この度は、当ホテルをご予約いただき、誠にありがとうございます。

This opening expresses gratitude and acknowledges the new reservation politely.

**Body**

ご予約内容を確認いたしました。
ご滞在中、快適にお過ごしいただけますよう、スタッフ一同準備を進めております。
ご到着前にご不明点などございましたら、お気軽にお問い合わせくださいませ。

This body section:

- confirms the reservation,
- reassures the guest,
- and offers support before arrival.

The phrase:

「スタッフ一同」

means:

"all of our staff"

and creates a stronger hospitality feeling.

**Closing**

ご到着を心よりお待ち申し上げております。

This closing creates emotional warmth and anticipation.

## Professional Tip

Reservation confirmation emails should sound:

- calm,
- welcoming,
- and organized.

Avoid sounding too casual or overly robotic.

## Quiz

**What does the phrase 「スタッフ一同」 add to a reservation confirmation email?**

- A. A sense of urgency
- B. A stronger hospitality feeling by involving all staff
- C. A casual tone
- D. Technical information

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** 「スタッフ一同」 means "all of our staff" and creates a stronger hospitality feeling by showing that the entire team is prepared to welcome the guest.
