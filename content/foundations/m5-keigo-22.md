---

Restaurant service requires precise keigo for seating, taking orders, presenting food, and handling the bill. The sonkeigo forms for eating and drinking are used constantly in this department.

## Core Dining Phrases

<div class="keigo-card yellow">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>何名様でいらっしゃいますか。</code></span><br>
<em>nanmei-sama de irasshaimasu ka.</em><br>
How many in your party?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>こちらのお席へどうぞ。</code></span><br>
<em>kochira no o-seki e douzo.</em><br>
Please come this way to your seat.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご注文はお決まりでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>go-chumon wa o-kimari deshou ka.</em><br>
Have you decided on your order?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>アレルギーや苦手なものはございますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>arerugi ya nigate na mono wa gozaimasu deshou ka.</em><br>
Do you have any allergies or foods you cannot eat?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ごゆっくりお召し上がりくださいませ。</code></span><br>
<em>go-yukkuri o-meshiagari kudasaimase.</em><br>
Please enjoy your meal at your leisure.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お会計はこちらでございます。</code></span><br>
<em>o-kaikei wa kochira de gozaimasu.</em><br>
Here is your bill.
</div>

## Key Dining Keigo Notes

**召し上がります** is the 尊敬語 form of 食べる and 飲む. It is used whenever a guest is eating or drinking.

<div class="keigo-pair">
<div class="bad-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Plain form (wrong)</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>朝食は食べましたか？</code></span><br>
<em>choushoku wa tabemashita ka?</em><br>
Did you eat breakfast?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge bad">Wrong</span> Plain form does not elevate the guest's action.
</div>
<div class="good-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">尊敬語 (correct)</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>朝食はお召し上がりになりましたか？</code></span><br>
<em>choushoku wa o-meshiagari ni narimashita ka?</em><br>
Did you have breakfast?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge good">Correct</span> 召し上がる is the 尊敬語 form for eating and drinking.
</div>
</div>

## Quiz

**A restaurant server wants to ask "Have you decided on your order?" Which is correct?**

- A. 注文は？
- B. 何にしますか？
- C. ご注文はお決まりでしょうか。
- D. 注文をしましたか？

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** ご注文はお決まりでしょうか uses ご (prefix for 注文, a 漢語 word), お決まり (honorific pattern for the guest's decision), and でしょうか (polished teineigo question). Option A is too abrupt. Option B (何にしますか) is casual and lacks any keigo. Option D (注文をしましたか) is grammatically plain and does not apply any of the three keigo systems.
