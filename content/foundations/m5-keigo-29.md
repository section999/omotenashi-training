title: Error 5: わかりました vs かしこまりました
---

The choice between わかりました and かしこまりました is one of the most visible markers of whether a staff member is operating at hotel standard. The difference is not just vocabulary; it reflects how the staff is receiving the guest's instruction.

## The Four Levels of Acknowledgment

| Expression | Register | Appropriate Context |
|---|---|---|
| <span>わかった</span> | Casual | Never in hotel service |
| <span>わかりました</span> | Everyday polite | Between colleagues only |
| <span>承知しました</span> | Business polite | Internal staff communication |
| <span>かしこまりました</span> | Hotel keigo | Every guest-facing acknowledgment |

わかりました is grammatically correct and polite. In hotel service, however, it signals a casual register. かしこまりました communicates both understanding and a service commitment. The guest hears not just "I understand" but "I understand and I am committed to acting on this for you."

## かしこまりました in Practice

<div class="keigo-card yellow">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>かしこまりました。ただいまご確認いたします。</code></span><br>
<em>kashikomarimashita. tadaima go-kakunin itashimasu.</em><br>
Certainly. I will confirm that right now.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>かしこまりました。お部屋までご案内いたします。</code></span><br>
<em>kashikomarimashita. o-heya made go-annai itashimasu.</em><br>
Certainly. I will guide you to your room.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>かしこまりました。ご予約を承りました。</code></span><br>
<em>kashikomarimashita. go-yoyaku o uketamawarimashita.</em><br>
Certainly. I have received your reservation.
</div>

かしこまりました works best when immediately followed by the specific action the staff will take. This acknowledgment-plus-action pair is the standard hotel response structure.

## Quiz

**A guest asks the front desk to hold their luggage. The staff responds: 「わかりました。荷物を預かります。」What are the two errors?**

- A. わかりました should be かしこまりました; 荷物を預かります should be お荷物をお預かりいたします
- B. わかりました is correct at hotel level; only the second sentence has an error
- C. The staff should have said 承知しました instead; the second sentence is correct
- D. Both sentences are correct hotel keigo

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Two errors are present. First, わかりました is below hotel keigo standard; the correct acknowledgment is かしこまりました. Second, 荷物を預かります lacks the keigo required for a guest-facing service statement: 荷物 should be お荷物 (お prefix for 和語 noun), and 預かります should be お預かりいたします (humble form of 預かる plus いたします). The corrected response is: かしこまりました。お荷物をお預かりいたします。
