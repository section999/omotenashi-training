title: Keigo for Difficult Situations
---

Difficult situations such as complaints, mistakes, and policy limitations demand keigo that balances sincere apology with professional composure. The right language can de-escalate tension and preserve the guest's trust.

```dialogue
Guest:    ちょっと、隣の部屋の騒音がひどくて全然眠れません。
          どうなっているんですか？
Staff:    この度は、大変ご不快な思いをおかけいたしまして、
          誠に申し訳ございません。ただちに確認してまいります。
Guest:    本当に困っているんですよ。もう午前1時ですよ。
Staff:    おっしゃる通りでございます。早急に原因を調査し、
          対応させていただきます。少々お時間を頂戴できますでしょうか？
Guest:    分かりました。でも早くしてください。
Staff:    かしこまりました。支配人より直接お部屋に伺い、
          状況を確認した上で、ご報告申し上げます。
          今しばらくお待ちいただけますと幸いでございます。
Guest:    お願いします。
Staff:    重ね重ね、ご迷惑をおかけしておりますことを
          深くお詫び申し上げます。
```

## Key Phrases

| Japanese | English | Usage Note |
|---|---|---|
| ご不快な思いをおかけいたしまして | Causing you unpleasant feelings | おかけいたす is the humble form of かける; acknowledges responsibility |
| 誠に申し訳ございません | We sincerely apologize | The most standard formal apology phrase in service settings |
| ただちに確認してまいります | I will go confirm immediately | てまいります is humble for ていきます (going and coming back) |
| おっしゃる通りでございます | You are absolutely right | Validates the guest's complaint; uses honorific おっしゃる |
| 対応させていただきます | Allow me to handle it | させていただく humbly requests permission to take action |
| 時間を頂戴できますでしょうか | May I receive some time? | 頂戴する is the humble form of もらう, very polite request pattern |
| ご報告申し上げます | I will humbly report to you | 申し上げる is the humble form of 言う, used for formal reporting |
| 深くお詫び申し上げます | We offer our deep apologies | The strongest standard apology, suitable for serious complaints |

## Step-by-Step Breakdown

**Step 1: Apologize Immediately and Sincerely**

When a guest complains, the first response must be a full apology. 大変ご不快な思いをおかけいたしまして acknowledges the guest's specific distress. Never begin with excuses or explanations lead with the apology to show you understand the gravity of the situation.

**Step 2: Validate the Guest's Position**

Use おっしゃる通りでございます to confirm that the guest's complaint is reasonable. This validation reduces defensiveness and shows you are on the guest's side. It is a powerful de-escalation technique that uses the honorific おっしゃる to elevate the guest's words.

**Step 3: Propose Concrete Action with させていただく**

After apologizing, state exactly what you will do. 対応させていただきます uses the humble form to present your action as a service to the guest. Follow it with a specific step such as 支配人より直接お部屋に伺い to demonstrate commitment.

**Step 4: Use 申し上げる for Formal Promises**

Ending with ご報告申し上げます uses the deepest humble form of 言う. This communicates that reporting to the guest is not a routine task but an act of respect. Use 申し上げる for promises, apologies, and thank-yous in difficult situations.

**Step 5: Do Not End the Interaction Abruptly**

Close with 重ね重ねご迷惑をおかけしておりますことを深くお詫び申し上げます. Repeating the apology before the guest leaves shows sincerity and ensures the guest feels heard even after they have accepted your proposed solution.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It Is Wrong | Correct |
|---|---|---|
| すみません | Too casual for a formal complaint situation | 誠に申し訳ございません |
| 隣の部屋がうるさいんですね | Stating the problem back without apologizing first | この度はご不快な思いをおかけいたしまして、申し訳ございません |
| ちょっと確認してきます | Too casual; lacks humble framing | ただちに確認してまいります |
| 後で来ます | Vague and casual; no specific commitment | 支配人より直接お部屋に伺い、ご報告申し上げます |
| すみませんでした | Past tense is less sincere; ongoing situations need present apology | 深くお詫び申し上げます |

## Quiz

**What is the most important first step when handling a guest complaint?**

- A. Explain the hotel's policy immediately
- B. Apologize sincerely and acknowledge the guest's specific distress
- C. Call the manager before responding
- D. Ask the guest to wait without any apology

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The first step in any complaint handling is a sincere apology that acknowledges the specific issue. ご不快な思いをおかけいたしまして directly addresses the guest's distress, while 誠に申し訳ございません delivers the apology. Explanations and solutions come after the apology.
