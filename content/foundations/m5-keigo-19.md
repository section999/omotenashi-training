title: Guest-Oriented Sentence Patterns
---

These three sentence patterns cover the majority of guest-facing interactions where the focus is on the guest: confirming their identity, delivering information, and making polite requests.

## Pattern 1: Guest Identity Confirmation

Used at arrival and any time a guest's identity or status is being confirmed.

<div class="keigo-card yellow">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>[Name]様でいらっしゃいますか。</code></span><br>
<em>[Name]-sama de irasshaimasu ka.</em><br>
Are you Mr./Ms. [Name]?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>[Name]様でいらっしゃいますね。</code></span><br>
<em>[Name]-sama de irasshaimasu ne.</em><br>
You are Mr./Ms. [Name], correct?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご予約の[Name]様でいらっしゃいますか。</code></span><br>
<em>go-yoyaku no [Name]-sama de irasshaimasu ka.</em><br>
Are you Mr./Ms. [Name] with a reservation?
</div>

Components: ご (prefix for 予約, 漢語) + でいらっしゃいます (sonkeigo for the guest's existence).

## Pattern 2: Information Delivery

Used when telling the guest facts about the hotel, their room, or a service.

Structure: <span class="keigo-jp"><code>[subject]は[information]でございます。</code></span>

<div class="keigo-card yellow">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お部屋は702号室でございます。</code></span><br>
<em>o-heya wa 702-goushitsu de gozaimasu.</em><br>
Your room is Room 702.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>朝食は1階のレストランでございます。</code></span><br>
<em>choushoku wa ikkai no resutoran de gozaimasu.</em><br>
Breakfast is at the restaurant on the 1st floor.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>チェックアウトは11時でございます。</code></span><br>
<em>chekku-auto wa juuichi-ji de gozaimasu.</em><br>
Checkout is at 11:00.
</div>

Components: お (prefix for 部屋, 和語) + でございます (teineigo refined statement).

## Pattern 3: Asking the Guest to Act

Used when requesting a guest to do something. Always use the softened form to avoid sounding demanding.

<div class="keigo-card yellow">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>○○いただけますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>○○ itadakemasu deshou ka.</em><br>
Could you please ○○?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>こちらにご記入いただけますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>kochira ni go-kinyu itadakemasu deshou ka.</em><br>
Could you please fill this in?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>パスポートをご提示いただけますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>pasupooto o go-teiji itadakemasu deshou ka.</em><br>
Could you please show your passport?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>少々お待ちいただけますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>shoushou o-machi itadakemasu deshou ka.</em><br>
Could you please wait a moment?
</div>

Components: ご/お (prefix) + いただける (humble receiving) + でしょうか (teineigo question).

## Quiz

**A staff member wants to confirm a guest's name at check-in. Which is correct?**

- A. 名前は何ですか？
- B. 田中様ですか？
- C. 田中様でいらっしゃいますか。
- D. 田中様でございますか。

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** 田中様でいらっしゃいますか uses でいらっしゃいます, the sonkeigo form for confirming the guest's identity. This is Pattern 1 for identity confirmation. Option A is casual and direct, with no keigo. Option B is grammatically correct but uses ですか, which drops below hotel register. Option D uses でございますか, which is 丁寧語; it is polite but lacks the sonkeigo that properly elevates the guest in a personal identity confirmation.
