title: 尊敬語 (Sonkeigo) in Depth
---

尊敬語 is the foundation of guest-oriented language. Its purpose is to linguistically elevate the guest's actions and existence. This lesson covers every major sonkeigo pattern used in hotel service, with the precision needed for daily application.

## The Core Rule

Use 尊敬語 whenever the **guest** is the subject of the action. This is not simply a matter of swapping verbs. It reflects a core hospitality value: the guest is the honored party, and the language used must reflect that at every moment.

Before selecting a verb form, ask one question: **Is the guest the one performing this action?**

| Answer | Keigo Type | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Yes, the guest is acting | <span>尊敬語</span> | Elevates the guest's action |
| No, the staff is acting | <span>謙譲語</span> | Lowers the staff's position |
| Neither (general statement) | <span>丁寧語</span> | Maintains a polished, professional tone |

## Group 1: Irregular Sonkeigo Verbs

These verbs have entirely new forms in sonkeigo. They cannot be constructed by formula and must be memorized individually.

| Plain Form | Sonkeigo Form | Context |
|---|---|---|
| <span>いる</span> | <span>いらっしゃいます</span> | Guest is present |
| <span>来る</span> | <span>いらっしゃいます</span> | Guest arrives |
| <span>行く</span> | <span>いらっしゃいます</span> | Guest goes |
| <span>食べる</span> | <span>召し上がります</span> | Guest eats |
| <span>飲む</span> | <span>召し上がります</span> | Guest drinks |
| <span>言う</span> | <span>おっしゃいます</span> | Guest says |
| <span>する</span> | <span>なさいます</span> | Guest does |
| <span>見る</span> | <span>ご覧になります</span> | Guest looks / sees |
| <span>知る</span> | <span>ご存知です</span> | Guest knows |
| <span>くれる</span> | <span>くださいます</span> | Guest gives |
| <span>休む</span> | <span>お休みになります</span> | Guest rests |

## Group 2: Regular Sonkeigo Pattern

For verbs that do not have a special irregular sonkeigo form, apply this formula:

**お + [masu-stem] + になります**

The masu-stem is the verb in its ます form with ます removed. For example:

| Plain | ます form | Masu-stem | Sonkeigo |
|---|---|---|---|
| <span>書く</span> | <span>書きます</span> | <span>書き</span> | <span>お書きになります</span> |
| <span>待つ</span> | <span>待ちます</span> | <span>待ち</span> | <span>お待ちになります</span> |
| <span>使う</span> | <span>使います</span> | <span>使い</span> | <span>お使いになります</span> |

**More examples:**

<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お使いになります</code></span><br>
<em>o-tsukai ni narimasu</em><br>
Guest uses<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お読みになります</code></span><br>
<em>o-yomi ni narimasu</em><br>
Guest reads<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お書きになります</code></span><br>
<em>o-kaki ni narimasu</em><br>
Guest writes<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お待ちになります</code></span><br>
<em>o-machi ni narimasu</em><br>
Guest waits<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お持ちになります</code></span><br>
<em>o-mochi ni narimasu</em><br>
Guest carries / has
</div>

## Group 3: ご + Sino-Japanese Verb Stems

For verb stems of Chinese origin (漢語), use **ご** instead of お. These words are typically two-kanji compound nouns used as verbs with する.

**ご + [漢語 noun stem] + になります / なさいます**

| Plain (する form) | Noun stem | Sonkeigo |
|---|---|---|
| <span>利用する</span> | <span>利用</span> | <span>ご利用になります</span> |
| <span>確認する</span> | <span>確認</span> | <span>ご確認になります</span> |
| <span>予約する</span> | <span>予約</span> | <span>ご予約なさいます</span> |

**More examples:**

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご利用になります</code></span><br>
<em>go-riyou ni narimasu</em><br>
Guest uses a service<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご確認になります</code></span><br>
<em>go-kakunin ni narimasu</em><br>
Guest confirms<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご予約なさいます</code></span><br>
<em>go-yoyaku nasaimasu</em><br>
Guest makes a reservation<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご到着になります</code></span><br>
<em>go-touchaku ni narimasu</em><br>
Guest arrives<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご滞在になります</code></span><br>
<em>go-taizai ni narimasu</em><br>
Guest stays
</div>

## Critical Error: Sonkeigo Applied to Staff

Sonkeigo verbs elevate whoever performs the action. If a staff member uses them to describe their own actions, it linguistically places the staff above the guest, the opposite of what hospitality requires. This is not a minor slip; it signals a fundamental misunderstanding of keigo structure.

**Rule: いらっしゃいます and other 尊敬語 verbs are for guests only. For staff, use 謙譲語.**

<div class="keigo-pair">
<div class="bad-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Wrong</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>私がいらっしゃいます。</code></span><br>
I am here.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge bad">Wrong</span> いらっしゃいます elevates the speaker above the guest.
</div>
<div class="good-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Correct</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>私がおります。</code></span><br>
I am here.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge good">Correct</span> おります is the 謙譲語 form of いる; it lowers the staff's position.
</div>
</div>

<div class="keigo-pair">
<div class="bad-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Wrong</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>担当者がいらっしゃいます。</code></span><br>
The person in charge is here.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge bad">Wrong</span> Elevates a colleague above the guest.
</div>
<div class="good-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Correct</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>担当者がおります。</code></span><br>
The person in charge is here.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge good">Correct</span> おります keeps the staff in the appropriate humble position.
</div>

## Sonkeigo Question Forms

These three patterns cover the majority of questions directed at guests in a hotel setting.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>○○でいらっしゃいますか</code></span><br>
<em>○○ de irasshaimasu ka</em><br>
Are you ○○? Used to confirm the guest's identity or status.<br>
Example: <span class="keigo-jp"><code>田中様でいらっしゃいますか。</code></span><br>
<em>tanaka-sama de irasshaimasu ka.</em><br>
Are you Mr./Ms. Tanaka?
</div>

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>○○はいかがでしょうか</code></span><br>
<em>○○ wa ikaga deshou ka</em><br>
Would you like ○○? Used to offer something to the guest without applying pressure.<br>
Example: <span class="keigo-jp"><code>お飲み物はいかがでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>o-nomimono wa ikaga deshou ka.</em><br>
Would you like something to drink?
</div>

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>○○はいかがなさいますか</code></span><br>
<em>○○ wa ikaga nasaimasu ka</em><br>
How would you like to handle ○○? Used when asking the guest to make a decision.<br>
Example: <span class="keigo-jp"><code>朝食はいかがなさいますか。</code></span><br>
<em>choushoku wa ikaga nasaimasu ka.</em><br>
How would you like to handle breakfast?
</div>

## Quiz

**A staff member wants to ask "Will you be checking in?" Which is correct?**

- A. チェックインしますか？
- B. チェックインいたしますか？
- C. チェックインなさいますか？
- D. チェックインされていますか？

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The guest is performing the check-in action, so 尊敬語 is required. なさいます is the sonkeigo form of する, making option C correct. Option A uses plain language. Option B uses いたす, which is 謙譲語 and implies the staff is checking in. Option D is grammatically awkward and not standard hotel keigo. The guest's actions always require the honorific form.
