title: Why Hotel Keigo Sounds Different
---

Many learners study keigo from textbooks. However, real hotel keigo sounds different from classroom Japanese.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Why?</span>
Because hotels prioritize <strong>smoothness</strong>, <strong>clarity</strong>, <strong>comfort</strong>, and <strong>speed of communication</strong> &mdash; not perfectly academic keigo.
</div>

Real hotel keigo is: short, predictable, repeatable, and easy for guests to understand.

## Textbook vs. Hotel Standard

<div class="keigo-pair">
<div class="bad-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Textbook Style</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お名前を頂戴してもよろしいでしょうか</code></span>
</div>
<div class="good-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Hotel Standard</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご予約のお名前をお伺いしてもよろしいでしょうか</code></span>
</div>
</div>

| Dimension | Textbook Keigo | Hotel Keigo |
|---|---|---|
| Priority | Grammatical correctness | Guest comfort and clarity |
| Length | Longer, formal | Shorter, efficient |
| Rhythm | Written language | Natural speaking rhythm |
| Goal | Perfect grammar | Daily repetition and flow |

Hotel keigo is **operational language**. Not literary language.

## Key Phrases

| Japanese | English | Note |
|---|---|---|
| ご予約のお名前をお伺いしてもよろしいでしょうか。| May I ask the name on your reservation? | Hotel standard — smooth and clear |
| 少々お待ちくださいませ。| Please wait just a moment. | Short, repeatable, easy for guests |
| かしこまりました。| Certainly. | Daily confirmation — preferred over わかりました |
| ごゆっくりお過ごしくださいませ。| Please enjoy your stay at your leisure. | Warm closing — simple and professional |

## Quiz

**Why does hotel keigo differ from textbook keigo?**

- A. Hotels prefer academic language
- B. Hotels prioritize smoothness, clarity, and comfort over academic perfection
- C. Hotels use casual language only
- D. There is no difference

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Hotel keigo is operational language designed for daily repetition, natural rhythm, and guest comfort, not perfectly academic textbook Japanese.
