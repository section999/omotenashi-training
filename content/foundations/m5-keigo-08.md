While the core rules of keigo are consistent, each department has its own set of high-frequency phrases, service priorities, and linguistic conventions. This lesson presents the essential keigo vocabulary and sentence patterns by department.

## Front Desk

The front desk requires the widest keigo range. Staff must handle arrivals, identity confirmations, reservation management, problem-solving, and farewells, all in rapid succession.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>いらっしゃいませ。</code></span><br>
<em>irasshaimase.</em><br>
Welcome.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご予約のお名前をお聞かせいただけますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>go-yoyaku no o-namae o o kika se i ta dake masu deshou ka.</em><br>
Could you tell me the name on your reservation?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>○○様でいらっしゃいますね。</code></span><br>
<em>○○ sama de irasshaimasu ne.</em><br>
You are Mr./Ms. ○○, correct?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>こちらがお部屋の鍵でございます。</code></span><br>
<em>kochira ga o-heya no kagi de gozaimasu.</em><br>
Here is your room key.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>何かございましたらお申し付けくださいませ。</code></span><br>
<em>nani ka gozai mashitara o moushitsuke kudasaimase.</em><br>
Please let us know if you need anything.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>またのお越しをお待ちしております。</code></span><br>
<em>mata no okoshi o omachi shite orimasu.</em><br>
We look forward to your next visit.
</div>

## Dining and Restaurant

Restaurant service requires precise keigo for seating, taking orders, presenting food, and handling the bill. The staff frequently uses sonkeigo for the guest's eating and drinking actions.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>何名様でいらっしゃいますか。</code></span><br>
<em>nani mei sama de irasshaimasu ka.</em><br>
How many in your party?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>こちらのお席へどうぞ。</code></span><br>
<em>kochira no o seki e douzo.</em><br>
Please come this way to your seat.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご注文はお決まりでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>go chumon wa okimari deshou ka.</em><br>
Have you decided on your order?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>アレルギーや苦手なものはございますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>arerugi ya nigate na mono wa gozaimasu deshou ka.</em><br>
Do you have any allergies or foods you cannot eat?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ごゆっくりお召し上がりくださいませ。</code></span><br>
<em>go yukkuri o meshiagari kudasaimase.</em><br>
Please enjoy your meal at your leisure.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お会計はこちらでございます。</code></span><br>
<em>o kaikei wa kochira de gozaimasu.</em><br>
Here is your bill.
</div>

## Concierge and Bell Service

The concierge role combines information delivery with physical service. Keigo here emphasizes readiness to assist and the humble offering of services.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お荷物をお持ちいたします。</code></span><br>
<em>o nimotsu o o-mochi itashimasu.</em><br>
I will carry your luggage.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お荷物はお預かりいたします。</code></span><br>
<em>o nimotsu wa o azukari itashimasu.</em><br>
I will hold your luggage for you.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>タクシーをご手配いたします。</code></span><br>
<em>takushi o go tehai itashimasu.</em><br>
I will arrange a taxi for you.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>観光のご案内でしたら、こちらのパンフレットをご覧くださいませ。</code></span><br>
<em>kankou no go-annai deshi tara, kochira no panfuretto o goran kudasaimase.</em><br>
For sightseeing information, please take a look at this brochure.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご不明な点はいつでもお申し付けくださいませ。</code></span><br>
<em>go fumei na ten wa itsu demo o moushitsuke kudasaimase.</em><br>
Please ask us anytime if you have any questions.
</div>

## Housekeeping

Housekeeping frequently operates without direct guest interaction, but when communication is needed, it requires clear keigo for entering rooms, handling requests, and reporting.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ハウスキーピングでございます。お部屋のお掃除をさせていただいてもよろしいでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>hausukipingu de gozaimasu. o-heya no o souji o sa se te itadai te mo yoroshii deshou ka.</em><br>
This is housekeeping. May we clean your room?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご不便をおかけいたしました。大変申し訳ございません。</code></span><br>
<em>go fuben o okakeitashimashita. taihen moushiwake gozaimase n.</em><br>
I am very sorry for the inconvenience.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>タオルをお持ちいたしました。</code></span><br>
<em>taoru o o-mochi itashimashita.</em><br>
I have brought you towels.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご要望の品をお届けにまいりました。</code></span><br>
<em>go-youbou no shina o o-todoke ni mairimashita.</em><br>
I have come to deliver the items you requested.
</div>

## Telephone (Internal Transfer)

When transferring or routing calls between departments, a clear and consistent keigo structure prevents confusion and projects professionalism.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お電話ありがとうございます。[Hotel name]でございます。</code></span><br>
<em>o-denwa arigatou gozaimasu. [ Hotel name ] de gozaimasu.</em><br>
Thank you for calling. This is [Hotel name].<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ただいま担当部署におつなぎいたします。少々お待ちくださいませ。</code></span><br>
<em>tada ima tantou busho ni o tsunagi itashimasu. shoushou omachi kudasaimase.</em><br>
I will transfer you to the relevant department now. Please wait a moment.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お待たせいたしました。</code></span><br>
<em>o-matase itashimashita.</em><br>
Thank you for waiting.
</div>

## Comparing Department Priorities

| Department | Primary Keigo Challenge | Key Phrases |
|---|---|---|
| Front Desk | Smooth identity confirmation + information delivery | いらっしゃいます, でございます, 承りました |
| Dining | Sonkeigo for eating/drinking; allergy sensitivity | 召し上がります, お決まりでしょうか |
| Concierge | Humble service offering; readiness | お持ちいたします, ご手配いたします |
| Housekeeping | Permission to enter; apology language | させていただいてもよろしいでしょうか |
| Telephone | Opening line; hold and transfer language | おつなぎいたします, お待たせいたしました |

## Quiz

**A restaurant server wants to ask "Have you decided on your order?" Which is correct?**

- A. 注文は？
- B. 何にしますか？
- C. ご注文はお決まりでしょうか。
- D. 注文をしましたか？

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** ご注文はお決まりでしょうか uses the correct combination of ご (prefix for the Chinese-origin word 注文), お決まり (honorific pattern for the guest's decision), and でしょうか (polished teineigo question form). Option A is too abrupt. Option B (何にしますか) is casual and lacks keigo. Option D (注文をしましたか) is grammatically plain and does not apply any of the three keigo systems.
