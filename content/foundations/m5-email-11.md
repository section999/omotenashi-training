title: Closings for Apology Emails
---

Apology emails require especially careful endings.

The closing should:

- remain soft,
- support the guest,
- and maintain sincerity.

Avoid closings that sound:

- overly cheerful,
- promotional,
- or sales-oriented.

For example:

今後ともよろしくお願い申し上げます。

may feel inappropriate if used immediately after a serious apology because it can sound too focused on future business.

Instead, hotels often use support-oriented closings such as:

ご不明点などございましたら、お気軽にご連絡くださいませ。

or

何かございましたら、お申し付けくださいませ。

These expressions communicate:

- support,
- openness,
- and emotional care.

The guest should feel:

"The hotel is still listening and willing to help."

A good apology email should end gently rather than abruptly.

The final emotional tone should feel:

- calm,
- considerate,
- and professional.

## Quiz

**Which closing is most appropriate for a hotel apology email?**

- A. 今後ともよろしくお願い申し上げます
- B. 何かございましたら、お申し付けくださいませ
- C. また来てくださいね
- D. ありがとうございました

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Support-oriented closings like お申し付けくださいませ communicate openness and care. A can sound too focused on future business after an apology, and C/D are too casual.
