title: Cancellation Handling Template
---

Cancellation emails serve a dual purpose: they confirm the cancellation from an administrative standpoint and help preserve the guest relationship for future stays. Even when a guest cancels, the way the hotel responds can determine whether they return in the future.

This email must accomplish three key goals: clearly confirm that the cancellation has been processed; communicate any applicable cancellation fees in a professional and non-confrontational manner; and maintain a positive relationship with the guest by closing with warmth that leaves the door open for a future booking.

## Template 1: Simple Cancellation (No Fee)

<div class="keigo-card yellow email-example">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Opening</span>
<code>この度は、ご連絡いただきありがとうございます。</code><br>
<em>kono tabi wa, go-renraku itadaki arigatou gozaimasu.</em><br>
<span class="email-en">Thank you for contacting us.</span><br>
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Body</span>
<code>[Name]様のご予約（[Date]〜[Date]）を承りました取り消しが完了いたしましたことをご確認申し上げます。</code><br>
<em>[Name]-sama no go-yoyaku ([Date]〜[Date]) o uketamawarimashita torikeshi ga kanryou itashimashita koto o go-kakunin moushiagemasu.</em><br>
<span class="email-en">We confirm that the cancellation of Mr./Ms. [Name]'s reservation ([Date] to [Date]) has been completed.</span><br><br>
<code>キャンセル料は発生しておりません。</code><br>
<em>kyanseru-ryou wa hassei shite orimasen.</em><br>
<span class="email-en">No cancellation fee has been charged.</span><br>
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Closing</span>
<code>またの機会に、ぜひ当ホテルをご利用いただけますと幸いでございます。ご来訪をお待ちしております。</code><br>
<em>mata no kikai ni, zehi tou hoteru o go-riyou itadakemasu to saiwai de gozaimasu. go-raihou o omachi shite orimasu.</em><br>
<span class="email-en">We hope to have the opportunity to welcome you again. We look forward to your future visit.</span>
</div>

## Key Phrase

<div class="keigo-card yellow">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>誠に恐れ入りますが</code></span><br>
<em>makotoni osoreirimasu ga</em><br>
I am truly sorry to say, but...
</div>

This phrase softens the notification of a cancellation fee. It acknowledges the potential inconvenience without apologizing for the policy itself. It positions the fee as a circumstance of the booking terms rather than something the hotel is imposing arbitrarily.

## Quiz

**A guest cancels a reservation that incurs a fee. How should the cancellation email communicate the fee?**

- A. キャンセル料を払ってください
- B. 誠に恐れ入りますが、ご予約条件に基づき、キャンセル料が発生いたします
- C. キャンセル料はありません(when there is a fee)
- D. Say nothing about the fee and send a bill separately

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** 誠に恐れ入りますが acknowledges the inconvenience of the fee while maintaining a professional tone. ご予約条件に基づき (based on the reservation terms) frames the fee as a policy rather than a personal decision, which reduces the guest's sense of grievance. Option A (キャンセル料を払ってください) is too direct and sounds demanding. Option C is dishonest. Option D leaves the guest uninformed and creates distrust when the unexpected bill arrives.
