title: Mistake Using Repeat Guest Language Incorrectly
---

One of the most common mistakes is using repeat guest expressions for first-time guests.

<div class="keigo-pair">
<div class="bad-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Incorrect</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>いつもお世話になっております。</code></span>
<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge bad">Wrong context</span> This sounds unnatural because <span class="keigo-jp"><code>いつも</code></span> implies repeated visits, continued support, or an existing relationship. If the guest has never stayed before, the email may feel careless or automatically generated.
</div>
<div class="good-col">
<span class="keigo-pair-label">Correct for First-Time</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>この度は、ご予約いただき、誠にありがとうございます。</code></span>
<br><br>
<span class="keigo-badge good">Natural</span> This phrase naturally acknowledges a new reservation and a new relationship.
</div>
</div>

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Professional Practice</span>
Hotel staff should always confirm guest history before choosing greetings. Small wording choices strongly affect the impression of professionalism in hospitality culture.
</div>

## Quiz

**Why is 「いつもお世話になっております」 inappropriate for a first-time guest?**

- A. It is grammatically incorrect
- B. It is too short
- C. It is too formal
- D. 「いつも」 implies repeated visits or an existing relationship

**Correct Answer:** D

**Explanation:** 「いつも」 means "always" and implies repeated visits or an ongoing relationship. Using it for a first-time guest sounds careless or automatically generated.
