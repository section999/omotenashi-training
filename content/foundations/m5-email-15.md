title: Mistake Using Repeat Guest Language Incorrectly
---

One of the most common mistakes is using repeat guest expressions for first-time guests.

**Incorrect example:**

いつもお世話になっております。

This sounds unnatural because:

「いつも」

implies:

- repeated visits,
- continued support,
- or an existing relationship.

If the guest has never stayed before, the email may feel careless or automatically generated.

**Correct expression for first-time guests:**

この度は、ご予約いただき、誠にありがとうございます。

This phrase naturally acknowledges:

- a new reservation,
- and a new relationship.

Hotel staff should always confirm guest history before choosing greetings.

Small wording choices strongly affect the impression of professionalism in Japanese hospitality culture.

---

## Quiz

**Why is 「いつもお世話になっております」 inappropriate for a first-time guest?**

- A. It is too formal
- B. 「いつも」 implies repeated visits or an existing relationship
- C. It is grammatically incorrect
- D. It is too short

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** 「いつも」 means "always" and implies repeated visits or an ongoing relationship. Using it for a first-time guest sounds careless or automatically generated.
