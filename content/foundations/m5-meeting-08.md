Before ending shift, always confirm:

## 1. Guest Status

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Guest Status Checklist</span>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Check</span> VIP updated?<br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Check</span> complaints recorded?<br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Check</span> special requests logged?
</div>

## 2. Room Status

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Room Status Checklist</span>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Check</span> maintenance reported?<br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Check</span> cleaning complete?<br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Check</span> room changes reflected?
</div>

## 3. Pending Tasks

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Pending Tasks Checklist</span>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Check</span> anything waiting approval?<br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Check</span> anything not completed?
</div>

## 4. Communication Clarity

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Communication Checklist</span>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Check</span> sentences short?<br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Check</span> status included?<br>
<span class="keigo-badge info">Check</span> no emotional wording?
</div>

## Final Standard

<div class="keigo-card green">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Perfect Handover</span>
A perfect Japanese hotel handover is: short, factual, structured, unambiguous, and immediately actionable.
</div>

## Final Principle

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Final Principle</span>
"The guest should never notice that a shift change has happened."
</div>

## Quiz

**What is the final principle of hotel handover?**

- A. Write as much as possible
- B. The guest should never notice that a shift change has happened
- C. Always include the guest's name
- D. Use long sentences for clarity

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The ultimate goal of handover is seamless service continuity. If done correctly, the guest never realizes that different staff members have been handling their requests across shifts.
