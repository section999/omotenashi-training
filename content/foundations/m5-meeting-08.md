title: Handover Checklist
---

Before ending shift, always confirm:

## 1. Guest Status
- VIP updated?
- complaints recorded?
- special requests logged?

## 2. Room Status
- maintenance reported?
- cleaning complete?
- room changes reflected?

## 3. Pending Tasks
- anything waiting approval?
- anything not completed?

## 4. Communication Clarity
- sentences short?
- status included?
- no emotional wording?

## Final Standard

A perfect Japanese hotel handover is:

- short
- factual
- structured
- unambiguous
- immediately actionable

## Final Principle

"The guest should never notice that a shift change has happened."

---

## Quiz

**What is the final principle of hotel handover?**

- A. Write as much as possible
- B. The guest should never notice that a shift change has happened
- C. Always include the guest's name
- D. Use long sentences for clarity

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The ultimate goal of handover is seamless service continuity. If done correctly, the guest never realizes that different staff members have been handling their requests across shifts.
