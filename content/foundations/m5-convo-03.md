## Step 1: Welcoming the Guest

**Objective**

The first 10 seconds strongly affect the guest's impression of the hotel.

The staff should:

- greet immediately
- maintain calm eye contact
- avoid rushing
- speak clearly and softly

**Important Hospitality Principle**

Hospitality values:

- emotional reassurance
- predictability
- calm atmosphere

The guest should feel:

- guided
- respected
- safe
- welcomed

before any administrative procedures begin.

## Step 2: Reservation Confirmation

**Why Confirmation Matters**

The confirmation stage is not only for accuracy.

It also communicates:

- attentiveness
- preparation
- professionalism

Repeating reservation details reassures the guest that:

- the hotel is organized
- the booking was handled correctly
- the stay will proceed smoothly

**Best Practice**

Always confirm:

- guest name
- room type
- number of nights

Speak slowly enough for the guest to process information comfortably.

## Step 3: Information Delivery

**Recommended Information Order**

1. Room Key

Guests need immediate access information first.

2. Room Location

Explain:

- floor
- room number
- elevator direction if necessary

3. Breakfast

Guests often want to plan the next morning.

4. Wi-Fi

This is now considered essential information in modern hotels.

**Common Service Mistake**

Some learners speak too quickly because they focus only on grammar.

However, hospitality communication prioritizes:

- clarity
- pacing
- emotional comfort

The guest should never feel rushed.

## Step 4: Professional Closing

ごゆっくりお過ごしくださいませ

This phrase is extremely common in hospitality.

It does not simply mean "Goodbye."

It communicates:

- care
- warmth
- attentiveness
- hospitality professionalism

The phrase leaves the interaction feeling complete and elegant.

## Quiz

**Which of the following is the most important principle to follow when delivering information during check-in?**

- A. Let the guest ask questions first before providing any information
- B. Deliver all information as quickly as possible to save time
- C. Prioritize clarity, pacing, and emotional comfort over speed
- D. Use the most advanced keigo you know to impress the guest

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Hospitality communication prioritizes clarity, pacing, and emotional comfort. Speaking too quickly, even with correct grammar, makes the guest feel rushed and uncomfortable. The information should be delivered at a pace that allows the guest to process comfortably.
