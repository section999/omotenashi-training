title: Check-Out Dialogue and Closing Protocol
---

Check-out is the final interaction between a guest and the hotel during a stay. It is the last opportunity to leave a lasting positive impression, and the moment that often determines whether a guest returns or recommends the hotel to others.

Unlike check-in, which is about welcome and reassurance, check-out is about **graceful completion**. The guest is preparing to leave. The hotel's role is to process their departure efficiently, express genuine gratitude, and send them away with a warm farewell that leaves the relationship feeling unfinished, in the sense that they want to return.

## Check-Out Flow

Guest arrives at front desk → Greeting → Room number confirmation → Bill presentation → Payment → Final verification → Luggage offer → Farewell

## Complete Check-Out Dialogue

```dialogue
Guest:   チェックアウトをお願いします。
(EN)     I'd like to check out, please.

Staff:   かしこまりました。お部屋番号をお伺いしてもよろしいでしょうか。
(EN)     Certainly. May I ask for your room number?

Guest:   702号室です。
(EN)     Room 702.

Staff:   702号室の田中様でいらっしゃいますね。少々お待ちくださいませ。
(EN)     Mr./Ms. Tanaka in Room 702, correct? Please wait a moment.

         お待たせいたしました。こちらがご請求書でございます。ご確認いただけますでしょうか。
(EN)     Thank you for waiting. Here is your bill. Could you please take a look?

Guest:   はい、問題ありません。
(EN)     Yes, everything looks fine.

Staff:   ありがとうございます。お支払いはいかがなさいますか。現金またはカードでございますが。
(EN)     Thank you. How would you like to pay? We accept cash or card.

Guest:   カードでお願いします。
(EN)     Card, please.

Staff:   かしこまりました。こちらにカードをお通しください。
(EN)     Certainly. Please insert your card here.

         こちらがレシートでございます。
(EN)     Here is your receipt.

         この度は2泊ご利用いただきまして、誠にありがとうございました。
(EN)     Thank you very much for staying with us for two nights.

         お荷物はお持ちいたしましょうか。
(EN)     May I carry your luggage for you?

Guest:   大丈夫です。ありがとう。
(EN)     I'm fine, thank you.

Staff:   またのお越しをお待ちしております。お気をつけてお帰りくださいませ。
(EN)     We look forward to welcoming you again. Please take care on your journey home.
```

### Asking About Payment

**お支払いはいかがなさいますか**  
*o-shiharai wa ikaga nasaimasu ka*  
How would you like to pay?

いかがなさいますか is the honorific form of どうしますか. It asks the guest to make a decision without any pressure. The guest feels they are choosing, not being processed.

### The Thank-You for Staying

**この度は○泊ご利用いただきまして、誠にありがとうございました。**  
*kono tabi wa ○-haku go-riyou itadakimashite, makoto ni arigatou gozaimashita.*  
Thank you very much for choosing to stay with us for ○ nights.

ご利用いただきまして uses the humble form of "to use," expressing the guest's choice to stay as something humbly received by the hotel. This phrasing communicates genuine appreciation.

### The Farewell

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>またのお越しをお待ちしております</code></span><br>
<em>mata no okoshi o omachi shite orimasu</em><br>
We look forward to welcoming you again.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お気をつけてお帰りくださいませ</code></span><br>
<em>oki o tsukete o-kaeri kudasaimase</em><br>
Please take care on your journey home.
</div>

The first phrase keeps the relationship open, treating this not as goodbye but as "until we meet again." The second phrase expresses genuine care for the guest's safety beyond the hotel. Together they communicate that the hotel's concern for the guest does not end at the door.

## Luggage Assistance

Always offer to help with luggage, even if the guest appears to be managing. The offer itself communicates attentiveness:

**お荷物はお持ちいたしましょうか。**  
*o-nimotsu wa o-mochi itashimashoo ka*  
May I carry your luggage for you?

If the guest declines, respond with 承知いたしました (Understood) and accept gracefully with no disappointment shown.

## Quiz

**What is the most appropriate farewell phrase combination at check-out?**

- A. さようなら。またどうぞ。
- B. ありがとう！またね！
- C. またのお越しをお待ちしております。お気をつけてお帰りくださいませ。
- D. お気をつけて。ありがとうございました。

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The two-phrase farewell またのお越しをお待ちしております + お気をつけてお帰りくださいませ is the hospitality standard. The first phrase invites a return visit (keeping the relationship open). The second expresses genuine care for the guest's safety. Together they communicate that the hotel's concern for the guest continues beyond the interaction, which is the essence of omotenashi.
