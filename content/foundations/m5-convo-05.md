title: Restaurant and Dining Service Situations
---

Restaurant service within a hotel presents unique language demands. Unlike a standalone restaurant, hotel dining staff often serve guests who are tired, unfamiliar with the menu, or in a hurry before an event. The language of hotel restaurant service must be welcoming without being overwhelming, efficient without feeling rushed.

This lesson covers the complete flow of a hotel restaurant interaction — from seating to bill — with the specific keigo appropriate to each stage.

## Seating a Guest

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Greeting and Seating Script</span>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>いらっしゃいませ。何名様でしょうか。</code></span><br>
Welcome. How many guests are in your party?<br><br>
<strong>Guest:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>2人です。</code></span><br>
Two.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>2名様でございますね。ただいまご案内いたします。こちらへどうぞ。</code></span><br>
Two guests, correct? Please follow me this way.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>こちらのお席はいかがでしょうか。</code></span><br>
How does this table look for you?<br><br>
<strong>Guest:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>はい、大丈夫です。</code></span><br>
Yes, that's fine.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ありがとうございます。こちらがメニューでございます。ご注文がお決まりになりましたら、お声がけくださいませ。</code></span><br>
Thank you. Here is the menu. Please call us when you are ready to order.
</div>

## Taking an Order

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Order Taking Script</span>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご注文はお決まりでしょうか。</code></span><br>
Have you decided on your order?<br><br>
<strong>Guest:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>サーモンのグリルと白ワインをお願いします。</code></span><br>
Grilled salmon and a white wine, please.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>サーモンのグリルと白ワインでございますね。かしこまりました。</code></span><br>
Grilled salmon and white wine — certainly.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>アレルギーや苦手なものはございますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
Do you have any allergies or foods you prefer to avoid?<br><br>
<strong>Guest:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>特にありません。</code></span><br>
Nothing in particular.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>かしこまりました。少々お待ちくださいませ。</code></span><br>
Certainly. Please wait a moment.
</div>

## Serving the Food

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Food Service Script</span>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>お待たせいたしました。サーモンのグリルでございます。</code></span><br>
Thank you for your patience. Here is the grilled salmon.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>白ワインでございます。どうぞごゆっくりお召し上がりくださいませ。</code></span><br>
And the white wine. Please enjoy your meal at your leisure.
</div>

The phrase ごゆっくりお召し上がりくださいませ mirrors ごゆっくりお過ごしくださいませ from check-in. Both express the same hospitality philosophy: the guest is not rushed. They are invited to take their time and enjoy the experience fully.

## Checking on the Guest Mid-Meal

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Mid-Meal Check</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お料理のお味はいかがでしょうか。</code></span><br>
How is your food?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>何かご不明な点やご要望はございますでしょうか。</code></span><br>
Is there anything you need or any questions?
</div>

Checking in once during the meal shows attentiveness. However, checking too frequently interrupts the dining experience. In hotel restaurants, the standard is one gentle check after the main course arrives.

## Handling a Dietary Request

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Dietary Request Script</span>
<strong>Guest:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>アレルギーがあるので、ナッツを使わないものをお願いしたいのですが。</code></span><br>
I have allergies — could I get something without nuts?<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>かしこまりました。シェフに確認いたします。少々お待ちくださいませ。</code></span><br>
Certainly. I will check with the chef. Please wait a moment.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>お待たせいたしました。〜のメニューはナッツを使用しておりませんのでご安心くださいませ。</code></span><br>
Thank you for waiting. The 〜 dish does not contain nuts, so please rest assured.
</div>

## Presenting the Bill

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Bill Presentation Script</span>
<strong>Guest:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>お会計をお願いします。</code></span><br>
Could I have the bill, please?<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>かしこまりました。少々お待ちくださいませ。</code></span><br>
Certainly. One moment please.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>お待たせいたしました。こちらがお会計でございます。</code></span><br>
Thank you for waiting. Here is your bill.<br><br>
<strong>Staff:</strong> <span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご利用いただきまして、誠にありがとうございました。またのご来店をお待ちしております。</code></span><br>
Thank you very much for dining with us. We look forward to serving you again.
</div>

## Quiz

**After delivering a meal to a guest, what is the most appropriate closing phrase in a hotel restaurant?**

- A. ゆっくり食べてください
- B. どうぞ
- C. ごゆっくりお召し上がりくださいませ
- D. 美味しいですよ

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** ごゆっくりお召し上がりくださいませ is the standard hospitality phrase after delivering food. It invites the guest to enjoy their meal unhurriedly, communicating care and unhurried service. ゆっくり食べてください is grammatically plain and lacks service tone. どうぞ alone is too brief. 美味しいですよ ("It's delicious") is inappropriate as staff are not guests and should not evaluate the food on the guest's behalf.
