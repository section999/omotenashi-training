## Hospitality Speech Is Performance

In hotels, pronunciation is not only about correctness.

It also communicates:

- professionalism
- confidence
- emotional atmosphere

Even perfect grammar sounds unnatural if:

- intonation is too sharp
- rhythm is rushed
- tone sounds robotic

## Core Tone Characteristics

Professional hotel staff typically sound:

- calm
- warm
- stable
- reassuring
- controlled

Avoid sounding:

- overly energetic
- casual
- hurried
- monotone

## Shadowing Practice 1

ご予約のお名前をお聞かせいただけますでしょうか？

**Focus Points**

- slight pause after お名前を
- soften でしょうか
- avoid strong rising intonation

The sentence should sound like:

- an invitation
- a gentle request

not an interrogation.

## Shadowing Practice 2

こちらがお部屋の鍵でございます

**Focus Points**

- slow pacing
- smooth breathing
- stable volume

The phrase should sound:

- reassuring
- elegant
- organized

## Shadowing Practice 3

ごゆっくりお過ごしくださいませ

**Focus Points**

- warm emotional delivery
- gentle sentence ending
- downward intonation

This phrase should feel:

- comforting
- sincere
- welcoming

## Daily Practice Routine

1. Listen to the model audio (if available) or read aloud slowly
2. Focus on one phrase at a time, repeating 5-10 times
3. Record yourself and compare the tone, not just the words
4. Practice in front of a mirror to observe your facial expression
5. Aim for a calm, warm delivery rather than perfect speed

## Quiz

**What is the most important quality to focus on when shadowing hospitality phrases?**

- A. Speaking as quickly as possible
- B. Using the loudest voice possible
- C. Delivering with a calm, warm tone and natural pacing
- D. Memorizing the kanji for every word

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** In hospitality speech, calm and warm delivery matters more than speed. Even perfect grammar sounds unnatural if intonation is too sharp, rhythm is rushed, or tone sounds robotic. The goal is to sound reassuring and welcoming.
