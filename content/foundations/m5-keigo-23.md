Each support department has its own primary keigo challenge. This lesson covers the essential phrases and the one phrase from each department that most clearly represents its keigo priorities.

## Concierge and Bell Service

The concierge role combines information delivery with physical service. Keigo here emphasizes readiness to assist and the humble offering of help.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お荷物をお持ちいたします。</code></span><br>
<em>o-nimotsu o o-mochi itashimasu.</em><br>
I will carry your luggage.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お荷物はお預かりいたします。</code></span><br>
<em>o-nimotsu wa o-azukari itashimasu.</em><br>
I will hold your luggage for you.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>タクシーをご手配いたします。</code></span><br>
<em>takushi o go-tehai itashimasu.</em><br>
I will arrange a taxi for you.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご不明な点はいつでもお申し付けくださいませ。</code></span><br>
<em>go-fumei na ten wa itsu demo o-moushitsuke kudasaimase.</em><br>
Please ask us anytime if you have any questions.
</div>

## Housekeeping

Housekeeping often operates without direct guest interaction. When communication is needed, the priority is asking permission to enter and handling complaints with appropriate apology language.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ハウスキーピングでございます。お部屋のお掃除をさせていただいてもよろしいでしょうか。</code></span><br>
<em>hausukipingu de gozaimasu. o-heya no o-souji o sasete itadaite mo yoroshii deshou ka.</em><br>
This is housekeeping. May we clean your room?<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご不便をおかけいたしました。大変申し訳ございません。</code></span><br>
<em>go-fuben o o-kake itashimashita. taihen moushiwake gozaimasen.</em><br>
I am very sorry for the inconvenience.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご要望の品をお届けにまいりました。</code></span><br>
<em>go-youbou no shina o o-todoke ni mairimashita.</em><br>
I have come to deliver the items you requested.
</div>

## Telephone

When handling calls, keigo must be applied based on conversational role without visual cues. The opening line and hold/transfer language are the most important.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お電話ありがとうございます。[Hotel name]でございます。</code></span><br>
<em>o-denwa arigatou gozaimasu. [Hotel name] de gozaimasu.</em><br>
Thank you for calling. This is [Hotel name].<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ただいま担当部署におつなぎいたします。少々お待ちくださいませ。</code></span><br>
<em>tadaima tantou-busho ni o-tsunagi itashimasu. shoushou o-machi kudasaimase.</em><br>
I will transfer you to the relevant department now. Please wait a moment.<br><br>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>お待たせいたしました。</code></span><br>
<em>o-matase itashimashita.</em><br>
Thank you for waiting.
</div>

## Department Priorities at a Glance

| Department | Primary Keigo Challenge |
|---|---|
| Front Desk | Identity confirmation + information delivery |
| Dining | <span>尊敬語 for eating and drinking; allergy sensitivity</span> |
| Concierge | Humble service offering; readiness language |
| Housekeeping | Permission to enter; apology language |
| Telephone | Opening line; hold and transfer language |

## Quiz

**A housekeeping staff member wants to ask permission to clean the room. Which is the correct phrase?**

- A. 掃除してもいいですか？
- B. 部屋を掃除します。
- C. お部屋のお掃除をさせていただいてもよろしいでしょうか。
- D. ご掃除しましょうか？

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** お部屋のお掃除をさせていただいてもよろしいでしょうか uses お掃除 (fixed hotel expression with お), させていただく (humble form for requesting permission to act), and よろしいでしょうか (polished teineigo question). This is the standard housekeeping permission phrase. Options A and B are too casual and direct. Option D applies ご to 掃除 incorrectly; お掃除 is the fixed hotel form, not ご掃除.
