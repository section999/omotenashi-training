---

## Mistake 1: Writing Like Conversation

**Wrong:**

お客様がちょっと怒ってて…

This is unacceptable in handover logs.

## Mistake 2: Missing Status Information

**Wrong:**

エアコン壊れています

Missing: repair status, timing, action owner

## Mistake 3: Emotional Language

**Wrong:**

かなり大変なクレームでした

Handover must NEVER include emotion.

## Mistake 4: Vague Responsibility

**Wrong:**

誰かが対応する予定です

This is operational risk.

---

## Quiz

**Why is emotional language prohibited in hotel handover notes?**

- A. It takes too long to write
- B. Handover must be factual and actionable, not emotional
- C. Only managers can use emotional language
- D. It is acceptable in some cases

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Handover notes must be objective and actionable. Emotional language like かなり大変 (very tough) does not convey clear operational information and is unprofessional.
