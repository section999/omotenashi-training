title: Template Practice: Pre-Arrival Guidance
---

Pre-arrival emails help guests prepare for their stay before check-in. They typically cover check-in times, transportation guidance, parking, amenities, and hotel policies. The tone should be informative, supportive, and professional, reassuring the guest that everything is ready.

A pre-arrival email should reduce guest anxiety by making information clear, organized, and easy to follow.

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Opening</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>この度は、当ホテルをご予約いただき、誠にありがとうございます。</code></span>
<br>
<em>kono tabi wa, tou hotel wo go-yoyaku itadaki, makoto ni arigatou gozaimasu</em>
<br>
Thank you very much for reserving our hotel on this occasion.
</div>

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Body</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>ご到着前のご案内をお送りいたします。チェックイン開始時間は15時からでございます。駐車場をご利用のお客様は、ホテル正面入口までお越しくださいませ。また、ご不明点などございましたら、お気軽にお問い合わせください。</code></span>
<br>
<em>go-touchaku mae no go-annai wo o-okuri itashimasu. chekkuin kaishi jikan wa 15-ji kara de gozaimasu. chuushajou wo go-riyou no o-kyaku-sama wa, hoteru shoumen iriguchi made o-koshi kudasaimase. mata, go-fumeiten nado gozaimashitara, o-kigaru ni o-totawase kudasai.</em>
<br>
We are sending you information before your arrival. Check-in begins at 3:00 PM. Guests using the parking lot, please come to the hotel front entrance. If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us.
</div>

<div class="keigo-card blue">
<span class="keigo-card-title">Closing</span>
<span class="keigo-jp"><code>スタッフ一同、ご到着を心よりお待ち申し上げております。</code></span>
<br>
<em>staffu ichidou, go-touchaku wo kokoro yori o-machi moushiagete orimasu</em>
<br>
All of our staff sincerely look forward to your arrival.
</div>

This delivers essential details in a polite, guest-friendly tone. Notice how each piece of information is presented clearly, one step at a time, so the guest never feels overwhelmed.

## Quiz

**A pre-arrival email should prioritize which of the following over all else?**

- A. Listing every hotel policy in detail
- B. Making information clear, organized, and reassuring to reduce guest anxiety
- C. Promoting add-on services like spa treatments and dining packages
- D. Asking guests to confirm their reservation a second time

**Correct Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The primary goal of a pre-arrival email is to reduce guest anxiety by presenting practical information in a clear, organized, reassuring way. Overloading the guest with policies, promotions, or redundant confirmations undermines that purpose and can feel overwhelming rather than helpful.
