title: Summer Heat Greeting — July & August
---

**Season:** Summer · June — August

During Japan's hot, humid summer, acknowledging the heat in greetings is standard practice — both between colleagues and toward guests. It is a form of care, not small talk.

---

**Summer Greeting**

暑い中をお越しいただきありがとうございます。

*Atsui naka wo okoshi itadaki arigatou gozaimasu.*

"Thank you for coming in this heat."

---

**Written Summer Greeting (formal)**

暑中お見舞い申し上げます。

*Shochuu omimai moushiagemasu.*

"I offer my summer greetings to you." *(Used on seasonal cards and formal communications.)*

---

## Quiz

**A guest arrives in July looking flushed from the heat. What is the most appropriate greeting?**

- A. Say nothing about the weather — it may embarrass the guest
- B. Acknowledge the heat warmly: "Thank you for coming in this heat"
- C. Comment that the guest should have taken a taxi
- D. Ask if the guest needs medical attention immediately

**Correct Answer:** Acknowledge the heat warmly: "Thank you for coming in this heat"

**Explanation:** In Japan, acknowledging weather conditions — especially heat in summer and cold in winter — is a natural expression of care. The phrase 暑い中をお越しいただきありがとうございます communicates genuine appreciation and concern for the guest's comfort.
