---

**Season:** Winter · December — February

Oseibo is the traditional year-end gift-giving custom, where businesses and individuals send gifts of appreciation to those who have helped them during the year. High-end department stores and hotels process large volumes of gift deliveries throughout December.

**Hospitality context:** Business guests may be coordinating gift deliveries to their company's clients. Provide efficient assistance with receiving and forwarding deliveries. Gift wrapping and luggage storage services may see increased demand.

---

**Year-End Appreciation**

本年も大変お世話になりました。

*Honnen mo taihen osewa ni narimashita.*

"Thank you very much for your continued support throughout this year."

---

## Quiz

**In December, a business guest asks for help coordinating the delivery of gift packages to multiple recipients. What is the best response?**

- A. Explain that this is outside the hotel's responsibilities
- B. Offer efficient, proactive assistance with receiving and forwarding the deliveries
- C. Ask the guest to handle it themselves at the front desk
- D. Refer them to a nearby post office

**Correct Answer:** Offer efficient, proactive assistance with receiving and forwarding the deliveries

**Explanation:** During Oseibo season, gift logistics are a meaningful business concern for Japanese guests. Offering attentive, efficient support — without the guest needing to ask twice — reflects the precision and care of professional omotenashi.
