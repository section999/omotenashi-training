---

**Season:** Spring · March — May

From late March through mid-April, Japan's cherry blossoms transform cities and countryside alike. This is one of the peak foreign tourist seasons, and guests arrive in an elevated, celebratory mood. Parks fill with picnickers, and the atmosphere is festive yet fleeting.

**Hospitality context:** Guests often want local recommendations for viewing spots. The sakura season is brief — weather-dependent and lasting only one to two weeks — so urgency matters. Offer seasonal amenities where possible: sakura-themed sweets, tea, or small decorations in rooms.

- Recommend the best nearby viewing times and locations
- Have umbrellas available — spring rains arrive during peak bloom
- Celebrate the season in greetings without overstating it

---

**Seasonal Greeting**

今年の桜はとても綺麗ですね。

*Kotoshi no sakura wa totemo kirei desu ne.*

"The cherry blossoms are especially beautiful this year, aren't they."

---

## Quiz

**A guest asks where to see the best cherry blossoms. What is the most helpful response?**

- A. "I'm not sure, you should check online."
- B. Recommend the nearest convenient spot, regardless of bloom quality
- C. Offer specific local recommendations and mention that peak bloom is brief and weather-dependent
- D. Suggest they visit in summer instead

**Correct Answer:** Offer specific local recommendations and mention that peak bloom is brief and weather-dependent

**Explanation:** During sakura season, guests are highly motivated to see the blossoms at their peak. Proactive, specific guidance — including timing and location — reflects genuine omotenashi.
