title: Tanabata — Star Festival (七夕) — July 7
---

**Season:** Summer · June — August

Tanabata celebrates the annual meeting of two star deities — Orihime and Hikoboshi — separated by the Milky Way. Tradition involves writing wishes on colourful paper strips (tanzaku) and hanging them on bamboo branches. Many hotels and ryokan display elaborate bamboo decorations throughout the lobby.

**Hospitality context:** This is an excellent guest-engagement opportunity. Placing a wish-writing station in the lobby invites participation without obligation. Japanese guests will be familiar with the tradition; international guests will find it charming and memorable.

---

**Guest Invitation**

お願い事を短冊に書かれますか？

*O-negaigoto wo tanzaku ni kaka re masu ka?*

"Would you like to write a wish on a strip of paper?"

## Quiz

**A hotel sets up a Tanabata wish-writing station in the lobby. An international guest looks curious but hesitant. What should you do?**

- A. Ignore them — they are probably not interested
- B. Explain the tradition briefly and invite them to participate without pressure
- C. Insist they participate to learn about Japanese culture
- D. Direct them to an information pamphlet and move on

**Correct Answer:** Explain the tradition briefly and invite them to participate without pressure

**Explanation:** Tanabata is an engaging cultural touchpoint for international guests. A brief, warm explanation followed by a gentle invitation — without pressure — creates a memorable experience and reflects the spirit of omotenashi.
