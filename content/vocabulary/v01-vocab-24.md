title: ご案内いたします (go-annai itashimasu)
---

**ご案内いたします** means "I will guide you" or "I will show you." 案内 (案内) means guidance, escort, or information, elevated by the honorific *go-* prefix and completed with いたします, the humble form of "to do." In effect, the phrase is a declaration of service: *I am taking responsibility for getting one where one needs to go.*

Guiding a guest is one of the more intimate forms of service in hospitality. Unlike answering a question at a desk, guiding places the staff member physically alongside the guest, creating a small, temporary bond of movement and trust. ご案内いたします is the formal phrase that starts this process. One says it when picking up a guest from the lobby, escorting them to their room, leading them to a facility, or walking them to the entrance at departure.

The phrase carries responsibility. Once staff say ご案内いたします, one is committed to the guiding role until the guest arrives at their destination. That means walking at their pace, announcing hazards, opening doors, and giving them their full attention throughout. The quality of that walk is itself a form of hospitality

## Related Expressions

| Expression | Description |
|---|---|
| こちらへどうぞ<br>kochira e dōzo | "Please come this way": the movement invitation that follows ご案内いたします |
| お連れいたします<br>o-tsure itashimasu | "I will accompany you": slightly warmer/more personal, sometimes used in VIP contexts |
| ご案内申し上げます<br>go-annai mōshiagemasu | Even more formal version of ご案内いたします; used in highly elevated service contexts or formal announcements |

## Common Mistakes

A common mistake is saying ご案内いたします and then not fully committing to the escort: walking too fast, failing to open doors, not mentioning hazards, or leaving the guest to find the final stretch on their own. The phrase creates an obligation that must be honoured. A common error is treating ご案内いたします as a room-escort-only phrase, but it applies any time a guest needs to be physically guided somewhere: restaurant, spa, meeting room, elevator. If one is leading them, it should be used.

## Quiz

**A guest has just checked in and is ready to go to their room. You have their key card. What is the ideal phrase to begin the escort?**

- A. エレベーターはあちらです。(The elevator is that way: delegates navigation rather than guiding)
- B. ついてきてください。(Please follow me: too casual and direct)
- C. お部屋までご案内いたします。こちらへどうぞ。(I will show you to your room. Please come this way.)
- D. 自分でどうぞ。(Please go on your own: entirely inappropriate)

**Correct Answer:** お部屋までご案内いたします。こちらへどうぞ。(I will show you to your room. Please come this way.)

**Explanation:** ご案内いたします declares the commitment to personally escort the guest, and こちらへどうぞ with an open-palm gesture turns that declaration into movement. Pointing at the elevator or telling the guest to go on their own passes the navigation burden back to them, which abandons the very responsibility the phrase was designed to fulfil.
