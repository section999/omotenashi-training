title: 空室 (kuushitsu)
---

**Pronunciation:** **kuu**-shi-tsu
**Register:** Formal — used by front desk and reservations staff; appears in both verbal and written contexts such as availability displays and inquiry responses.

空室 means "vacant room" or "available room," and it is one of the most operationally important terms in hotel management. When a guest arrives without a reservation, or when a caller inquires about availability, the question of 空室 becomes immediately central to the interaction. Japanese hospitality culture emphasises the importance of never dismissing a guest without first checking — even when fully booked periods are expected, a thorough and polite verification of 空室 demonstrates genuine effort and care. The word combines 空 (empty, vacant) and 室 (room), forming a precise and professional compound.

In daily front desk operations, 空室 often appears in phrases such as ただいま空室をお調べいたします (I will check available rooms for you now), which pairs the word with お調べ — the humble/polite form of 調べる (to check, to look into). This construction signals to the guest that the staff member is actively working on their behalf. Availability systems in modern hotels display 空室 status in real time, and staff are expected to relay this information promptly and with appropriate language.

## Example Sentences

**1.** 少々お待ちください。ただいま空室をお調べいたします。
*Shōshō o-machi kudasai. Tadaima kūshitsu o o-shirabe itashimasu.*
"One moment please. I will check available rooms for you now."

**2.** 本日はシングルルームのご空室が1室ございます。
*Honjitsu wa shinguru-rūmu no go-kūshitsu ga ichi-shitsu gozaimasu.*
"We have one single room available today."

**3.** 空室がございましたら、すぐにご連絡いたします。
*Kūshitsu ga gozaimashitara, sugu ni go-renraku itashimasu.*
"As soon as a room becomes available, we will contact you right away."

## Related Expressions

- **満室 (manshitsu)** — the opposite of 空室; means fully booked with no rooms available. Knowing both terms allows staff to handle any availability scenario correctly.
- **空き (aki)** — a more casual and general word for vacancy or openness; 空室 is the precise hospitality-specific compound used in professional contexts.

## Common Mistakes

Learners sometimes respond to walk-in availability inquiries with 満室です before actually verifying — this is a serious service error that can be avoided by always defaulting first to ただいまお調べいたします. Confusing 空室 (vacant room) with 空港 (kūkō, airport) is an occasional pronunciation mishap due to the shared initial sound — ensure clear enunciation when speaking. Finally, avoid adding the count suffix incorrectly; vacant rooms are counted with 〜室 (shitsu): 1室, 2室, 3室.

## Quiz

**A guest asks 「今日泊まれますか？(Can I stay tonight?)」— choose the best response.**

- A. 少々お待ちください。ただいま空室をお調べいたします。(One moment please. I will check available rooms now.)
- B. 空室はございません。(We have no vacancies.)
- C. 予約がないとお泊まりできません。(You cannot stay without a reservation.)
- D. 今日は無理です。(That's not possible today.)

**Correct Answer:** 少々お待ちください。ただいま空室をお調べいたします。(One moment please. I will check available rooms now.)

**Explanation:** The correct response is to check availability before answering — 少々お待ちください (one moment please) + ただいま空室をお調べいたします (I will check available rooms now). Never refuse without checking first, and always use polite keigo form.
