title: 追加 (tsuika)
---

"追加" means addition, such as extra items or services. Guests may request additional towels or beds. Staff should confirm details and provide promptly. Clear communication is essential.

---

## Quiz

**Guest asks for extra items. Best response?**

- A. 追加でご用意いたします (we will prepare additionally)
- B. ただいま在庫を確認いたします (I will check inventory now)
- C. 追加料金が発生する場合がございます (an additional charge may apply)
- D. ハウスキーピングにお伝えいたします (I will inform housekeeping)

**Correct Answer:** 追加でご用意いたします (we will prepare additionally)

**Explanation:** 追加でご用意いたします — ご用意いたします (we will prepare) shows immediate readiness; saying 追加で first acknowledges that it is an extra request, not a default, which sets correct expectations.
