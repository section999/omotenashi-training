title: 枕 (makura)
---

**Pronunciation:** ma-**ku**-ra
**Register:** Neutral — everyday vocabulary used in all guest service contexts; appropriate in both spoken requests and written amenity lists.

枕 (makura) means pillow and is a small but significant element of guest comfort in Japanese hospitality. Pillows have strong personal associations with sleep quality, and many guests are particular about firmness, height, or material. Upscale hotels and ryokan in Japan often offer a 枕メニュー (pillow menu), allowing guests to select from a range of options including buckwheat (そばがら), memory foam (低反発), latex, and feather-filled varieties. The ability to accommodate such preferences is a hallmark of attentive omotenashi, as it demonstrates that the property anticipates individual needs rather than applying a one-size-fits-all approach.

When a guest requests an additional or different 枕, staff should respond immediately and positively, using the phrase 枕をご用意いたします or 枕をお持ちいたします. In ryokan settings, traditional cylindrical pillow shapes or 高枕 (takamakura, high pillows) may be offered alongside modern options, and explaining these choices with patience and warmth is part of the service experience. Proactively asking about pillow preferences during check-in, particularly in luxury or extended-stay contexts, elevates the perceived quality of service.

## Example Sentences

**1.** ご希望でしたら、枕の硬さをお選びいただけます。
*Go-kibou deshitara, makura no katasa wo o-erabi itadakemasu.*
"If you wish, you may choose the firmness of your pillow."

**2.** 追加の枕をすぐにお持ちいたします。少々お待ちくださいませ。
*Tsuika no makura wo sugu ni o-mochi itashimasu. Shoushou o-machi kudasaimase.*
"I will bring an extra pillow shortly. Please wait just a moment."

**3.** 枕が合わないなど、ご不便がございましたら、いつでもお申し付けください。
*Makura ga awanai nado, go-fuben ga gozaimashitara, itsudemo o-moushitsuke kudasai.*
"If the pillow does not suit you or you have any inconvenience, please let us know at any time."

## Related Expressions

- **枕カバー (makura kabaa)** — pillowcase; used when guests request a replacement or when housekeeping explains the daily linen change schedule.
- **毛布 (moufu)** — blanket; often requested alongside extra pillows as a pairing comfort item, especially during colder months or in air-conditioned rooms.

## Common Mistakes

Staff sometimes respond to pillow requests with vague language like 後ほど (later) without specifying a timeframe, which leaves guests uncertain — always give a specific timeframe or act immediately. Learners may also confuse 枕 with クッション (kusshon, cushion), which refers to decorative or sofa cushions rather than sleeping pillows; using the wrong word can cause confusion. Additionally, do not wait for a guest to complain about pillow discomfort — proactively mentioning the pillow menu during check-in is a more refined approach consistent with omotenashi values.

## Quiz

**Guest: "I would like an extra pillow." How do you respond?**

- A. 枕をご用意いたします (we will prepare a pillow)
- B. 枕はありません (there are no pillows)
- C. 毛布をお持ちいたします (I will bring a blanket)
- D. 枕どうぞ (here is a pillow)

**Correct Answer:** 枕をご用意いたします (we will prepare a pillow)

**Explanation:** 枕をご用意いたします uses ご用意いたします — the polite and professional way to confirm you will prepare something for the guest.
