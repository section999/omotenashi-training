title: 喫煙 (kitsuen)
---

"喫煙" means smoking. Hotels assign rooms accordingly. Staff must politely confirm smoking preferences to avoid complaints.

---

## Quiz

**Guest asks: "Can I have a smoking room?" Which is the correct polite response?**

- A. 喫煙でございます (it is a smoking area)
- B. 禁煙でございます (it is non-smoking)
- C. 喫煙しません (non-smoking)
- D. 禁煙お願いします (please refrain from smoking)

**Correct Answer:** 喫煙でございます (it is a smoking area)

**Explanation:** 喫煙でございます confirms the room type matches the request — using ございます maintains the professional register.
