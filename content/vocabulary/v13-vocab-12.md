title: 研修 (kenshuu)
---

**Pronunciation:** **ken**-shuu
**Register:** Formal — used in HR, management, and staff communications when referring to structured training programmes, induction periods, or skills workshops.

研修 refers to a structured, often intensive training programme designed to equip employees with the knowledge, skills, and attitudes required for their role. In Japan's hospitality industry, 研修 is taken extremely seriously: new hotel staff may undergo a week or more of initial 研修 before ever being placed in a guest-facing position, covering everything from greeting etiquette (礼儀作法) and phone manner to fire safety and emergency protocols. This investment in 研修 reflects the industry's commitment to consistent, high-quality service — the foundation of Japanese omotenashi (おもてなし).

研修 is not limited to new employees. Seasonal 研修 updates staff on new menus, services, or technologies, while ongoing 研修 sessions address changing customer expectations and industry standards. In prestigious establishments such as traditional ryokan, 研修 may also include cultural education — teaching staff about the history of the property, local customs, and the deeper meaning of hospitality in the Japanese tradition. For learners entering the industry, being a willing and attentive participant in 研修 is one of the fastest ways to build credibility with management.

## Example Sentences

**1.** 新入社員は入社後に1週間の研修があります。
*Shinnyu-shain wa nyuusha-go ni isshuukan no kenshuu ga arimasu.*
"New employees have a one-week training programme after joining."

**2.** フロントスタッフ向けに接客マナーの研修を実施しました。
*Furonto sutaffu muke ni sekkyaku manaa no kenshuu o jisshi shimashita.*
"We conducted a guest service manner training for front-desk staff."

**3.** 研修期間中は先輩スタッフが丁寧に指導してくれます。
*Kenshuu-kikan-chuu wa senpai sutaffu ga teinei ni shidou shite kuremasu.*
"During the training period, senior staff will guide you carefully."

## Related Expressions

- **教育 (kyouiku)** — education or development in a broader, ongoing sense; while 研修 is typically a defined programme with a start and end, 教育 encompasses the wider continuous development of employees over their career.
- **OJT (On-the-Job Training)** — hands-on learning within the actual work environment, often used alongside or following formal 研修 in Japanese hospitality onboarding.

## Common Mistakes

Learners sometimes confuse 研修 with 教育, treating them as synonyms. The key distinction is that 研修 is a structured, time-bounded programme (often with a schedule, materials, and assessment), while 教育 is the broader, more holistic process of nurturing an employee's capabilities. Using 研修 when referring to a manager's ongoing informal mentoring of their team is too narrow — 教育 or 指導 would be more appropriate there.

## Quiz

**Fill in the blank: ___**

**Question:** Programmes to develop employee skills and knowledge are called ____ in Japanese.

**Answer:** 研修

**Explanation:** 研修 (kenshuu) means training — structured programmes to improve employee skills, knowledge, and adherence to company standards.
