title: 滞在 (taizai)
---

**Pronunciation:** tai-**zai**
**Register:** Formal — used by front desk staff, concierge teams, and management across hotels and ryokan to describe the entirety of a guest's visit, from arrival to departure.

滞在 refers to the full period a guest spends at the property — it is broader than 到着日 or 出発日, capturing the complete guest experience as a continuous whole. In omotenashi philosophy, thinking in terms of 滞在 rather than isolated transactions encourages staff to consider the guest's comfort, mood, and needs across the entire visit, not just at check-in or checkout. Phrases like お滞在中はごゆっくりおくつろぎください (please relax during your stay) are standard hospitality expressions that frame the hotel as a sanctuary for the duration of the guest's time there. This holistic perspective is one of the defining qualities of Japanese hospitality.

Practically, 滞在 appears in billing summaries (ご滞在中の料金), service announcements (滞在中はルームサービスをご利用いただけます), and post-stay follow-ups (ご滞在はいかがでしたでしょうか). It also crops up in conversation when staff enquire about a guest's overall comfort — choosing 滞在 over narrower terms signals that staff are attending to the whole experience, not just a specific moment. When calculating charges or planning services, 滞在 encompasses every element booked from arrival to departure.

## Example Sentences

**1.** お滞在中はごゆっくりおくつろぎください。
*O-taizai chuu wa go-yukkuri o-kutsurogi kudasai.*
"Please relax and enjoy your stay with us."

**2.** ご滞在は3泊4日のご予定でございますね。
*Go-taizai wa san-paku yokka no go-yotei de gozaimasu ne.*
"Your stay is scheduled for 3 nights and 4 days."

**3.** ご滞在中にご不便がございましたら、いつでもフロントまでお申しつけください。
*Go-taizai chuu ni go-fuben ga gozaimashitara, itsudemo furonto made o-moushitsuke kudasai.*
"If you experience any inconvenience during your stay, please let us know at the front desk at any time."

## Related Expressions

- **宿泊 (shukuhaku)** — focuses specifically on overnight accommodation; 滞在 is broader and can include day-use stays, while 宿泊 always implies at least one overnight.
- **お過ごし (o-sugoshi)** — a softer, warmer expression meaning "how you spend your time"; used in phrases like お過ごしはいかがでしょうか (how are you getting on?) as a guest-comfort check during a stay.

## Common Mistakes

Learners sometimes substitute 宿泊 for 滞在 in phrases like お滞在中、mistakenly saying お宿泊中, which sounds unnatural in spoken hospitality Japanese — 滞在中 is the standard construction for "during your stay." Another error is treating 滞在 as synonymous with 泊 (a counter for nights): 3泊 means three nights, but 3日間の滞在 means a three-day stay that may or may not be entirely overnight — conflating them leads to billing confusion.

## Quiz

**Which term describes the overall time a guest spends in the hotel?**

- A. 滞在 (stay)
- B. 宿泊日 (stay date)
- C. 到着日 (arrival date)
- D. 出発日 (departure date)

**Correct Answer:** 滞在 (stay)

**Explanation:** 滞在 covers the full guest experience from arrival to departure — saying お滞在中はごゆっくりおくつろぎください (please relax during your stay) is a standard welcome phrase that shows warmth and sets a positive tone.
