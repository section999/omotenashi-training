title: 確定 (kakutei)
---

**Pronunciation:** ka-ku-**tei**
**Register:** Neutral to Formal — 確定 is a precise, professional term used in written confirmation documents, reservation system status fields, and spoken check-in interactions. Its tone is formal and reassuring when used by staff; it signals finality and commitment. In polite speech, it typically appears in constructions such as ご予約は確定しております, where the honorific prefix ご and the polite form しております frame the directness of the term itself.

確定 (kakutei) means "confirmed" or "finalised" and indicates that a reservation has moved from tentative or pending status to a guaranteed, committed state. In hotel operations, 確定 is one of the most reassuring words a guest can hear, because it means their room is secured, their plan is locked in, and their stay is guaranteed. From a property management perspective, 確定 status triggers room allocation, housekeeping scheduling, and — where applicable — meal preparation planning, so the transition from tentative to 確定 is a meaningful operational milestone, not merely an administrative label.

In practice, staff use 確定 most visibly at check-in when verbally reassuring guests that their booking is in order, and in written or emailed reservation confirmations that accompany the 確定 status change. Best practice is to state 確定 alongside the reservation number (予約番号), as the number gives the guest a concrete reference point for any future inquiry and serves as written proof of the booking. For guests who have travelled long distances or are attending important events, the combination of ご予約は確定しております + a stated 予約番号 is a particularly powerful reassurance.

## Example Sentences

**1.** ご予約は確定しております。予約番号は12345でございます。
*Go-yoyaku wa kakutei shite orimasu. Yoyaku bangou wa ichi-ni-san-yon-go de gozaimasu.*
"Your reservation is confirmed. Your reservation number is 12345."

**2.** お支払いが完了いたしましたので、ご予約が確定となりました。
*O-shiharai ga kanryou itashimashita node, go-yoyaku ga kakutei to narimashita.*
"Your payment has been completed, and your reservation is now confirmed."

**3.** 確定後は内容の変更に制限がある場合がございますので、ご確認をお願いいたします。
*Kakutei go wa naiyou no henkou ni seigen ga aru baai ga gozaimasu node, go-kakunin wo o-negai itashimasu.*
"Please note that there may be restrictions on changes after confirmation."

## Related Expressions

- **仮予約 (kari yoyaku)** — The provisional state that precedes 確定: a tentative reservation that has not yet been finalised. Understanding the progression from 仮予約 to 確定 is essential for front desk staff, as it governs what the property is and is not committed to at each stage.
- **予約番号 (yoyaku bangou)** — The reservation number issued when a booking reaches 確定 status. These two terms are operationally linked: whenever 確定 is communicated to a guest, the 予約番号 should accompany it as tangible proof.

## Common Mistakes

A common mistake is using 確定 loosely to mean "I think your booking is fine" before actually verifying it in the system, which can result in a guest arriving to find their reservation was never properly secured. Staff sometimes also neglect to provide the 予約番号 alongside 確定, which means the guest has no reference if a discrepancy arises. A third error is failing to inform the guest of any post-confirmation restrictions — particularly that certain 確定 plan types are 変更不可 after a certain date — which sets up future disputes when the guest later discovers the flexibility they assumed does not exist.

## Quiz

**Guest asks: "Is my booking guaranteed?" Which term describes this?**

- A. 確定 (confirmed)
- B. 仮予約 (tentative reservation)
- C. 空き状況 (availability)
- D. 延長 (extension)

**Correct Answer:** 確定 (confirmed)

**Explanation:** 確定 is the final committed state — ご予約は確定しております is the reassuring phrase guests want to hear; follow it with the confirmation number (予約番号) so they have written proof, which reduces anxiety for long-distance travelers.
