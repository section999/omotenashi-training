title: 手配 (tehai)
---

手配 means to arrange, organize, or coordinate something on someone's behalf. In the hotel context, it encompasses a wide range of activities: booking airport transfers, reserving restaurant tables, arranging flower deliveries, or preparing room setups for special occasions. The word conveys active effort — staff are not merely passing on a request but taking personal responsibility to ensure everything is properly set up. This sense of proactive coordination is central to Japanese hospitality culture, where the unseen labor behind a seamless guest experience is just as valued as the visible service itself. Using 手配 signals to the guest that the matter is being handled with care and attention to detail.

When using 手配 in speech, the humble form 手配いたします is the standard expression at luxury and upscale properties, while 手配します is acceptable in more casual settings. Before committing with 手配いたします, staff should confirm all key details — departure time, number of passengers, destination, and any special requirements — to avoid misalignments. Following up with the guest after the arrangement is complete (例：タクシーの手配が完了いたしました) closes the communication loop and reassures the guest that nothing has been overlooked. Coordination with third-party vendors or internal departments often requires clear documentation to ensure the 手配 is executed as promised.

## Example Sentences

**1.** 空港までのタクシーを手配いたします。
*Kuukou made no takushii wo tehai itashimasu.*
"We will arrange a taxi to the airport for you."

**2.** お部屋にお花を手配させていただきました。
*Oheya ni ohana wo tehai sasete itadakimashita.*
"We have arranged flowers for your room."

**3.** ご要望のレストランのご予約も手配いたします。
*Go-youbou no resutoran no go-yoyaku mo tehai itashimasu.*
"We will also arrange a reservation at your requested restaurant."

## Related Expressions

- **準備 (junbi)** — preparation; focuses on getting things ready rather than the coordination process itself; often used alongside 手配 when both planning and setup are involved
- **調整 (chousei)** — adjustment or coordination; used when fine-tuning schedules or balancing multiple parties, as opposed to the initial act of arranging a service

## Common Mistakes

A common error is saying 手配します immediately before confirming the guest's details, which can lead to incorrect arrangements that disappoint rather than delight. Learners also sometimes confuse 手配 with 準備 — 手配 emphasizes the act of organizing or commissioning a service (often involving a third party), while 準備 refers to getting something ready oneself. Additionally, forgetting to follow up after a 手配 is complete leaves guests uncertain about whether their request was fulfilled, which undermines the trust that careful arrangement was meant to build.

## Quiz

**Guest asks: "Can you organize a taxi for me?" Which term applies?**

- A. 手配 (arrangement)
- B. 予約変更 (reservation change)
- C. 追加料金 (additional charge)
- D. 割引 (discount)

**Correct Answer:** 手配 (arrangement)

**Explanation:** 手配いたします (we will arrange it) is the humble commitment phrase for any coordination task — always confirm the details (時間、人数、行き先) before saying 手配いたします so the guest knows you have captured everything correctly.
