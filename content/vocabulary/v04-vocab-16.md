title: 雑巾 (zoukin)
---

雑巾 is a cleaning cloth or rag, traditionally made from worn-out fabric, used to wipe down surfaces by hand. In Japanese hospitality, cleanliness is directly linked to guest comfort and the hotel's reputation, so knowing exactly when and how to use a 雑巾 is a core housekeeping competency. Unlike a mop (モップ), which covers large open floors, the 雑巾 is the tool of precision — used on tables, window ledges, bathroom counters, and anywhere that requires close-contact wiping.

In practical hotel work, staff use 雑巾 as part of regular room turnover and deep-cleaning routines. Phrases such as 濡れた雑巾で拭きます ("wipe with a damp cloth") and 乾いた雑巾で仕上げます ("finish with a dry cloth") are common instructions given during training. It is important to distinguish between cloths used for different surfaces — using the same 雑巾 on a toilet and then on a desk, for example, violates hygiene standards and must be strictly avoided.

## Example Sentences

**1.** テーブルは清潔な雑巾で丁寧に拭いてください。
*Teeburu wa seiketsu na zoukin de teinei ni fuite kudasai.*
"Please wipe the table carefully with a clean cloth."

**2.** 濡れた雑巾で汚れを落とした後、乾いた雑巾で仕上げます。
*Nureta zoukin de yogore wo otoshita ato, kawaita zoukin de shiageimasu.*
"After removing the stain with a damp cloth, we finish with a dry cloth."

**3.** 用途ごとに色分けされた雑巾をご使用ください。
*Youto goto ni iroduke sareta zoukin wo go-shiyou kudasai.*
"Please use the color-coded cloths designated for each purpose."

## Related Expressions

- **モップ (moppu)** — A wet mop used for cleaning large floor areas. While 雑巾 is used for detailed hand-wiping of surfaces and furniture, モップ covers expansive floor space more efficiently; both tools have distinct roles and should not be substituted for each other.
- **タオル (taoru)** — A towel, which in a hospitality context is a guest-facing item. Unlike 雑巾, which is a staff cleaning tool, タオル is provided for guests and should never be repurposed as a cleaning cloth.

## Common Mistakes

A frequent mistake is confusing 雑巾 with タオル — in professional settings, guest towels must never be used as cleaning rags, and trainees sometimes blur this boundary. Another error is neglecting to specify wet or dry: 濡れた雑巾 (damp cloth) and 乾いた雑巾 (dry cloth) serve different finishing purposes, and using the wrong one can leave streaks or residue. Finally, learners sometimes omit the で particle: the correct form is 雑巾**で**拭きます, not 雑巾を拭きます.

## Quiz

**テーブルは____で拭きます。
(We wipe the table with a cleaning cloth.)**

- A. 雑巾 (cleaning cloth / rag)
- B. 掃除機 (vacuum cleaner)
- C. モップ (mop)
- D. ゴミ箱 (trash can)

**Correct Answer:** 雑巾 (cleaning cloth / rag)

**Explanation:** 雑巾 is used for wiping surfaces — 拭きます means "to wipe", and knowing the right tool for each task is part of professional housekeeping.
