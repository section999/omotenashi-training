title: Wi-Fi (waifai)
---

**Pronunciation:** [wai-**fai**]
**Register:** Neutral/Informal term, but used in formal service phrases when explaining connectivity to guests

Wi-Fi (ワイファイ) has become one of the most expected amenities in modern hospitality, and its quality and availability frequently show up in guest satisfaction surveys. In Japan, where domestic business travellers and international tourists alike depend heavily on connectivity, a hotel's ability to provide fast and reliable Wi-Fi says something real about its service standard. Staff who can explain access procedures clearly, including how to find the network name (SSID) and enter the password, demonstrate an attentiveness that goes well beyond the basics.

Wi-Fi information is typically shown on a card in the room, printed on the key sleeve, or available at the front desk. Common phrases include 「Wi-Fiをご利用いただけます」 (Wi-Fi is available for your use) and 「Wi-Fiのパスワードはこちらでございます」 (here is the Wi-Fi password). When a guest reports a connectivity problem, walk through it with them step by step and escalate to technical support if needed. In practice, proactively mentioning Wi-Fi during check-in has become standard at higher-end properties. Guests should not have to hunt for it.

## Example Sentences

**1.** 当ホテルでは、全館で無料のWi-Fiをご利用いただけます。
*Tou hoteru de wa, zenkan de muryou no waifai wo go-riyou itadakemasu.*
"At our hotel, complimentary Wi-Fi is available throughout the entire building."

**2.** Wi-FiのネットワークIDとパスワードは、お部屋のカードにご記載しております。
*Waifai no nettowaaку ID to pasuwaado wa, o-heya no kaado ni go-kisai shite orimasu.*
"The Wi-Fi network ID and password are printed on the card in your room."

**3.** Wi-Fiに接続できない場合は、フロントまでお申しつけください。
*Waifai ni setsuzoku dekinai baai wa, furonto made o-moushitsuke kudasai.*
"If you are unable to connect to Wi-Fi, please let us know at the front desk."

## Related Expressions

- **インターネット (intaanetto)**: the broader term for internet access. Wi-Fi refers to the wireless delivery method, while インターネット refers to the network itself. A property might offer インターネット via both Wi-Fi and a wired LAN cable (有線LAN), so knowing the distinction helps when you are troubleshooting.
- **接続 (setsuzoku)**: means "connection" and is used when describing the act of linking a device to the network. Wi-Fi is the service; 接続 is the action of using it, as in 「接続方法をご案内いたします」 (I will guide you on how to connect).

## Common Mistakes

A frequent error is dropping the honorific language when discussing Wi-Fi, saying 「Wi-Fiあります」 instead of 「Wi-Fiをご利用いただけます」. The second version positions the service as something the property has prepared for the guest's benefit, which is the right framing. Students often forget to clarify whether Wi-Fi is 無料 (free) or 有料 (charged) as well, and that omission can lead to billing disputes later. Worth noting: when you write the password for a guest, always double-check uppercase, lowercase, and special characters before handing it over. A single wrong character sends the guest back to the desk frustrated.

## Quiz

**Guest asks: "Do you have Wi-Fi?" Best response?**

- A. Wi-Fiをご利用いただけます (Wi-Fi is available)
- B. ありません (there is not / we don't have)
- C. 知らない (don't know)
- D. 無理 (impossible / not feasible)

**Correct Answer:** Wi-Fiをご利用いただけます (Wi-Fi is available)

**Explanation:** Wi-Fiをご利用いただけます: ご利用いただけます (you are able to use) is the polite way to confirm service availability; it positions access as a privilege prepared for the guest.
