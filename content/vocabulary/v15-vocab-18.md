title: ロッカー (rokkaa)
---

**Pronunciation:** **rok**-kaa
**Register:** Neutral — everyday term used in hotels, train stations, tourist sites, and gyms; staff use it matter-of-factly when directing guests to self-service storage options.

ロッカー (rokkaa) is the Japanese adoption of the English word "locker," referring to a secure, individual storage compartment that guests can use independently to store bags, clothing, or other personal belongings. In Japan, coin-operated ロッカー are found at virtually every major train station, tourist attraction, and shopping area, making them an essential piece of infrastructure for travelers who want to explore freely without carrying luggage. Unlike the luggage storage service (保管) offered by hotel staff, a ロッカー is a self-service solution — the guest operates it directly using a key, digital code, or coin without needing to interact with staff.

In hotels, ロッカー may be found in gym changing rooms, spa facilities, or even lobby areas for day-use guests. Hotel staff are frequently asked by guests about nearby ロッカー options, particularly at train stations close to popular tourist areas. A good hospitality professional is familiar with the local ロッカー infrastructure: which stations have them, what sizes are available (small, medium, large), how the payment works (most now accept IC cards like Suica as well as coins), and what to do if a ロッカー malfunctions. Guiding a guest confidently to the right storage solution — whether a hotel 保管 service or a nearby coin ロッカー — reflects the practical dimension of omotenashi.

## Example Sentences

**1.** ロッカーはロビー横のコインロッカーコーナーにございます。
*Rokkaa wa robii yoko no koin-rokkaa koona ni gozaimasu.*
"The lockers are located in the coin-locker area beside the lobby."

**2.** 駅構内のロッカーは大・中・小のサイズがございます。
*Eki kounai no rokkaa wa dai, chuu, shou no saizu ga gozaimasu.*
"The lockers inside the station come in large, medium, and small sizes."

**3.** ロッカーのご利用はSuicaまたは小銭でお支払いいただけます。
*Rokkaa no go-riyou wa Suika mata wa kozeni de o-shiharai itadakemasu.*
"Lockers can be paid for using Suica or coins."

## Related Expressions

- **コインロッカー (koin-rokkaa)** — coin locker; the most common type of public ロッカー in Japan, historically operated with 100-yen coins, now increasingly accepting IC cards
- **手荷物一時預かり所 (te-nimotsu ichiji azukari-jo)** — staffed luggage storage counter; an alternative to ロッカー where a person accepts and holds bags, offering more flexibility for oversized items

## Common Mistakes

Because ロッカー is a direct borrowing from English, learners sometimes assume its meaning is identical to the English "locker" in all contexts. However, in Japanese hospitality, ロッカー typically refers specifically to coin-operated public storage compartments, whereas changing room lockers in gyms or onsen may be described differently. Staff should also take care not to shorten the word to ロック (rokku, meaning "lock" or "rock music") when speaking quickly — ロッカー must retain both syllables of the ending.

## Quiz

**Fill in the blank: ___**

**Question:** A small secure storage compartment is called ____ in Japanese.

**Answer:** ロッカー

**Explanation:** ロッカー (rokkaa) means locker — a secure compartment for storing personal belongings, operated with a key, code, or coin.
