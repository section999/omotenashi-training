title: 一括払い (ikkatsu barai)
---
一括払い means paying the full amount in a single transaction, as opposed to spreading it across installments. In Japanese hospitality, this is the standard assumed payment mode for most hotel stays, restaurant bills, and spa services. Staff confirm it as the default before processing. The term matters most in card transactions, where the terminal prompts a choice between 一括 (lump sum) and 分割 (installments). Selecting the wrong option requires voiding and reprocessing a payment, which delays service and can unsettle a guest.

For shorter stays or charges, 一括払い is usually appropriate. When a bill is particularly large, such as a multi-night banquet package, staff may introduce 分割払い as an option. The confirmation phrase ご一括でよろしいでしょうか is a standard check before running any card, and staff should use it consistently regardless of the bill amount.

## Related Expressions

| Expression | Description |
|---|---|
| 分割払い<br>bunkatsu barai | the opposite of 一括払い; payment split into multiple installments, which may incur additional fees depending on the card issuer. |
| 全額<br>zengaku | "full amount"; used in related contexts such as 全額ご負担いただきます (staff will be responsible for the full amount), but not a payment method term itself. |

## Common Mistakes

A common error is forgetting to confirm 一括払い before processing a card, so the terminal defaults to installments if the guest selects the wrong option. Some staff also confuse 一括払い with 前払い (prepayment): 一括払い refers to paying everything at once, not necessarily paying in advance. In guest-facing speech, the casual shorthand 一括で without a polite ending should be avoided; the full phrase ご一括でよろしいでしょうか is appropriate for formal hospitality contexts.

## Quiz

**全額を一度に支払う方法は____です。
(The method of paying the full amount at once is lump-sum payment.)**

- A. 一括払い (lump sum payment)
- B. 分割払い (installment payment)
- C. 前払い (prepayment)
- D. 後払い (deferred payment / pay later)

**Correct Answer:** 一括払い (lump sum payment)

**Explanation:** 一括払い is the standard assumption for hotel transactions and means paying the entire amount in one transaction, which is why confirming ご一括でよろしいでしょうか before running the card saves everyone the trouble of a void and reprocess. 分割払い splits the total into multiple payments, 前払い refers to timing rather than structure, and 後払い defers payment entirely, all of which describe different arrangements than paying in full at once.
