title: 明細書 (meisaisho)
---

**Pronunciation:** mei-**sai**-sho
**Register:** Formal — used by front desk, accounting, and administrative staff when preparing and presenting detailed financial documentation to guests or corporate clients.

明細書 is a comprehensive itemized statement that lists each charge, its date, the amount, and a description of the service rendered. Unlike a 領収書 (receipt), which simply confirms that payment has been made, a 明細書 provides the evidence for why each charge was incurred. In Japanese hospitality, the 明細書 is an essential document for corporate clients who must submit expense reports, as their accounting departments require line-by-line verification of every charge. Proactively preparing and offering a 明細書 to business guests demonstrates an understanding of their professional needs and is a key expression of the omotenashi spirit.

In hotel settings, the 明細書 is typically generated by the property management system and can be provided in print or emailed upon request. Some guests only need the total confirmed by a 領収書, while others need the full 明細書 for compliance or auditing purposes — staff should ask rather than assume which document is needed. If a guest disputes a charge, the 明細書 is the reference document used to walk through the charges item by item, making accuracy at the point of entry critical.

## Example Sentences

**1.** ご滞在中のすべてのご利用明細をまとめた明細書でございます。
*Go-taizai chuu no subete no go-riyou meisai wo matometa meisaisho de gozaimasu.*
"This is the itemized statement covering all charges incurred during your stay."

**2.** 明細書はメールにてお送りすることも可能でございます。
*Meisaisho wa meeru nite o-okuri suru koto mo kanou de gozaimasu.*
"We can also send the itemized statement to you by email."

**3.** 経費精算のため、領収書と明細書の両方が必要でしょうか。
*Keihi seisan no tame, ryoushuusho to meisaisho no ryouhou ga hitsuyou deshou ka.*
"Do you need both the receipt and the itemized statement for your expense claim?"

## Related Expressions

- **領収書 (ryoushuusho)** — receipt; confirms payment has been received but does not detail individual charges; often issued together with the 明細書 for corporate guests.
- **利用明細 (riyou meisai)** — usage statement; similar in content but used more in the context of credit card statements or loyalty account histories rather than a formal hotel document.

## Common Mistakes

Learners often assume that a 領収書 and a 明細書 serve the same purpose and offer only one when the guest actually needs both. Another mistake is issuing a 明細書 with incorrect or missing service descriptions, rendering it useless for expense verification. Staff should also be aware that a 明細書 showing charges that differ from what the guest was quoted requires explanation and correction before the document is handed over.

## Quiz

**料金の詳細が書かれた書類は____です。
(The document with detailed charge information is an itemized statement.)**

- A. 明細書 (itemized statement)
- B. 請求書 (invoice / bill)
- C. 領収書 (receipt)
- D. 伝票 (bill slip)

**Correct Answer:** 明細書 (itemized statement)

**Explanation:** 明細書 differs from 領収書 — a 領収書 confirms payment was made, while a 明細書 shows what was charged and why; corporate guests often need both: the 明細書 to verify charges and the 領収書 to prove payment to their accounts department.
