title: 明細書 (meisaisho)
---
明細書 is a comprehensive itemized statement that lists each charge, its date, the amount, and a description of the service. Unlike a 領収書 (receipt), which confirms that payment was made, a 明細書 provides evidence for why each charge was incurred. In Japanese hospitality, this document is essential for corporate clients whose accounting departments require line-by-line verification of every charge before approving reimbursement. Proactively preparing and offering one to business guests demonstrates an understanding of their professional needs, which is a meaningful expression of omotenashi in a billing context.

In hotel settings, the 明細書 is generated by the property management system and can be printed or emailed. Some guests need only the total confirmed by a 領収書, while others require the full 明細書 for compliance or audit purposes. Staff should ask rather than assume. If a guest disputes a charge, the 明細書 is the document used to review line by line, which is why accuracy at the point of entry is important.\n## Example Sentences

**1.** ご滞在中のすべてのご利用明細をまとめた明細書でございます。
*Go-taizai chuu no subete no go-riyou meisai wo matometa meisaisho de gozaimasu.*
"This is the itemized statement covering all charges incurred during the stay."

**2.** 明細書はメールにてお送りすることも可能でございます。
*Meisaisho wa meeru nite o-okuri suru koto mo kanou de gozaimasu.*
"We can also send the itemized statement to you by email."

**3.** 経費精算のため、領収書と明細書の両方が必要でしょうか。
*Keihi seisan no tame, ryoushuusho to meisaisho no ryouhou ga hitsuyou deshou ka.*
"Do you need both the receipt and the itemized statement for one's expense claim?"


## Related Expressions

- **領収書 (ryoushuusho)**: receipt; confirms payment has been received but does not detail individual charges; often issued together with the 明細書 for corporate guests.
- **利用明細 (riyou meisai)**: usage statement; similar in content but used more in the context of credit card statements or loyalty account histories rather than a formal hotel document.


## Common Mistakes

A common error is assuming 領収書 and 明細書 serve the same purpose and offering only one when the guest needs both. Another mistake is issuing a 明細書 with missing or vague service descriptions, making it useless for expense verification. If the 明細書 shows charges that differ from what the guest was quoted, that needs to be explained and corrected before handing over the document, not after.

## Quiz

**料金の詳細が書かれた書類は____です。
(The document with detailed charge information is an itemized statement.)**

- A. 明細書 (itemized statement)
- B. 請求書 (invoice / bill)
- C. 領収書 (receipt)
- D. 伝票 (bill slip)

**Correct Answer:** 明細書 (itemized statement)

**Explanation:** 明細書 provides a line-by-line account of every charge incurred, making it indispensable for corporate guests who need to justify expenses to their accounting departments. 領収書 confirms that payment was made but does not itemize what was charged; 請求書 requests payment before it is received; and 伝票 is an internal transaction slip, not a formal issued document. A corporate guest who receives a 明細書 without being asked can feel their professional needs were understood, which is exactly the spirit behind offering it proactively.
