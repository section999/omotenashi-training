title: 宿泊 (shukuhaku)
---

**Pronunciation:** shu-**ku**-ha-ku
**Register:** Formal — used by hotel and ryokan staff in written communications, verbal check-in procedures, and billing documents; more formal than 泊まる, which is used in everyday casual speech.

宿泊 is the formal Japanese word for an overnight stay or lodging. Unlike the casual verb 泊まる (to stay overnight), 宿泊 carries a professional register that makes it the natural choice for any written or spoken communication in a hospitality environment. It appears on registration forms, billing sheets, welcome letters, and in verbal exchanges between staff and guests. The word combines 宿 (lodging, inn) and 泊 (overnight stay), reflecting its roots in traditional Japanese travel culture where inns and lodgings played a central social role.

In practical hotel operations, 宿泊 is used to discuss the number of nights (宿泊数), the period of stay (宿泊期間), and the guest's overall accommodation arrangement. The polite prefix ご (go-shukuhaku) is always added when referring to a guest's stay. Phrases like ご宿泊の期間はいつからいつまででしょうか are used to confirm check-in and check-out dates. Understanding the difference between 宿泊 and other related words — such as 滞在 (taizai, a broader term for one's time spent somewhere) — helps staff use precisely the right term in every context.

## Example Sentences

**1.** ご宿泊の期間はいつからいつまででしょうか。
*Go-shukuhaku no kikan wa itsu kara itsu made deshō ka.*
"From when to when will you be staying with us?"

**2.** 本日より2泊ご宿泊のご予定でよろしいでしょうか。
*Honjitsu yori futa-haku go-shukuhaku no go-yotei de yoroshii deshō ka.*
"Is a two-night stay beginning today correct?"

**3.** ご宿泊中、何かご不明な点がございましたら、いつでもフロントまでお申し付けください。
*Go-shukuhaku-chū, nanika go-fumei na ten ga gozaimashitara, itsudemo furonto made o-mōshitsuke kudasai.*
"During your stay, please do not hesitate to contact the front desk at any time with any questions."

## Related Expressions

- **滞在 (taizai)** — a broader term meaning "time spent" at a place; while 宿泊 specifically implies overnight accommodation, 滞在 can refer to any duration of presence, including day visits.
- **泊まる (tomaru)** — the casual verb meaning "to stay overnight"; appropriate in everyday conversation but not in professional hospitality speech, where 宿泊 or ご宿泊 is expected.

## Common Mistakes

A very common error among learners is using 泊まる or 泊まります in formal guest interactions — while these are natural in casual conversation, front desk staff should consistently use 宿泊 or ご宿泊 to maintain the appropriate professional register. Some learners also confuse 宿泊 with 宿舎 (shukusha, employee dormitory) — these are completely different words with distinct meanings. Additionally, always pair 宿泊 with the honorific ご when referring to the guest's stay; omitting it can feel dismissive in a formal service context.

## Quiz

**ご____の期間はいつからいつまででしょうか。
(From when to when will you be staying with us?)**

- A. 宿泊 (accommodation / stay)
- B. 到着 (arrival)
- C. 出発 (departure)
- D. 予約 (reservation)

**Correct Answer:** 宿泊 (accommodation / stay)

**Explanation:** ご宿泊の期間 means "your stay period." This is the standard phrasing used at the front desk to confirm a guest's check-in and check-out dates. 宿泊 specifically refers to the act of staying overnight, making it the precise and professional choice here.
