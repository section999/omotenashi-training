title: 注意 (chuui)
---
**Pronunciation:** [chuu-**i**]
**Register:** Neutral to Formal, safety communication

注意 (chuui) means caution or attention, and in the context of safety signage it sits at the lowest tier of hazard communication. It appears on wet floor signs after mopping, near steps or uneven surfaces, around pool decks, and beside hot surfaces in buffet setups. The word is built from 注 (to pour attention into) and 意 (mind, intention), meaning the literal sense is to direct full awareness toward a situation. 注意 belongs to a three-tier scale alongside 警告 (warning) and 危険 (danger), so each word carries a specific weight that should not be swapped casually.

Beyond signage, 注意 is part of everyday spoken service. The phrase 足元にご注意ください (please watch one's step) comes up constantly when guiding guests through construction zones, down staircases, or over uneven ground. Similarly, お荷物にご注意ください is a natural reminder in busy lobbies. Because 注意 signals a moderate and precautionary level of risk, staff should not use it when the situation is dangerous. Those cases call for 危険 or 警告, and choosing the wrong word could cause guests to underestimate a real hazard
## Example Sentences

**1.** 清掃後で床が濡れておりますので、足元に十分ご注意ください。
*Seisou go de yuka ga nurete orimasu node, ashimoto ni juubun gochuui kudasai.*
"The floor is wet after cleaning, so please take extra care with one's footing."

**2.** 温泉エリアは床が滑りやすくなっておりますので、十分ご注意ください。
*Onsen eria wa yuka ga suberi yasuku natte orimasu node, juubun gochuui kudasai.*
"The hot spring area has slippery floors, so please exercise great caution."

**3.** 回転扉をご利用の際は、お子様にご注意いただきますようお願いいたします。
*Kaiten tobira wo goriyou no sai wa, okosama ni gochuui itadakimasuyou onegai itashimasu.*
"When using the revolving door, we ask that you please watch young children carefully."


## Related Expressions

- **危険 (kiken)**: danger; a stronger hazard designation used when there is significant risk of serious injury. The two words represent different rungs on the safety warning scale, so choosing the right one matters.
- **警告 (keikoku)**: warning; sitting between 注意 and 危険, it signals that the risk is serious and immediate action is required, even if the situation isn't yet life-threatening.


## Common Mistakes

A common error is to mix up 注意 and 危険, treating them as rough equivalents, but they signal very different levels of risk. Using 注意 in a dangerous situation can cause guests to underestimate what they are dealing with. There is also a pronunciation difference between 注意 (chuui) and 注文 (chuumon, order), and since 注文 comes up constantly in restaurant settings, context and kanji recognition both matter. When giving verbal cautions to guests, 注意してください on its own can sound abrupt. Pairing it with softer phrasing like お足元にお気をつけくださいませ keeps the tone in hospitality register.

## Quiz

**Which word is used to alert someone to potential risk?**

- A. 注意 (caution / attention)
- B. 危険 (danger)
- C. 安全 (safety / safe)
- D. 応急処置 (first aid)

**Correct Answer:** 注意 (caution / attention)

**Explanation:** 注意 sits at the lowest tier of the Japanese safety warning scale, used for moderate, precautionary risks like wet floors or uneven steps where care is needed but the hazard is not severe. 危険 is the right word when there is genuine risk of serious harm, 安全 is the desired state you are protecting, and 応急処置 is the response after an injury has already occurred.
