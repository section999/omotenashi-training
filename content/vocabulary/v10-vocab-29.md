title: 税別 (zeibetsu)
---

税別 is formed from 税 (zei), "tax," and 別 (betsu), meaning "separate" or "excluding." When a price is labeled 税別, it means the listed amount does not include consumption tax (消費税), and the tax, typically 10% for most hotel and dining services in Japan, will be added to calculate the final total. Understanding and correctly communicating 税別 is critical for hospitality staff, as many rate cards, menu prices, and package offers are quoted this way. Failing to disclose that a price is 税別 can lead to guests feeling misled when they see a higher total at checkout, which damages trust and the overall guest experience.

In practice, whenever staff quote a 税別 price, they should immediately follow up with the 税込 equivalent so the guest always knows their final out-of-pocket cost. For example, saying 「税別〇〇円、税込ですと〇〇円となります」 prevents any confusion. This is especially important for international guests who may not be aware that Japan's consumption tax is not always included in displayed prices, unlike in some other countries.

## Example Sentences

**1.** こちらの料金は税別でございます。
*Kochira no ryōkin wa zeibetsu de gozaimasu.*
"This rate is exclusive of tax."

**2.** 税別一万円ですので、税込では一万千円となります。
*Zeibetsu ichiman-en desu node, zeikomi de wa ichiman issen-en to narimasu.*
"The price is 10,000 yen excluding tax, so the total including tax will be 11,000 yen."

**3.** メニューの価格はすべて税別となっておりますのでご了承ください。
*Menyū no kakaku wa subete zeibetsu to natte orimasu node goryōshō kudasai.*
"Please note that all prices on the menu are exclusive of tax."

## Related Expressions

- **税込 (zeikomi)**: tax-inclusive; the stated price already includes consumption tax, making it the final amount
- **消費税 (shōhizei)**: consumption tax; the 10% tax added to 税別 prices to arrive at the 税込 total

## Common Mistakes

The most common mistake is quoting a 税別 price without mentioning that tax will be added, leaving guests surprised at the final bill. Learners also sometimes confuse 税別 with 税込 under pressure, particularly when a guest asks for clarification on the spot. When in doubt, always state both the 税別 price and the 税込 equivalent in the same sentence to eliminate any ambiguity.

## Quiz

**税金は含まれていない場合は____です。
(When tax is not included, the price is tax-exclusive.)**

- A. 税別 (tax excluded)
- B. 税込 (tax included)
- C. 合計 (total)
- D. 精算 (settlement / final billing)

**Correct Answer:** 税別 (tax excluded)

**Explanation:** 税別 prices are common in hotel rate cards: when quoting 税別, immediately follow with the 税込 total (税別〇〇円、税込ですと〇〇円となります) so guests always leave the conversation knowing the final amount they will pay.
